the token journey - two scales

What actually travels, and what survives it.

The same question asked twice. At the scale of one call: what the rod produced and what was kept. At the scale of a rung: what was claimed, what settled it, and whether the bytes still exist.

Judged on content alone the rod looks silent for forty seconds and then speaks. It was never silent. Across 84 instrumented ticks it produced 585,513 characters of reasoning against 103,662 of answer, and until 2026-08-04 the first number was discarded at the socket.

One rung

R5 is the first rung whose output is a chain rather than an action, so it is the first that can be walked end to end: a claim written and fsynced before any bytes existed, a probe, bytes hashed at the socket, a verdict, a consequence. 67 such chains exist, and every citation on R5’s page resolves to a file on disk.

The ordering is the mechanism, not a convention. settle() refuses any citation whose artefact was read BEFORE the claim was proposed. It is the one property that cannot be satisfied by writing more carefully afterwards.
