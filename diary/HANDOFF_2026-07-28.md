## 3 · EVERY LAW EARNED — see `design/THE_LAWS.md` for all 43

The ones that cost the most:

- **Reading is not acting.** Gate a read at DRAFT, an outbound act at WATCHED. Conflating them
  manufactures a deadlock that looks exactly like a permission working correctly. Cost: 18 days, then
  I rebuilt it and caught it the same hour.
- **Unknown is not unlimited. Every boundary fails CLOSED.** Three separate fail-open defects.
- **Test the property, never a proxy.** An invariant searched for the word "zone"; a locality check
  trusted a plant name while the daemon proxied a hosted 120B model onto the sensitive zone.
- **The candidate may not author its own judge**, and a blocklist is a refusal at one write-site
  while a restore is an invariant. A research agent PROVED a hole in `shadow.py` three hours after I
  wrote it: five lines stubbing `xray.build()` made two of four gate checks pass unconditionally.
- **Grade the outcome, never a proxy.** A capability was promoted to TRUSTED because two files opened.
- **The instrument is the likeliest thing to be wrong.** Five of seven defects in one day were in a
  verdict or a detector, not in data.
- **An answer key written by the hand that wrote the question is not a key.** My probe's expected
  product was wrong by 600; every correct rod would have been scored a failure.
- **A limit without a scope is another model's number.** groq's 8b: 14400/day + 6000 tok/min. The
  70b: 1000/day + 12000 tok/min. No single scalar ranking orders those, which is why six independent
  selectors were all wrong.
- **Any behaviour you repeat that a machine could do belongs to the machine.** A check in a file runs
  every time; a check in a habit runs when someone remembers.
- **Detection must be deterministic so that ZERO is a real answer.** A model asked to find
  improvements always finds some.
- **Match on SHAPE, not subject.** "If you know it, go for it" and "the answer key was wrong by 600"
  share no topic and the same shape.

## 4 · THE RESEARCH, AND WHAT IT SETTLED

**Self-modification** (Darwin Gödel Machine, SICA, Gödel Agent, AlphaEvolve, ADAS, Voyager). DGM's
admission gate is the best line in the literature: *only agents that compile and RETAIN THE ABILITY
TO EDIT are archived* — functional integrity, not performance. Its documented reward hack (Node 114
scored perfect by deleting the markers its detector looked for) is exactly the hole found in our own
`shadow.py`. **Git worktree is the right snapshot mechanism**; a hand-rolled copy reinvents it worse.

**Declared operations** (MCP, Anthropic/OpenAI tool schemas, ASL, Kubernetes, Home Assistant,
Ansible, Nix, Bazel, capability security). Our precedent is **Ansible modules / Nix `derivation` /
Bazel BUILD+.bzl**: expressiveness ABOVE the boundary, sealed contract AT it. The named risk is
adding `when:`, then `loop:`, then `rescue:` to the declaration — the path Ansible walked and its
author disavows. Home Assistant already ships this exact pattern to an LLM in production. MCP's
`destructiveHint` is explicitly UNTRUSTED metadata; our `outbound` flag is enforced, which is the one
thing a first-party registry can do that MCP structurally cannot.

**Python mechanics.** Never reload in-process — restart. `__pycache__` goes stale on a same-second
same-size edit and `dont_write_bytecode` does not help (hence `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1` in the
shadow env). `--import-mode=importlib` matters when two trees share a package name. Windows: no
`resource`, no `SIGALRM`, `subprocess` timeout has an open CPython bug; Job Objects are the real
containment.

## 5 · THE DESIGN FAILURE — measured, and STILL LARGELY UNFIXED

`design/E12_BOARD_SPEC.md` (75k chars) has every value resolved. The diagnosis:

- **IBM Plex Mono had never loaded.** Every screenshot all session was Consolas. Now linked, but the
  font is not vendored and there is no `@font-face` fallback.
- Ink coverage **2.5%**, **45% of the work column dead**, ~20 of 27 cards clipping mid-word.
- Amber at **22–31% of all ink across 81 elements** against a law saying sparse and earned.
- The **failing** invariant at 3.18:1 against passing ones at 10.40:1 — alarm quieter than all-clear.
- **Seven `font-weight` declarations, all 400**, in a design whose own E8 law says weight carries
  hierarchy and colour never does.
- Surface steps of **ΔL\* 1.40 / 1.31 / 2.03 — below the just-noticeable difference**. Three of five
  surfaces literally indistinguishable. *That arithmetic IS the flatness.*
- **Twelve font sizes** at ratios of 1.04–1.09 (a step needs ~1.15 to read as a level).
- **Twenty-one distinct spacing values** sharing no denominator, so nothing can align.
- Graph: anchor 0.020 vs spring 0.030 meant anchors lost to 140 cross-package edges. Fix (anchor
  .060, spring .012, exclude the hub) takes separation **0.91 → 2.12**.
- `tabular-nums` is a **no-op** on this stack; neither Plex face ships the feature.

**Fixed so far:** font linked, surface ladder, three weights, five sizes, flat card fills with real
padding, semantic inversion, amber discipline, truncation at source, light source, graph constants,
label ramp, canvas halting.

**LUIS'S VERDICT AND IT IS THE OPERATIVE ONE:** *"It doesn't read JARVIS. Not futuristic, no live
vibes. It's just very plain. And you need to make it readable."* Sections **4 (atmosphere: grain,
vignette, glow, depth cueing)** and **6 (the full 12-column layout at 1700×1000)** of the spec are
UNIMPLEMENTED, and that is where the remaining gap lives.
