# THE PROBE · THE FIELD GUIDE

*The whole game, written as a strategy guide — as if it were built, because that is how you build the right
thing: backward from the felt experience. Panel-authored (wf wrzza8i7u), honesty-critic-vetted, 2026-07-24.
Kept deliberately tight: the critic warned that a 30k-word guide around an unbuilt center is the 110:1 failure
reproducing at the guide layer. This is the north-star, not the epic.*

## THE OATH (page one — the honesty applied to the guide itself)

**EVERY LIGHT IN THIS GUIDE IS SOMETHING THAT REALLY HAPPENED.** And the honest disclosure that makes the
rest trustworthy: *as this is written, the loop at the center of the game — compose a mind, fire it, watch it
run differently, earn its name — has fired ZERO times through a player surface (COMPOSED 0). This guide
describes the finished PROBE as if built. Every beat is marked SHIPPED, NEEDS-A-FORGE, or FOG. Do not mistake
the vividness for a claim.* (The substrate itself already fires: `r-01` is a real PASS on disk — THE DRAW hit
nvidia's mistral-small in 827.5ms, one clean hop, 6 rods seen. What is missing is a **player** firing it.)

## DOES THE PROBE STAY A PROBE? — yes, and it is the cleanest intuition in the game

**You never evolve. The MIND does.** The probe is an angular matte-black speck, ~3% of frame, one warm amber
aperture, no exhaust — from first boot to THE WHOLE ONE, it never grows, never gains weapons, never glows
brighter. It is your **eye, not your character** — the incorruptible reader of live truth. Three things exist,
and conflating them is the failure to avoid: the **PROBE is you** (the constant witness/composer); the **MIND**
is the world you fly through (it grows organs); the **CONSTRUCTS** are the beings you compose (some become the
enemies). A player who expected to level UP instead **levels the WORLD** — and the moment they feel that, the
game has taught them what it is about. The one lawful avatar growth is **REACH**, metroidvania-style, mapped
1:1 onto the real zone ladder (LOCAL > NO-TRAIN > TRAINS > KEYLESS): you earn passage *inward*, ring by ring,
toward the sensitive core, each crossing a real permission event. The only visible token is a **reach-mark** —
a hairline grey notch milled into the hull, never amber, never glowing. *The mind earns amber titles; the probe
earns quiet grey notches. The light is always the mind's.* You end as the same small speck, now carrying seven
grey notches, docking at a burning core. **The smallness is the payoff: a speck that comes to KNOW a giant, not
a hero that becomes one.**

## HOW THE MIND IS REPRESENTED — concentric, because radius is TRUST

The mind is a **concentric radial instrument**, and concentric is not a style choice — it is the literal
geometry of the real permission model, which is why it survives the false-connection test. **RADIUS ENCODES
TRUST:** outer dashed = PUBLIC/OPEN, middle solid = PRIVATE/NO-TRAINING, inner sealed-double = SENSITIVE/
LOCAL-ONLY. Because radius = strictness, the map **teaches the core viability law by itself** — a construct
touching one sensitive part is visibly pulled inward and cannot operate on the public rim (`zone(A∘B) =
STRICTER`). No other layout is honest: a skill-tree implies arbitrary adjacency; a node-graph reads as a
dashboard on sight; a city lies about structure. Concentric is the one layout where **every position carries
honest meaning** — radius = trust, angle = organ-domain, depth = DAG depth. At the center: the two-tori core
(THE MOUTH · THE HUB) and, beneath it, the honesty-loop and the autonomy battery — *honesty at the heart,
literally.* You read the whole mind in one glance through a tiny live mark-vocabulary: core brightness = alive
now; embers-lit vs hollow-fog-cages = how complete ("4 of 6 organs lit"); the ring a thing sits on = how
exposed; fog extent = how much of the mind exists yet. **Labels-on-approach** keep it an instrument, not a
dashboard: silhouette at distance, detail engraves only as you fly close.

### THE FOCUS RING — one object, no menus (the metaphor that keeps 8 menus coherent)
There are not eight screens. There is **one instrument racked to different focus depths:**
- **INFINITY** → THE LADDER (your whole life, 8 rungs).
- **MID** → THE WORLD (fly the concentric mind).
- **DOCK / push-in** → THE BENCH (compose).
- **MACRO on one object** → THE BEING SHEET.

The glass **margins never change** (codex index on the right rail, battery gauges in the four corners, aperture
tag top-left). You are never lost — you are always looking at the mind, just closer or farther. **The dive from
world into an organ IS THE MERGE expressed as geometry:** the ring-map and the bench are the same space at two
zooms, which is exactly why there was never meant to be a cut between flying and composing.

## THE SCENARIO (the guided spine, condensed)

- **Ch.0 · COLD OPEN** — no menu, no splash: black, then your bow aperture kindles; you are already inside.
  LEYBER (terminal-register, never "I think/alive"): *"power is live. 1998 tokens on the free hearth. you are
  inside something that is running."* The void resolves into the great dark instrument; four organs lit, two
  fog. One beacon pulses. *"fly there."* One verb, under a minute. (NEEDS-A-FORGE: requires THE MERGE.)
- **Ch.1 · THE GUIDED TOUR** — you learn the architecture by *moving through it*: cross the rings, their
  permission labels engrave (PUBLIC → PRIVATE → SENSITIVE); organs label on approach; SENSES and HANDS are the
  two hollow cages you will fill. *"that is the guided journey — two organs, lit by you."*
- **Ch.2 · THE FIRST FIRE** — DOCK; the world pushes into the bench (REF-10). You SEAT a chip, press RUN — a
  **real metered call** on the free hearth. The tried-list animates; the receipt lands as **drama, not a
  table**. First amber (aha #1). If a rod is throttled, the Ladder **falls through** to the hearth and still
  PASSES (aha #2 — survived failure, warm-amber `FELL THROUGH`, never red).
- **Ch.3 · THE HELD WORD** — you meet THE WALL (a real rate limit). A refusal is **warm amber, HELD** — the
  system keeping its word is a scoring event, not an alarm (aha #3). You learn the frightening thing here is
  *darkness and draining*, and the reassuring thing is the warm refusal.
- **Ch.4 · THE NAME** — RECALL forged, the construct runs *differently* (grounded across a reset), and earns a
  title in the one lawful amber text (REF-09, aha #4). A mind two organs more complete than you found it. Then
  BUILDER and ARCHITECT are shown as honest cold-blue **fog** on the way out. Graduation, not a stop.

## THE ANTAGONIST — enemies without a single monster (the no-red law)

THE PROBE has enemies but never draws one. There is **no bestiary, no red ink, no HP bar, no invented number.**
A threat is always a **real event inside the running mind**, rendered only in the three inks: **STARVATION**
(earned amber that starts to gutter and drop — threat is the *absence* of light, never a new color), **COLD
MATTER** (a dead node = a dark chamfered slat with a cold X; a collapsed construct = a hollow ember-cage), and
**THE CLIMB TO THE WALL** (the real budget bar filling toward its rpm/rpd ceiling). The five honest threats:

- **THE WALL** *(real now, no construct needed)* — every cloud rod sits behind a real rate limit
  (`Meter.can_spend → reason='rpm'`); the Ladder falls to the free hearth so the fire still lands.
- **MODEL ROT** *(real, intermediate)* — your rods measurably decay (`model_fitness` sweeps; a pool contracted
  44→29); reach quietly shrinks. The honest defense: need the rods less — **crystallize** a proven behavior to
  cheap deterministic code that cannot rot.
- **THE RUNAWAY** *(your named enemy; needs a construct first)* — a LOOP with no GOVERNOR draws again and again
  against the **shared** budget; it spikes a plant's rpm window and `can_spend` returns False for **all your
  other work** — it doesn't attack your creatures, it **starves them through the rate limit they share**
  (`COUNT × FREQUENCY × COST ≤ capacity`, every term real). *"It keeps running after you close the tab" is the
  wonder AND the horror — a runaway is the heartbeat you built badly.*
- **CORRUPTION** — a construct drifting off-goal or routing a private task to a training-zone rod is **REFUSED
  by HADES** and by zone arithmetic; the trust ledger makes it cost (a failed run demotes the capability;
  send/spend/keys sit FORBIDDEN at charter 0 over any construct — governance never unlocks).
- **YOUR-OWN-CREATIONS & FOUND RUINS** — non-viable beings left in the world become derelicts frozen in their
  real failure signature (STARVED / NEVER-INTEGRATED / COULDN'T-ACT); found ruins are collapsed constructs you
  didn't build, dangerous mainly if you re-ignite a no-governor ruin.

**The defense is not a new system** — it is the governance organs you already met as allies, seen from the
side of what they stop: **THE METER** (early warning + budget), **THE GOVERNOR** (containment — cap a runaway's
draw, or quarantine it to the unlimited hearth where it drains nothing shared), **HADES** (the watcher). Your
verbs are GOVERN, CONTAIN/QUARANTINE, WALL, REPAIR, KILL — **never *attack*, because the only honest answer to
a bad system state is better governance.** That is the whole AEA turned into stakes: *the mind that can stop
itself is the mind that survives.* **THE SEQUENCING GATE (hard rule):** no threat that needs a construct is
built before COMPOSE-ONE-CONSTRUCT fires — a threat with nothing to threaten is a scripted animation of a thing
that cannot happen, a worse honesty breach than any ugly render.

## THE ECONOMY — Cities-Skylines-class, one honest currency (R44)

There is no gold, no XP. The whole economy is **one currency — the right to make a call** — and **one
inequality**, the carrying-capacity law: **COUNT × FREQUENCY × MODEL-COST ≤ CONNECTED-SOURCE CAPACITY.** How
many living things you run, times how often each wakes, times how expensive each one's fuel is, can never
exceed the throughput of the sources you've wired in — pick any three, the fourth is decided. You don't grind
it up; you **raise the ceiling by connecting a source** (the local Ollama hearth is your free, unlimited,
never-dying floor; each hosted plant widens the inequality — a felt upgrade). This is the CS depth, honest:
every variable is a real endpoint reading (real rpm windows in `grid.py`, real fitness/rot, real budget). The
runaway is your exploding power plant; the GOVERNOR is the planner's dashboard with teeth. Guided teaches
**one variable** (free hearth vs paid reach); the full grid-sim is BUILDER+.

## THE THREE JOURNEYS — FIRE → WIRE → PROGRAM, one construct at three depths

- **GUIDED · the newcomer** ("I don't get AI") — Ch.0-4, on rails, SANDBOX-locked, menus reveal as rewards.
  Closes with a named being and a mind two organs more complete. **The only journey the game must ship first.**
- **BUILDER · the intermediate** ("I want to make one") — no rails, the full pool (5→17), the viability
  spectrum (most of what you compose won't run, and *that is the knowledge*). The antagonist enters as your own
  runaway. **FUTURE — there is no builder to graduate into yet; today it is only the fog shown at graduation.**
- **ARCHITECT · the custom** ("I want to change how it thinks") — crystallize a behavior, then EDIT its code;
  author parts/tools; the LIVE-reach gate (bytes may only meet THE MEMBRANE; the entity never presses send).
  Ends at THE WHOLE ONE via a real 100-tick endurance run. **FURTHEST future, built as nothing now.**

*The integrity of the three-modes design is entirely in refusing to BUILD builder and architect now — naming
them as fog at graduation is the whole near-term treatment.*
