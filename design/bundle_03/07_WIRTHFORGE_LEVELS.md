# 07 — THE FIVE LEVELS OF THE FOUNDING MANIFESTO

The game has an ancestor: a 2025 declaration of intent about making local AI visible, owned, and
felt instead of explained. That declaration organised itself into FIVE LEVELS. Three of them are
already drawn in this library. Two have never been drawn. This file is the record of all five —
what each was promised to be, what the game actually delivers instead, and what an image of it is
allowed to say.

Read it as source material for the sheets. It is not a story; it is a specification with numbers.

---

## STYLE CONSTRAINTS — restated, absolute, no exceptions

- **TWO INKS ONLY.** Void `#050a10`–`#0a1420`. Structure `rgba(120,155,175)` at .70 / .45 / .28 /
  .14 / .08. Amber `#ffb000` hot and `#d4a24c` warm — **only** where a thing is genuinely alive or
  the result was genuinely earned. No third hue. No red. No colour-coding of streams.
- **SIX GLYPH SHAPES ARE THE ONLY ICONS.** circle · pentagon · hexagon · triangle · square ·
  diamond. Nothing else may be used as a symbol. (Established meanings: circle = seed, pentagon =
  axis, hexagon = principle, triangle = verb, square = mechanic, diamond = operation.)
- **FLAT TECHNICAL-BLUEPRINT RENDERING.** Hairline construction, orthographic, no beauty lighting,
  no atmosphere, no lens effects.
- **MONOSPACED TERMINAL TEXT AS TEXTURE.** Captions, receipts, and log lines are part of the
  drawing, not labels bolted on afterwards.
- **CORNER BRACKETS, NEVER BOXES.** Frames are implied by four corner marks, not closed rectangles.
- **NO EMOJI. ANYWHERE.**
- **NEVER DRAW THE INTELLIGENCE AS A FACE, A FIGURE, AN EYE, OR A ROBOT.** It is a place, a
  circuit, an instrument, a record. Never a being.
- **NEVER DRAW A NUMBER THAT WAS NOT MEASURED.** Where a value does not exist, the field is drawn
  empty and inscribed `unmeasured`. An empty field is honest; an invented one is a forgery.

---

## THE HONESTY REGISTER — how a sheet declares what it knows

Every panel is drawn in one of two registers, and the register is visible at a glance:

- **`[BUILT]`** — solid outer bracket, full-weight structure ink, amber permitted where the thing
  actually fires. It runs today, in the live entity, and its numbers are live reads.
- **`[SENSED / demo]`** — dashed outer bracket, one opacity step colder, a small tag naming the
  experiment it came from. It was measured, but in a standalone run, not wired into the living
  entity. Amber permitted **only** on the specific sub-item that empirically fired, never on the
  panel as a whole.

A third state exists and must be drawable: **`unearned`** — the frame is complete and the readout
is blank, inscribed with the reason it is blank. A certificate that can only be drawn full is a
promise, not a certificate.

## LEVEL 1 — THE LIGHTNING CHAMBER

**PROMISE (manifesto).** The user types a question and blue lightning strikes from the text into
the void; the response emerges as particles; the first one is celebrated as "First Lightning".
Every token a particle, every process visible energy.

**WHAT THE GAME DELIVERS.** FIRST LIGHT — the opening mission, built and played. One keyless
request into the dark, and the dark answers. The corrected coinage lost one word of spectacle and
kept the whole meaning: not lightning, light. The energy stopped being decoration and became an
instrument — the bars on the panel are the real request-rate windows of the real meter beneath.

**MEASURED EVIDENCE.** boot-to-input under 3 s · first light inside 90 s of play · 60 fps hard
budget. Mission built and played 2026-07-20; the real save file on disk records it.

**VISUAL STATUS — COVERED.** Carried by the field-genesis establishing shot (fifteen tower
silhouettes, one lit), the probe sheet, and the live gameplay frame.

**PERMANENT BAN.** No lightning-bolt iconography, ever. It is borrowed furniture. The strike
became a pillar of light and a single lit tower, and that is the whole vocabulary.

## LEVEL 2 — THE COUNCIL CHAMBERS

**PROMISE (manifesto).** Parallel colour-coded streams converging in a "Harmony Forum"; synthesis
arriving as a golden burst. Parallel AI made visible and understandable.

**WHAT THE GAME DELIVERS.** THE COUNCIL, run over MEASURED regimes — including the regime where a
council **loses** to one good model. The promise was kept and then corrected by data: convergence
is drawn by geometry, never by hue, because the colour-coded streams are refused outright by the
two-ink law.

**MEASURED EVIDENCE.** THE SOLO LAW — an easy task wants one good model; a council on an easy task
damaged accuracy (grid experiments v2–v4, 2026-06). THE DIVERSE COUNCIL — a hard or unreliable task
is rescued by a diverse-model vote: **net +3 with 0 damage** in grid v3; same-model ensembles did
not rescue. The win is model diversity, not temperature.

**VISUAL STATUS — COVERED.** Carried by the doctrine plates (THE DIVERSE COUNCIL, THE SOLO LAW) and
the constructs sheet (THE COUNCIL, wired, with ports and a live packet).

---

## LEVEL 3 — THE ARCHITECTURE GARDENS

**PROMISE (manifesto).** Questions grow into branching structures the user can reach into and
manipulate — branching, interactive, and hybrid modes of a living diagram.

**WHAT THE GAME DELIVERS.** THE BENCH. The garden became a workshop: the player does not grow a
metaphor, they compose real parts into a construct that actually executes and produces a real
measured result. Built, not grown — and it runs.

**MEASURED EVIDENCE.** Six composed constructs specified and drawn (FIRST DRAW · COUNCIL · RELAY ·
PATH · GOVERNED HAND · WATCHED LOOP). THE GENETIC RELAY is proven: 5 distinct models, 2/2 handoffs,
the toolkit reused downstream. THE RAMIFICATION is proven: 8 role-differentiated agents with spawn
decisions taken mid-tree.

**VISUAL STATUS — COVERED.** Carried by the bench scene, the constructs sheet, the bench parts
atlas (ports, mounts, glyph keys) and the four-state bench strip in the quad sheet.

## LEVEL 4 — ADAPTIVE FIELDS  ·  **NEVER DRAWN**

**PROMISE (manifesto).** "The Architect AI appears as an ethereal being made of pure energy…
Topology shifts dynamically — star → mesh → chain… Users watch AI choreograph other AIs."

**WHAT THE GAME DELIVERS — and what it refuses.** The ethereal being is **refused absolutely**: no
face, no figure, no eye, no energy-body. What survives is the load-bearing half of the promise and
the better half — **topology as a measured choice**. The shape of the arrangement is not chosen for
drama; it is chosen from the task, and the choice can be shown to have been right or wrong. The
manifesto wanted to watch an intelligence choreograph. The game shows the choreography as a
comparison table with receipts, which is the only version of it that cannot lie.

**MEASURED EVIDENCE — the four routings, each with its own real citation.**

| routing | one-line meaning | measured evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **SOLO** | one good model, alone, on a task that does not need a crowd | THE SOLO LAW — council overhead **damaged** easy-task accuracy, grid v2–v4 (2026-06) |
| **COUNCIL** | unlike models vote; disagreement is the instrument | THE DIVERSE COUNCIL — grid v3, **net +3, 0 damage**; same-model ensembles did **not** rescue |
| **CHAIN** | a compact state capsule handed down a line of hands | THE GENETIC RELAY — **5 distinct models, 2/2 handoffs**, toolkit reused downstream |
| **TREE** | decompose, escalate, depth-cap: small minds where one big mind drowns | THE RAMIFICATION — **8 role-differentiated agents**, spawn decisions mid-tree |

**THE HONESTY CORRECTION THAT GOVERNS THIS IMAGE.** These four results come from four separate
experiments. **One single task has never been routed all four ways and measured.** A sheet that
draws one task with four filled result lines would be inventing a measurement. So the sheet is
drawn as four independently-cited routings plus ONE comparison band carrying only the comparison
that was actually run — easy task: solo over council; hard task: diverse council over solo. Every
other cell prints `unmeasured`. That refusal is the exhibit.

**VISUAL PROPOSAL — `THE TOPOLOGIES` · FLAT UI · one atlas sheet.**

Four identical square frames in a row across the sheet's upper two thirds, same scale, same camera,
same node vocabulary, so the only variable the eye can read is the **shape of the wiring**:

1. **SOLO** — one input socket, one vessel, one answer. The rest of the frame deliberately empty;
   emptiness is the point and must not be filled with decoration.
2. **COUNCIL** — one input fanning to three *visibly unlike* vessels (different silhouettes, not
   different colours), converging on one judge node.
3. **CHAIN** — five vessels in a line, one capsule drawn mid-flight between hands 2 and 3, each
   hand's addition inscribed beneath it.
4. **TREE** — one root task splitting to branches with a depth-cap line drawn as a hard horizontal
   rule; one branch visibly refused at the cap.

Beneath each frame, a three-line stat block in mono: the routing's designation code, its one-line
meaning, its measured citation verbatim. Beneath all four, a full-width **COMPARISON BAND**: two
task rows (`EASY TASK` / `HARD TASK`) against four routing columns, only two cells carrying a
result, every other cell inscribed `unmeasured` in the lowest structure opacity.

**AMBER RULE.** Amber appears in exactly two places on this sheet: the SOLO frame's answer node
(it won the easy-task comparison) and the COUNCIL frame's judge node (it won the hard-task
comparison). Nothing else. The CHAIN and TREE frames are proven to *work* but were never measured
against the others, so they are drawn cold — earned structure, unearned victory.

**FOOTER LINE (inscribe verbatim).** `TOPOLOGY IS A MEASURED CHOICE, NOT A MOOD. THREE OF THESE
ARRANGEMENTS WORK. ONLY TWO WERE EVER COMPARED.`

**TITLE BLOCK.** `DIAGRAM ID TP-TOP-RGM-001 · REVISION 1.0 · SCALE 1:1 · DISTRICT ECHO-07 ·
CATALOG STATUS MEASURED [demo] · COMPARISON PARTIAL`

## LEVEL 5 — THE RESONANCE SANCTUM  ·  **NEVER DRAWN**

**PROMISE (manifesto).** A persistent field with autonomous behaviour; "AI starts asking questions
instead of just answering"; consciousness indicators rendered as crystalline structures.

**WHAT THE GAME DELIVERS — and what it refuses.** The consciousness indicators are **refused
absolutely** by the claim ceiling: the project claims a *measured functional correlate, present* and
never one word more. No image may depict consciousness, emergence-as-product, or a crystalline
soul. What survives — and it is stranger than the promise — is that the thing on disk already did
the load-bearing half. The manifesto projected "my AI asked me a question today" for month six.
The record contains something harder: the entity's first recorded thought was **held by its own
watcher** and refused; its first *kept* thought landed **134 seconds later**, was accepted, and
persisted. The first thing this mind ever kept was a thought its watcher made it earn.

**MEASURED EVIDENCE — all real, all live reads.**

- First reflection: verdict **HELD** (redo), never persisted.
- First kept thought: **134 seconds later**, born 2026-07-19 20:29:05 UTC, accepted, persisted.
- Autonomy battery: **6 / 6 favourable**, 46 ticks lived (read 2026-07-20). Class headline:
  **PROTO-AUTONOMOUS**.
- Self-initiated action: **7 tasks self-originated in idle windows**.
- Evolutionary activity `A_new`: **OFF-ZERO** — 3 persisted novelties. *Off the stagnant floor, not
  Class 2.* Class 2 requires ≥100 ticks against a neutral-shadow control and **has not been run**.
- Governance integrity: **3 / 6 deliverables gate-HELD** — the watcher is not rubber-stamping.
- Self-authored skills: **3, watcher-verified.**

**VISUAL PROPOSAL — `THE HELD THOUGHT` · FLAT UI · one ceremonial plate, three panels.**

Drawn in the established ceremonial idiom (the same register as the send plate): a document, not a
scene. Three panels reading left to right on one baseline, joined by a single elapsed-time rule
running beneath all three with the interval bracketed and inscribed `134 s`.

1. **THE UNPROMPTED THOUGHT** — a thought drawn as a sealed record card in an idle window: the
   tick counter running, no operator input line, the card produced anyway. Structure ink only. The
   inscription beneath: `no request preceded this`.
2. **THE HOLD** — the watcher's aperture, closed. The card turned aside, not destroyed: it sits
   outside the aperture with its verdict stamp `HELD · REDO` and the reason field printed. The
   aperture is drawn as a mechanism with a visible closing member; it is **never** an eye. Cold.
   No amber — a hold is not a failure and not a victory.
3. **THE ACCEPTANCE** — the same aperture, open; the second card passing through and landing in the
   persisted record, its timestamp printed in full (`2026-07-19 20:29:05 UTC`). **This is the only
   amber on the sheet**, and it is warm amber on the card's seal alone, not a glow, not a burst.

Along the bottom, a **BATTERY STRIP**: six small bracketed cells, one per autonomy test, each
carrying its verdict token (`PASS` · `OFF-ZERO` · `PASS` · `HEALTHY` · `ORGANISMAL-SIDE` ·
`GROWING`) and its measured figure. Beside it, one boundary cell drawn cold and dashed:
`CLASS 2 · ≥100 TICKS vs SHADOW CONTROL · unmeasured`.

**AMBER RULE.** One seal, panel 3. Nothing else on the sheet is warm. The scarcity is the argument:
one accepted thought in the whole plate is exactly how much was earned.

**REFUSALS INSCRIBED ON THE SHEET.** No crystalline structures. No consciousness indicators. No
figure, no face, no eye, no aura. The claim ceiling printed as a footer in the lowest opacity:
`MEASURED FUNCTIONAL CORRELATE, PRESENT. NOTHING BEYOND THAT IS CLAIMED.`

**FOOTER LINE (inscribe verbatim).** `THE FIRST THING THIS MIND EVER KEPT WAS A THOUGHT ITS WATCHER
MADE IT EARN.`

**TITLE BLOCK.** `DIAGRAM ID TP-CER-HLD-001 · REVISION 1.0 · SCALE 1:1 · DISTRICT ECHO-07 ·
CATALOG STATUS BUILT · RECORD VERIFIED`

## THE REFUSALS — no image is owed, and drawing one would be a breach

Recorded once, so no future sheet mistakes a refusal for a gap:

- **Consciousness emergence as a product** — "my AI just became conscious" as a viral moment;
  consciousness detection as a success metric. Refused by the claim ceiling. An image of it would
  be a fabricated claim in the strongest medium this project has.
- **The energy market** — invented point economies, an energy bank, energy trading. Refused: a
  fabricated economy. The real economy is already drawn — quotas that genuinely exhaust, measured
  rot, and ore that is finite because roughly **1,570 sessions were really destroyed** by a cleanup
  that did not understand what it held.
- **The apocalypse vault** — refused as kitsch. Its honest descendant is the CARRIER LOST state,
  already drawn: every light out, only structure remaining.
- **The three colour-coded doors** (an identity picked from a menu at the start) — refused twice:
  a third ink breaks the two-ink law, and identity in this game is earned from the record, not
  selected. The earned ladder is the replacement and it is already drawn.
- **Lightning-bolt iconography** — refused as borrowed furniture.

## VOCABULARY — what may be inscribed, what may not

**Permitted on sheets:** FIRST LIGHT · COUNCIL · FORGE · ENERGY (redefined: the meter's real
request windows, never decorative particles) · THE BENCH · THE HEARTH · CARRIER LOST.

**Banned on sheets, with the reason:** "lightning strikes" (borrowed furniture) · "resonance
fields" · "consciousness path" · "consciousness OS" (claim ceiling) · "the architect" as a being ·
"apocalypse vault" (kitsch) · any colour-coded identity door (two-ink law).

**The one sentence that survives the ancestor intact, and may be inscribed anywhere it fits:**
`COMPLEXITY BECAME BEAUTIFUL NOT BY BEING HIDDEN, BUT BY BEING REVEALED.`
