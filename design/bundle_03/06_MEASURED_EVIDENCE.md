# 06 — THE MEASURED EVIDENCE

**THESE ARE THE ONLY NUMBERS THAT MAY APPEAR ON A SHEET.** Every figure below was produced
by a real run, a real sweep, a real log, or a published paper. Any other figure — any
percentage, count, rate, score, duration, or dimension not on this page — must be drawn as a
BLANK FIELD or a DASH. A plausible-looking invented number destroys the one thing this world
sells: that the readouts are true. When a value is unknown, write `unmeasured` and draw the
field empty. An empty field is correct; a filled one that nobody measured is a forgery.

Marks: `[LIVE]` read from the running system today · `[SWEPT]` produced by a dated
measurement pass · `[RUN]` produced by one standalone run, demonstrated but not wired into
the live mind · `[CITE]` a published external threshold · `[SPEC]` an authored dimension,
not a measurement.

## VISUAL LAW — restated, absolute, no exceptions

- **TWO INKS ONLY.** VOID ground `#050a10` deepening to `#0a1420`. STRUCTURE cold blue-gray
  `rgba(120,155,175, x)` at exactly five opacities: `.70` values and important lines · `.45`
  labels · `.28` secondary detail · `.14` hairlines and frames · `.08` faint rules and grids.
  LIFE amber at exactly two tiers: `#ffb000` HOT (working right now) · `#d4a24c` WARM
  (exists, earned, idle). Amber ONLY where something is alive, running, or earned — never as
  highlight, never as decoration. One or two amber accents per sheet. A cold sheet is correct.
  Forbidden absolutely: red, green, white, teal, purple, any third hue, any non-amber glow.
- **SIX GLYPH SHAPES ARE THE ONLY ICONS**: circle, pentagon, hexagon, triangle, square,
  diamond. No representational pictograms of any kind.
- **FLAT TECHNICAL-BLUEPRINT RENDERING.** Matte, screen-printed, orthographic where objects
  are shown. No photoreal, gloss, chrome, bloom, flare, god-rays, drop shadows, or bevels.
  ~0.10 mm detail lines, ~0.18 mm structure lines.
- **MONOSPACED TERMINAL TEXT AS TEXTURE.** Codes and readouts as surface material. Denser
  text means more evidence, never more ornament.
- **CORNER BRACKETS, NEVER BOXES.** Edge ruler scales. Title block in one corner with ID,
  revision, scale, status.
- **NO EMOJI.**
- **NEVER draw the intelligence as a face, a figure, an eye, or a robot.**

**Number typography on any sheet.** Figures set in mono, structure `.70`. A figure goes amber
ONLY if it is a live reading of something running at that moment. Cited thresholds are always
cold. Units always present. A ratio is written `a / b`, never as a bare percentage unless the
percentage itself is the measurement.
