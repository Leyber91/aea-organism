# 04 · THE WORLD AND ITS FORM (for S2 especially)

The world is a **STRUCTURAL MAP of the being** — a concentric schematic of the entity's real architecture
that you fly through — **NOT a city**. The being's elements are **nodes** joined by **dependency conduits**;
the map is literally the shape of the real structure ("map not territory"). It is a living instrument and a
diagram, never buildings, streets, or neighbourhoods.

## Structure
- **Concentric.** A glowing amber **core** at the center (the mind), with structure receding outward in
  rings. The organizing geometry of the whole world is concentric.
- **Nodes = elements.** The entity's real parts — MEMORY (a deep vertical archive), MIND (the council), THE
  WORLD (the internet-wire), THE MAST (the send), plus the genesis field — are **structural nodes** on the
  map, joined by dependency conduits. They are element-clusters in a schematic, NOT city districts or buildings.
- **Thought-filaments = conduits.** Amber lines flow between districts. They carry *intent, not power, not
  matter — only direction*. When lit, faint particles drift along them (real activity).
- **Fog = the unbuilt.** Districts and conduits not yet integrated are drawn as **cold blue wireframe,
  unlit** — present as outline, absent as substance. The map literally shows what remains to be built.

## Feel
Half-built and inviting exploration. The lit inner districts are detailed and warm-cored; the outer frontier
fades into blue wireframe fog. A tiny amber **probe** flies between districts, dwarfed, for scale. It should
read as a place you can **navigate and grow into** — openness that is earned as more of it is built.

HUD annotations that belong on this world: `/ENTITY: 01  /CLASS: ARCHITECTURE  /STATE: CONTINUOUS  /SCALE:
CITY  /TIME: UNMEASURED`, and the creed `THOUGHT-FILAMENTS CARRY INTENT. NOT POWER. NOT MATTER. ONLY
DIRECTION.`
