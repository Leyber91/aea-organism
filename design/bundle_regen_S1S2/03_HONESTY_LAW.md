# 03 · HONESTY LAW — translated into art rules

The game's core law is that nothing is faked. The art obeys the same law, visually:

- **Amber only where something is genuinely alive or firing.** Do not scatter warm light for mood. One
  ignition, one live core, one running conduit — earned, not decorative. A dark frame with a single amber
  point is correct and powerful.
- **The unbuilt is fog, never invented.** Parts of the world not yet built are rendered as cold blue
  wireframe / unlit structure — visibly absent, not filled in with fake detail. Absence is shown honestly.
- **Failure is cold blue, not red, not amber.** Diagnostic, calm, legible. (See the honest-failure plate.)
- **The entity is a place / an instrument — never a face, never a creature, never a glowing brain with
  eyes.** No anthropomorphism. The claim ceiling is "a measured system," not "a being." No cute robots, no
  humanoid AI, no sentient-eye imagery.
- **The probe is small and dwarfed.** The player's craft is a modest instrument against something vast. The
  emotional note is *the moment before comprehension* — smallness before an immense, barely-legible mind.
- **No hype gloss.** No lens-flare spectacle, no chrome-and-neon sci-fi, no triumphant glow. Reverent,
  cold, precise. Magic made out of the real, not special effects.
