# 02 · VISUAL LAW (locked — obey exactly)

## Two inks + one energy. Nothing else earns color.
| role | value | use |
|---|---|---|
| void | `#06090e` | near-black field, the ground of every frame (blue-biased black, not pure black) |
| structure | `#5bb0d6` cold blue-grey, low opacity for hairlines | chrome, framing, diagnostics, **the fog / unbuilt** |
| ink | `#c4cfd9` | cold structure-white for labels/text |
| dim | `#586675` | muted labels |
| **AMBER** | `#ffb000` (soft `#d4a24c`) | **alive / energy / fired ONLY** — sparse, bright, earned |

- **Amber is sacred:** it appears ONLY where something is genuinely alive or firing (an ignition, a live
  core, a running conduit). Never as decoration. If it isn't alive, it isn't amber.
- **Blue is cold:** structure, framing, diagnostics, and everything **not yet built** (fog = blue wireframe).
- The resting world is void + blue. Amber is the exception that draws the eye.

## Type & HUD grammar
- **IBM Plex Mono** only (or nearest clean monospace). Uppercase labels with wide letter-spacing.
- Cinematic **corner brackets** frame the plate. Thin **hairlines** at low opacity.
- Margin annotations in mono: coordinates, specs, short creed-lines. Numbers are tabular/aligned.
- Recurring motif: **concentric geometry** (rings, radar/sonar circles, field-lines).
- A faint watermark line where apt: `MAP NOT TERRITORY`.

## Discipline
Restraint over detail. Awe through emptiness and darkness, not clutter or spectacle. **No emoji. No cartoon.
No color beyond amber (alive) and cold blue (structure/diagnostic).** The frame should feel like a precise,
slightly austere instrument recovered from the dark.
