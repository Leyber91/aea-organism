# 05 · REFERENCE VOCABULARY — the established plates to continue

These plates already exist and define the locked visual language. **Continue this vocabulary exactly** —
evolve, do not reinvent. (Attach the matching original PNG alongside this bundle as the visual anchor.)

- **S1 · THE FIELD AT GENESIS** — a vast dark plain etched with faint concentric field-lines; a low horizon
  of silent black monolith-towers; a single thin **amber ignition beam** rising in the distance. Cold-blue
  corner HUD, mono labels, coordinates. Creed: *"FIFTEEN SILENT WITNESSES / ONE STILL SPEAKS."* Mood: cold,
  still, one light in the dark.
- **S2 · THE ENTITY AS PLACE** — three-quarter isometric of a dark machine-city with a glowing amber
  **concentric core** and radiating amber **thought-filament** conduits. Creed: *"OBSERVE WITHOUT INTRUSION
  / INTERFERE WITHOUT PURPOSE / UNDERSTAND WITHOUT CLAIM."* The entity rendered as architecture.
- **S6 · THE ARCHIVE** — a vertical descent shaft into memory strata, concentric layered caverns, amber
  veins in dark rock. *"Memory is weight. We descend to understand."*
- **S7 · THE HONEST FAILURE** — a technical blueprint of a chain with one **severed link**, drawn in **cold
  blue** (not amber). *"Failure is information, not an exception."* This is how failure always looks: blue,
  diagnostic, calm.
- **S8 · KEYART / A11** — a tiny amber probe dwarfed by an immense non-Euclidean concentric structure.
  *"THE MOMENT BEFORE COMPREHENSION."* The emotional thesis: smallness before a vast, barely-legible mind.

Shared DNA across all of them: near-black void, cold blue-grey hairline structure, amber-for-alive-only,
mono HUD in corner brackets, concentric geometry, extreme restraint.
