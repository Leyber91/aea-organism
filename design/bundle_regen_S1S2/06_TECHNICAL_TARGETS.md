# 06 · TECHNICAL TARGETS

## Format
- **S1 (Genesis):** 16:9 landscape, wide cinematic. High resolution.
- **S2 (Entity as place):** 4:3 or 3:2, three-quarter isometric plate. High resolution.
- Flat, matte, no heavy post-glow. Fine film grain acceptable. Deep blacks preserved (not lifted to grey).

## Composition
- Dominant negative space / darkness. The subject is small in the frame; the void does most of the work.
- A single clear focal point of amber. Everything else is void + cold blue structure.
- Cinematic corner brackets and mono HUD annotations framing the plate (thin, low-contrast, real labels).

## Negative list (do NOT include)
- No people, no faces, no hands, no humanoid or creature forms, no eyes.
- No cute robots, no anthropomorphic AI, no glowing brain-with-a-face.
- No color other than amber (alive) and cold blue-grey (structure/diagnostic). No red, green, purple, teal
  neon, no rainbow data-viz.
- No cartoon / anime / stylized-mascot look. No glossy chrome, no lens-flare spectacle, no god-rays overkill.
- No emoji anywhere. No gibberish or lorem HUD text — if HUD text is visible, use only the real labels given
  in the sheet brief.
- No triumphant / heroic mood. The register is cold, reverent, precise.

## Reference
Match the quality and restraint of the attached original plate (S1 or S2). Evolve it per the sheet brief;
keep the world identical.
