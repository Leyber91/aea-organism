# 07 · SHEET S1 — THE FIELD AT GENESIS (v2)

## Intent
The opening. The dark room before the game begins. A vast dark field, one distant amber ignition, and the
player's probe — tiny, alone, *seeking* the light. This is the first frame the player ever sees. It must
say, without words: you are small, the dark is vast, and one thing is alive out there.

## What changed from v1
v1 had the field, the towers, and the beam but no protagonist. v2 adds the **probe** in the foreground —
small, four-legged, amber running-lights, oriented toward the beam (STATUS: SEEKING). Keep everything else
as restrained as v1; do not add clutter. The probe makes it a *player's* opening, not just a landscape.
