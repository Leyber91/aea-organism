# 01 · WHAT THIS IS — the game the art belongs to

**THE PROBE** is a browser game where the player flies a small probe **inside a real, running autonomous AI
entity** on their own machine. The player composes real AI parts — a BRAIN (a real model), SENSES, HANDS
(real tool-calling), MEMORY, a LOOP, a GOVERNOR — into small **creatures** that actually run on real tokens.
A viable creature keeps looping on its own; a non-viable one visibly fails, and that failure is the lesson.

**The founding law is honesty:** every number, bar, and event is live system truth. Nothing is simulated;
an absent value is a dash. The game never claims the entity is conscious — a proof is a *receipt* (it ran),
never a claim. The whole appeal is **making magic out of the real**, not mystifying it.

**Why this matters for the art:** the image must read as a genuine instrument or place from *this* game —
grounded, cold, reverent — not generic sci-fi spectacle. It is a recovered instrument, not a product shot.
The world is the entity, seen as a place: concentric, dark, with one warm energy moving through it.

Key elements the art may reference: the **probe** (a small four-legged craft, the player's vehicle, always
dwarfed); the **entity-as-city** (a dark concentric machine-mind); **districts = organs** (memory, mind,
world); **thought-filaments** (amber conduits carrying intent, not power); **fog** (cold blue wireframe =
the parts not yet built); the **honest failure** (a severed link, in cold blue).
