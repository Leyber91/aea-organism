# 08 · SHEET S2 — THE ENTITY AS A NAVIGABLE WORLD (v2)

## Intent
The world the player navigates. The entity rendered as a concentric **structural map / schematic** you fly
through — **NOT a city, no buildings** — a diagram of the being's architecture, and, crucially in v2, a
world that is **half-built and explorable**: lit inner districts, fogged outer frontier.
It must read as a *place you can move through and grow into*, not a static diagram.

## What changed from v1
v1 showed the entity-as-place beautifully but as a finished, uniform structure. v2 must show the **game
state**: (1) inner districts lit and detailed with the amber core; (2) **outer districts as cold blue
wireframe — fogged, unbuilt**; (3) the **probe in flight** between districts, small, for scale; (4) faint
particles drifting along the amber thought-filament conduits (real event traffic). The point is *earned
openness*: the map visibly shows what is built vs what remains.

## The prompt (paste this)
> Three-quarter isometric concept plate of a vast dark **structural map / schematic of a mind** — NOT a
> city, no buildings — a navigable diagram of the entity's architecture you fly through. A glowing **amber**
> concentric core at its heart, with amber "thought-filament" **dependency conduits** radiating outward to
> labeled **element-nodes** (MEMORY, MIND, THE WORLD, THE MAST). Inner nodes near the core are lit and
> detailed; **outer nodes are drawn as cold blue-grey wireframe — fogged, unbuilt, unlit**. A tiny amber
> probe in flight between nodes for scale. Faint light-particles drift along the
> amber conduits, suggesting live traffic. Cold blue-grey corner HUD in IBM Plex Mono: "/ENTITY: 01",
> "/STATE: CONTINUOUS", "/SCALE: CITY", "THOUGHT-FILAMENTS CARRY INTENT. NOT POWER. ONLY DIRECTION", and a
> faint "MAP NOT TERRITORY" watermark. Two inks + amber-alive-only: near-black void, cold blue structure,
> amber only for the live core and lit conduits. Concentric geometry throughout, restraint over detail. No
> people, no faces, no creature, no color beyond amber and cold blue, no cartoon, no emoji. Mood: a living
> instrument, half-built, inviting exploration. 4:3, high resolution, matte, deep blacks.
