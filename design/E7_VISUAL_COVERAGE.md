## 1. METHOD — what the three statuses mean

- **COVERED** — a sheet on disk depicts this concept as an identifiable item, either in its own
  frame or as a named carrier whose drawing actually shows the behaviour. Cited by sheet id.
- **PARTIAL** — a sheet depicts the family, the carrier, or the mood, but the concept's
  *distinguishing feature* is invisible. The missing feature is named in the row. A partial is
  not a failure; it is a one-panel addition to a sheet that already exists.
- **NONE** — no sheet contains it in any form.

Compression rule (inherited from A15 §1.2.3): where A15 deliberately compressed a canonical
item into a carrier, this chapter asks a harder question than A15 did — *is the compression
VISIBLE?* A compression whose carrier is drawn but whose canonical distinction cannot be seen
scores PARTIAL, not COVERED. Silent many-to-one is what both censuses exist to prevent.

Proposal rule: every row that is not COVERED carries a one-sentence concrete visual proposal,
classed SOLID / HOLOGRAM / FLAT UI. A proposal may not depict a capability that does not run;
where the proof is [demo], the image carries the fog and the citation.

## 8. WHAT THIS CENSUS PROVES

Luis's claim was: "im sure we didnt represent all the concepts of the AEA, WirthForge and the 5
levels are examples of it, and the combinations and the sublevels."

**Proven, on all four counts:**

1. **The AEA** — 24 of 86 canonical items have an image. 39 have none.
2. **WirthForge's five levels** — three carried, two partial; neither of the two missing images
   is hard, and both are already true on disk.
3. **The combinations** — the six doctrines are drawn and everything else that combines is not:
   ten taught links, four mechanic-seed pairs, three composition rules, two measured null
   results, all uncovered or partial.
4. **The sublevels** — 0 of 30 axis rungs drawn, and half of them cannot be drawn yet because
   canon has not said what they are.

The failure was structural, not lazy: the sheets were commissioned from the game's 29-element
proof taxonomy, and the proof taxonomy is a projection of the canon. The library is a faithful
image of the wrong list. Correcting it costs roughly **nine generation runs and three build
sessions** — and one authorship session from Luis, which blocks the largest single batch.

Nothing in this chapter proposes a new visual language. Every proposal is an existing sheet
grammar (the B-series seal, the A6 panel, the D-series state strip, the B9 ceremonial plate)
pointed at a concept that has never been pointed at.
