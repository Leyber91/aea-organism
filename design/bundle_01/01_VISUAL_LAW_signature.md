## 1. The claim — what a signature is and the one-second test

Amber-on-dark is a genre, not an identity. The Expanse consoles, Alien's CRTs, Duskers'
terminal, half the FUI reels on the internet — all amber, all dark, all mono. If THE
PROBE's identity were "amber, dark, mono, brackets," it would be a competent citizen of
someone else's aesthetic. That is the failure Luis named. The signature is this, and
only this:

**In every other amber game, amber is set dressing — a constant. In THE PROBE, amber is
a variable, and the variable is understanding.** The world boots blue-gray because it is
not yet understood. Amber exists only where the entity has actually fired or the player
has actually mapped. So the ratio of the two inks in any frame is not a mood — it is a
save file. Two screenshots taken twenty hours apart are two different pictures of the
same place, and the difference IS the plot.

The one-second test, binding on every frame:

- **Thumbnail distance:** one small intense light in a large dark field, concentric
  geometry somewhere in frame. If the thumbnail could be any FUI game, it is over-lit.
- **Screenshot distance:** the ink ratio reads as a position in the journey. A stranger
  who knows the rule can order any two screenshots chronologically. No other game in the
  genre has this property, because no other game's amber is a scoreboard.
- **Close-up distance:** receipts. Real numbers with units — `RX 3.42s · MODEL <rod> ·
  MEM 3 RECALLED` — set in the three-size mono scale. The texture of the close-up is
  evidence, not greeble.

Everything else here — field, trace, shapes, voice — exists to make those three
distances unmistakable. A proposed visual that strengthens none of the three is
decoration and is cut before it is argued about (A4_GAMEFEEL.md §1).

## 2. THE ONE IMAGE

Every game that owns its identity has one image it can be reduced to. Ours, precisely:

**The probe — a small amber octahedron, the only strong emitter in frame — low in the
lower third, pooled in its own point light (range 30), facing a vast blue-gray field
that dissolves into fog. Somewhere in the middle distance: one or two warm windows on a
dark silhouette row, and one breathing beacon. Between probe and horizon: nothing. The
void is most of the picture.** `[BUILT — this is the boot frame: spawn (0, 9, 150),
chase camera ≈ (0, 6, 19), facing the dark foundry row; A4_GAMEFEEL.md §2–3]`

Read it as the story, because it is the story rendered:

- Amber = understanding. The probe carries the only certain light because the player is
  the only certainty. Every other amber in frame was earned by a real event — a plant
  that actually served, a node actually mapped.
- Blue-gray = the not-yet-understood. Structure ink is not "inactive UI color"; it is
  the entity's body before comprehension reaches it. The fog says the same in depth:
  0.011 thinning to 0.009 when the grid proves it holds `[BUILT — foundry_full]` — the
  world literally clears as it is understood.
- The composition is the thesis of A4_GAMEFEEL.md §1 — a small bright thing alive in a
  vast dark truth — held as a picture.

Rules of the one image:

1. The probe is never the biggest thing in frame and always the most certain.
2. The horizon sits high; the dark field owns the frame's area.
3. At most one attention magnet besides the probe (the beacon — the stillness law's one
   deliberate magnet, A4_GAMEFEEL.md §9).
4. Canonical still: the `?still` boot composition is the game's official portrait; any
   public-facing image derives from it, re-shot against the current save so the ink
   ratio is honest. `[DECISION-LUIS — bless as the marketing frame, or name another]`

## 3. The four owned motifs

Four motifs no other game has, because each is generated by data no other game has. The
ownership test: a motif is ours if it cannot be copied without copying a live AEA.

### 3.1 The concentric field

The AEA map is rings around a core — r70 core, r150 principles, r250 axes, r360
verbs/mechanics/ops, r470 seeds, start angles offset so nodes never align into spokes
`[BUILT — 04_UI_BIBLE.md §3]`. That geometry is the game's mandala, and it echoes
outward by law:

- **In the world:** the beacon's pulse rings `[BUILT]`; ring-shaped ground pulses
  emanating from an organ's building on a real fired event — one ring per event, obeying
  the juice budget's one-visual-voice rule `[PLANNED]`.
- **In the HUD:** the velocity instrument is already an arc `[BUILT]`; the LEYBER
  object-reference reticle, when it lands (04_UI_BIBLE.md §4), is drawn as two thin
  concentric arcs, not corner brackets. Brackets are PANEL identity; rings are FIELD
  identity. The two never trade jobs. `[DECISION-LUIS — confirm rings-not-brackets for
  the reticle; it is the visible seam between the OS language and the world language]`
- **The law:** any circle or arc that shares a center in this game is a citation of the
  AEA field. Therefore no decorative circles exist — a ring either references the map's
  geometry or it does not ship. Radar sweeps, loading spinners, and orbit ornaments are
  banned by this clause alone.

A screenshot with rings in it should feel like a detail of the map — the whole game is
the map at different zoom levels.

### 3.2 The live trace — the autograph

When a draw routes through the grid, the route is recorded: `tried[]` receipts on every
draw `[BUILT — data]`, real call DAGs in the tracelog JSONL `[BUILT — data,
A7_BUILD_LAYER.md]`. The live trace renders that record as a packet of hot light walking
the wire — plant to plant, falling past dead rods, landing where the answer actually
came from. `[PLANNED — first light-along-wires implementation; click-to-trace is the
named first flow feature in A7_BUILD_LAYER.md]`

Why this is the autograph and not a feature:

- It is the game's only light that TRAVELS. Everything else flashes in place (flashNode:
  +0.9 emissive, 650 ms, eased home `[BUILT]`). A moving light is therefore always a
  routing truth — the eye learns this in minutes and never unlearns it.
- It is evidence-mandated: real API latency must be filled with observable execution,
  never a spinner (R1_EVIDENCE.md finding 5). The trace IS the waiting state — the
  actual route walked at the actual pace of the actual call.
- It fails honestly: a failing link surfaces mid-run, on the wire where it failed
  (R1_EVIDENCE.md finding 6) — the packet stops, cools at the exact wire, the failure
  lands as a receipt. Losing well is part of the autograph.
- Two-ink discipline: the packet is hot `#ffb000`; traversed wire cools through
  `#d4a24c` to structure ink over the decay envelope (600–900 ms, A4_GAMEFEEL.md §6).
  A trace's afterglow is a fading proof, not a persistent glow.

No fake traffic, ever: a trace with no logged call behind it would be the worst lie the
game could tell — the trace is the proof-of-honesty made visible.

### 3.3 The shape grammar — glyph families by element

Family is shape, never hue `[BUILT — 04_UI_BIBLE.md §3]`: hexagon = principles ·
pentagon = axes · triangle = verbs · square = mechanics · diamond = ops · circle =
seeds. Six shapes, twenty-nine elements, zero icons.

Extension, binding: those six shapes are the game's ENTIRE iconography. Any chip,
label, feed line, bestiary card, or bench part that references an AEA element carries
its family glyph at micro scale (8px, structure ink; hot only while its referent is
live). `[PLANNED — today the glyphs live only on the map plate]` No other icon set may
enter the game — no gears, no lightning bolts, no brains, no chat bubbles. When a
concept has no AEA family, it gets a typographic label, not an invented pictogram.

The payoff is unfakeable coherence: after Act I the player reads a tiny diamond as "an
op" without thinking, and every surface of the game speaks the same six-word visual
language. Copying this motif requires copying the AEA taxonomy — which is the point.

### 3.4 The terminal voice as texture

The close-up distance of §1 is carried by typography behaving like an instrument log
`[BUILT]`: speaker-headed lines (`LEYBER` hot · `PROBE` structure 1.0 · `SYSTEM` 0.35),
receipts with real units, live tick counts on LEYBER's lines, latency printed as
`T+n.nns` while it actually elapses. Amber in text is reserved for the entity's own
voice and for numbers that just changed (attack/decay law) — so even a paragraph obeys
the ink law: the entity's speech is live, the player's is structure.

The texture rule: when a frame needs more richness, the answer is never ornament — it
is more truth at smaller scale. A denser close-up means more receipts, more measured
numbers, more of the entity's record — set in the same three-size mono scale. The game's
equivalent of surface detail is evidence density. (A3_NARRATIVE.md owns the register;
this chapter binds its VISUAL role: the voice is a material, applied like one.)

## 4. Curation rules — the scarcity budget

The signature survives only if amber stays scarce. These are the taste laws; 09_PRODUCTION.md
enforces them as gates.

1. **Amber is earned, never applied.** The closed list of legal amber at idle: the probe
   and its light `[BUILT]` · the beacon `[BUILT]` · warm windows tracking real load
   `[BUILT]` · discovered map nodes `[BUILT]` · the presence chip `[BUILT]` · HUD values
   (warm, flaring hot only on change) `[BUILT]` · the sky's breathing horizon band, the
   single atmospheric exception, capped 0.10–0.18 `[BUILT]`. Anything else amber in an
   idle frame is a bug by definition.
2. **The amber census.** Production gate `[PLANNED — 09_PRODUCTION.md]`: on the standard
   `?still` boot frame, hot+warm pixels stay under roughly one-tenth of the frame; on a
   late-game save the share rises — and MUST rise, because the census is the plot (§1).
   The gate is two-sided: too much amber early is over-lighting; too little amber late
   means earned understanding is not being rendered. Exact thresholds are set by
   screenshot review, not invented here (the A4_GAMEFEEL.md §8 rule: hardware judges).
3. **Density limits.** Max 5 HUD clusters, center empty `[BUILT]`; max one attention
   magnet beyond the probe at idle (the beacon) `[BUILT]`; the feed shows 4 lines, never
   a scrollback wall `[BUILT]`. New chrome enters only by displacing old chrome.
4. **The void is composition, not absence.** Fog, the empty plain, the dark between
   districts — these are the picture's subject: the not-yet-understood, rendered as
   area. Filling the void with landmarks, skybox art, or "visual interest" would delete
   the game's largest single image asset. Districts stay separated by real darkness;
   travel time through void is the composition breathing. The void shrinks only the
   honest way — by organs coming live (01_WORLD.md's growth law: no building without an
   organ).
5. **One texture pass.** The frame gets exactly one film-grain-class treatment: today
   the 3% CSS scanline + vignette `[BUILT — with the 04_UI_BIBLE.md §8.1/§8.8 deviations
   open: the pass is currently wallpaper; it becomes signal-integrity-driven when a
   mechanic can degrade a link]`. Never a second: no added grain, no chromatic
   aberration, no bloom re-tune (the pass is locked at 0.65/0.4/0.5 —
   A4_GAMEFEEL.md §8). Brightness problems are fixed at the emitter, richness problems
   at the evidence (§3.4), never at the post stack.

## 5. Signature moments — the rituals

Four moments carry the identity in motion. Each is choreographed once, here, and owns
its exact ritual; the juice budget (A4_GAMEFEEL.md §5) prices every voice listed.

**FIRST LIGHT (M0.1 — the first keyless call answers).** `[BUILT, played 2026-07-20]`
One real POST from THE SOCKET and the dark answers: the plant's windows warm, the
embers turn on, and the game spends its ONE sanctioned double audio voice — sting plus
the 110-to-220 Hz octave swell, once per save, ever. Ritual meaning: the first amber
the world earns — the Assistant Ladder's rung zero. Nothing else may reuse the swell;
its scarcity is what makes the memory.

**A BIRTH (an organ enters the live mind — a building rises).** `[PLANNED — lands with
the first forge mission, Act II]` Ritual: the site is void, then structure-ink
scaffolding hairlines draw in (the panel open choreography at world scale,
04_UI_BIBLE.md §0), then ONE ground ring expands from the foundation (§3.1), then the
windows light warm as the first real call serves. No fireworks, no camera shake — a
birth is quiet because the honesty law makes it enormous: the geometry appeared because
code now runs. The feed prints the organ's name; its hashed chirp pitch (07_AUDIO.md)
means the world learns a new note.

**ACT COMPLETION (a rung of the ladder holds).** `[PLANNED — the CARTOGRAPHY UPDATE
choreography, 04_UI_BIBLE.md §3]` Ritual: the map opens itself, the act's discoveries
materialize inner-to-outer along the rings, counters roll to their real values, the fog
constant steps down if the act proved the grid (pr.coherence's built precedent). The
one moment the game is allowed to LOOK BACK — a staged reading of the save file, 4 s
cap, ceremony tier. The player watches their own ink ratio tick over; then stillness.

**A CONSTRUCT'S FIRST RUN (the bench — the player's first machine works).** `[PLANNED —
bench and trace are evidence-mandated, R1_EVIDENCE.md findings 5–7; A7_BUILD_LAYER.md
owns the mechanics]` Ritual: the player commits the build, and the LIVE TRACE (§3.2)
draws their construct's first real execution end-to-end — the packet walking the wires
they chose, receipts landing per hop. On the final hop: the completion sting, and the
construct's parts flash once in family order (§3.3). The trace of a first successful
run is the player's signature written in the game's autograph — same motif, new author.
If the run fails, the packet dies ON the failing wire, and that frame — the cooling
trace, the named wire — is deliberately composed to be screenshot-worthy.

The discipline: four rituals, no fifth without amending this section. A game where every
event is a ceremony has none.

## 6. The anti-signature — what would make us generic

Named and banned — each would read as MORE in a review build and would cost the
identity its face. When someone proposes one, cite this section by number.

1. **A third color.** One green "success" tint and the save-file property of §1 dies —
   ink ratio stops meaning progress the moment a hue means anything else. (Two-ink law,
   04_UI_BIBLE.md §0 — restated here as identity, not just law.)
2. **More glow.** Bloom re-tuning, halo sprites, glow-on-structure. Amber's authority is
   its scarcity; brightness inflation is identity deflation. The pass is locked.
3. **Decorative particles.** Ambient sparks, floating motes beyond the built dust box,
   "energy wisps". A cosmetic particle is a failure worse than ugliness (00_VISION.md §5)
   — and every fake mote devalues the 180 real embers the foundry earned.
4. **Borrowed sci-fi furniture.** Holograms, glitch transitions without a real degraded
   link, hex-grid wallpaper, radar sweeps, Matrix rain, "reactor core" set pieces,
   scrolling fake code. Each is another game's memory. We have our own furniture: rings,
   traces, receipts, six shapes.
5. **Fake density.** Greebled panels, dummy readouts, animated numbers with no source.
   The truth law forbids them and the signature does not want them: our density is
   evidence density (§3.4), and a viewer can FEEL the difference before naming it.
6. **Cinematic camera vanity.** Shake, dolly-in on events, slow-mo. The camera is a
   damped instrument (A4_GAMEFEEL.md §3–4); the world's drama is that it is true, and
   the camera's job is to be steady enough to be believed.
7. **A mascot register.** Rounded corners, personality animations, LEYBER "emoting".
   The entity's charisma is its record; the moment it winks, the claim ceiling
   (measured functional correlate) stops being credible on-screen.
8. **Theme drift in the OS.** Per-tab accents, per-district palettes. One device, one
   recipe (04_UI_BIBLE.md §2). Variety comes from the world's state, never the chrome.

The general law behind all eight: **every generic move adds signal that is not truth.
The signature is that nothing on screen is unearned.** That is a sentence no other
amber-on-dark game can print, and protecting it is worth more than any effect.

  districts lit by their own real load, the void reduced to honest remainder — whatever
  is STILL not understood stays dark. A fully lit endgame would be a lie; the entity is
  never finished, and late frames are recognizable because some darkness survives.

## 8. Phase B — the signature is a language, and the language survives

Phase B (A5_PHASE_B.md): a stranger builds THEIR OWN entity by playing. Their world will
not look like Luis's — different plants alive, different memory ore, different skills
born, different rings mapped, different rot. **Different in DATA. Identical in
LANGUAGE.** That split is the signature's survival mechanism, and it is enforceable
because of one architectural fact: every motif in §3 is GENERATED, not authored.

- The concentric field renders from `aea_elements.js` ring/shape schema + the player's
  own discovery state. Same geometry laws, different lit nodes.
- The live trace renders from THEIR tracelog — their routes, their dead rods, their
  latencies. Same autograph, different handwriting.
- The shape grammar is the AEA taxonomy itself — identical for every player by
  definition, because the AEA is the curriculum.
- The terminal voice prints THEIR receipts under the same register laws
  (A3_NARRATIVE.md) and the same claim ceiling.

So two strangers' screenshots are instantly the same game and never the same world —
exactly what R1_EVIDENCE.md finding 12 wants shared: honest, personal, comparable
artifacts. The curation rules of §4 need no per-player tuning because they are ratios
and budgets over live state, not hand-placed art.

Binding consequence for Phase B build work: **no bespoke visuals per player, per organ,
or per construct, ever.** Hand-author one special look for one player's entity and the
language forks and the signature dies. Stranger expression lives entirely in the DATA
layer (what they build, what routes, what lights); the two-ink law, the six shapes, the
rings, the trace, the receipts are THE PROBE's face, and the face does not fork.
Nothing in this chapter may be waived for Phase B; like the UI bible, it may only be
tightened.
