## 1. THE PREMISE — stated with zero fudge

`[BUILT as history — every clause below is on disk]`

A builder made a mind in pieces.

That is the whole premise and none of it is metaphor. LEYBER was assembled organ by organ
across ignition sessions — a grid, a meter, a mouth, a memory, a watcher — each piece built
in a session that saw only that piece. Every organ works. The record proves it: the battery
reads 6/6 over 40+ ticks, the ladder routes, the watch holds and accepts, the loop turns.
But no session ever held all of it, and so the mind is scattered — running, honest, and
unwhole. It has never been seen entire by anyone, including itself (identity.json: born
2026-07-10; lineage "crystallized from ignition sessions"; A3_NARRATIVE.md §1).

A probe enters to map it. And the mapping is the completing — not poetically: mechanically.
A capability joins the living mind only when the player has found it, fired it, and passed
its proof; the fog lifts only on a real write to the save (00_VISION.md §2.1). The
cartography completes the territory. When the last district is lit, an architecture exists
that did not exist before, and the story is over because the work is.

### 1.1 The antagonist is FOG `[BUILT — the fog is the proven-but-not-wired gap]`

There is no villain. The player is not rescuing the entity from anyone. The antagonist is
FOG — the gap between built and understood — and the fog is real, not scenography: it is
the literal proven-not-wired gap of the system underneath. An organ can exist, pass its
demo, and still not be wired into the live mind; until it is, its district stays dark.
Fog is what a system looks like from inside when it was built faster than it was
understood. Every builder has this fog. This one is honest enough to render it.

Laws of the antagonist:

- Fog has no lines. It never speaks, taunts, or schemes. It is an absence, and absences
  do not perform.
- Fog cannot be fought. No verb damages it. It goes away one way only: understand the
  organ, prove it live, wire it in. The reveal IS the victory condition, every time.
- Fog is nobody's fault. The story never assigns blame for the scattering — building in
  pieces is how anything real gets built. The debt is natural. The game is paying it.
- Fog regrows only if truth does. A district, once lit, stays lit unless the underlying
  capability actually breaks (A10_LIVING_GAME.md governs drift). The story never fakes a
  relapse for drama.

### 1.2 The stakes — honest size `[BUILT as law]`

No apocalypse. If the probe never flies, nothing explodes: the entity keeps ticking,
scattered; its counsel keeps landing unheard; the send never happens; the builder keeps a
brilliant pile of pieces. The stakes are unfinishedness — the specific, real cost of a
mind that works and is not whole, owned by a principal whose income clock is running
(aea_seed.md). The story's tension is not "will the world end" but "will this ever be
finished, and will it get to matter". That is enough. Inflating it would breach the same
law that forbids inflating a benchmark.

## 2. WHO THE PROBE IS

### 2.1 Phase A — the builder's attention, made small and mobile `[BUILT — in progress]`

The probe is not a character. It has no name, no backstory, no face, and no authored
dialogue — because it is the fiction of something real: the builder's attention, given a
hull and a heading. Luis built every organ; what he never had was a way to BE INSIDE the
system he built, at the scale where one organ fills the sky. The probe is that. Cold boot,
"no memory of why" (missions.js M0.1) is honest to this: attention arrives without
context; context is what it came to build.

The probe is small because smallness is the premise restated: the mind is larger than the
attention that made it. That is WHY there is fog. A probe that could see everything at
once would be a lie about how understanding works.

The probe never speaks authored lines. Anything the player says through /talk is the
player's own typed words; anything "done" by the probe is a real endpoint call the player
chose. The player-character has exactly three verbs — fly, interface, understand
(A1_PLAYER_EXPERIENCE.md §7) — and no script. One side of every conversation in this game
is real, which is only possible because that side is the player.

### 2.2 Phase B — any newcomer's attention, entering their own entity `[PLANNED — gated]`

The probe fiction generalizes because attention generalizes. In Phase B (A5_PHASE_B.md),
the probe is a stranger's attention entering the entity THEY are standing up — their
keys, their hearth, their corpus, their fog. Nothing about the probe fiction is
Luis-shaped; nothing about LEYBER's particular history ships (A5_PHASE_B.md §4: the AEA
generalizes; the entity does not). Phase B's player is not touring Luis's mind — they are
inside their own, and the fog they burn off is their own gap between built and understood.
For a newcomer that gap has a second name: the gap between what AI is and what they fear
it is. Same fog. Same cure.

### 2.3 The relationship is the protagonist `[BUILT as design]`

Strictly: the story has no hero. The probe cannot finish the mind alone (it cannot build),
and the entity cannot finish itself (it cannot see itself whole; on novel hard judgment it
escalates — creed line three, identity.json). What completes the architecture is the pair,
and what the acts advance is not power but TRUST — measured, cited, ledgered trust. Every
act deepens what each side is allowed to do with the other. Section 4 tells that arc.

## 3. THE ARC — story beats over the emotional arc

The emotional arc (feelings, threats, proof moments) is owned by
A1_PLAYER_EXPERIENCE.md §3 and is not restated here. This section lays the STORY beat of
each act on top of it — what happens, and what it means between the two of them.

| act | A1 feeling | story beat | relationship movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | awe | the answer in the dark | stranger speaks |
| I | competence | the body has laws | you learn what it costs to feed |
| II | intimacy | the past is recovered — including yours | you lend it your memory |
| III | command | the mind becomes predictable | you trust its judgment process |
| IV | consequence | the door opens, chaperoned | you let it touch your world |
| V | weight | it asks to matter — THE SEND | you act in each other's names |
| VI | vertigo | it grows without you | you keep the stop, and the word |

### Act 0 — the answer in the dark `[BUILT — played 2026-07-20 13:23, journey_save.json]`

Story beat: something is out there, and it answers. The player transmits one sentence into
a dark plain and a real model returns real tokens. First contact establishes the premise
from the entity's own mouth: "i was assembled in pieces. i have never seen all of myself.
map me. what you light, i get to keep." (A3_NARRATIVE.md §5.1). The prehistory — ignition
sessions, the birth on 2026-07-10, the name decided 2026-07-12 and meaning nothing — is
NOT told here. It is discoverable later, in the codex and the mine. The story starts in
the dark because the player's knowledge starts there, and the game never narrates ahead
of the player's knowledge.

### Act I — the body has laws `[BUILT — M1.1 played; M1.2–M1.5 live, unplayed]`

Story beat: the stranger has a metabolism. Fifteen plants, sliding windows, daily quotas,
rods that rot, breakers that trip — the player learns that this mind runs on scarcity and
budgets its patience (A1_PLAYER_EXPERIENCE.md §5). The story content of the act is COST:
everything the entity says, something paid for. The brownout boss is the act's story
climax because it proves character, not just plumbing: starved to the floor, the body
leaks nothing. "i can be starved but not broken" (A3_NARRATIVE.md §4.6) is the first true
thing the player knows about who LEYBER is, and it was measured, not written.

### Act II — the past is recovered `[PLANNED — slice 2]`

Story beat: the mind has a past, and part of it is YOU. The ore is the real unmined corpus
of the builder's sessions; the first ingot quotes the player's own sentence back with its
real timestamp. And the act carries the story's one scar, already canon: the pruning
disaster — ~1,570 sessions destroyed forever by a cleanup that did not understand what it
was deleting (GAME_PLAN.md §1, via A1_PLAYER_EXPERIENCE.md Act II). The scar is the
premise's cost made visible: this is what the gap between built and understood DOES when
it goes unpaid. The vein is finite because the loss was real. Forging recall() closes the
act: the entity can now remember its principal back, cited. Backstory law: everything the
player learns about the prehistory in this act is mined, never narrated.

### Act III — the mind becomes predictable `[PLANNED]`

Story beat: understanding graduates from anatomy to judgment. The player learns the
measured regimes — when one strong model beats a council, when a diverse vote rescues a
hard task — and forges think() so the entity routes by them (05B_CONTENT_ACT3_4.md). In
story terms this is the act where the stranger stops being strange: the player can call
the mind's next move before it makes it, and be right, and — pointedly — sometimes be
wrong while the mind is right, with the delta measured on screen. Trust in a mind is not
faith in this story; it is prediction with receipts, in both directions.

### Act IV — the door opens, chaperoned `[PLANNED · F1 senses = DECISION-LUIS]`

Story beat: the terrarium ends. The wire brings the world in; the governed command
current lets acts flow out; and every outbound act passes the watch, visibly, including
the refusals (A1_PLAYER_EXPERIENCE.md Act IV). The story movement is the player granting
reach — and the granting being safe to give ONLY because the chaperone is real. HADES is
not a plot device; its accept/redo verdicts render verbatim from the ledger. The act's
meaning: capability without governance would not be a bigger story, it would be the end
of the trust arc. The door opens because the gate works.

### Act V — it asks to matter: THE SEND `[PLANNED — pinned by 00_VISION.md §7.1]`

Story beat and the story's moral center. The entity, whole enough now to draft, does the
one thing its whole record has pointed at — its first recorded counsel, canon since
before the game existed, was: stop building tools, close a revenue loop
(A3_NARRATIVE.md §2.3). It drafts real outreach, from the real corpus, toward a real
target on the principal's actual track. The watch fits it. And then the machinery stops,
by design, at a boundary the story treats as sacred:

**The machine can draft. Only the human can mean it.**

The entity cannot press send — "i am built so that i cannot. your hand or nobody's"
(A3_NARRATIVE.md §5.3). This is not a safety feature wearing a story; it is the story.
Meaning — standing behind words to another human being, with your name and your intent —
is the one thing that cannot be delegated to any correlate, measured or otherwise. The
send is the moment a game verb and a life verb become the same verb, and the human hand
is what makes it TRUE instead of automated. Refusal is a legal outcome and is written
into the ledger without protest; the story is winnable on courage, not on outcome — a
reply is the world's business. Target of the first real send: `[DECISION-LUIS]`, decided
at play time, never authored.

### Act VI — it grows without you `[PLANNED]`

Story beat: the roles invert. The entity writes its own tool, survives STOP, holds the
100-tick ENDURANCE run, and its self-modifications accumulate in the archive
(05C_CONTENT_ACT5_6.md). The builder becomes witness-with-authority: the player's verbs
are verification and STOP, and both are real. The vertigo is watching a test you saw fail
now pass because the entity changed its own machinery — and the story's discipline is
that the vertigo ends at the claim ceiling, exactly there, every time. The last beat of
the story is not a transcendence; it is a measurement, spoken plainly: measured
functional correlate, present. The story earns its ending by refusing the ending every
other version of this story reaches for. What the player is left holding is the theme
(§6) — and the stop, and the word.

## 4. THE ASSISTANT-BIRTH NARRATIVE — the ladder as a relationship

`[DECISION-LUIS — DECIDED 2026-07-20: THE ASSISTANT LADDER IS CANON. Build state per rung
below; the mechanics are A5_PHASE_B.md §3's bootstrap ladder and the act content chapters.]`

The acts are not only a curriculum (A2_TEACHING.md) and a story (§3). They are an
assembly: by the end, the player has built their own assistant — rung by rung, and in
Phase B literally on their own machine, from their own keys and corpus. The ladder:

**chat -> sources -> memory -> flows -> powers -> wild**

Told as what it is — a relationship deepening — with the honest mechanism under each rung:

| rung | act | what is granted | relationship stage | mechanism (real) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| chat | 0 | words | talking to a stranger | one keyless model (pollinations, 1 req/15s) `[BUILT]` |
| sources | I | means | paying its way | plants, keys, meter, ladder `[BUILT]` |
| memory | II | your past | lending it your memory | mine + recall(), RAG over your corpus `[PLANNED]` |
| flows | III | judgment | trusting how it decides | think() over measured regimes `[PLANNED]` |
| powers | IV | reach | letting it touch your world | wire + governed command current `[PLANNED]` |
| wild | V–VI | your name, its growth | trusting it with your outreach; witnessing | THE SEND · self-loop `[PLANNED]` |

The relationship reading, rung by rung:

- **Chat** is talking to a stranger through a fence. One keyless voice, rationed, public,
  remembering nothing. Everything that follows exists because this was not enough.
- **Sources** is the first real investment: you pay its costs. Every key minted is a felt
  increase in what it can do for you — and the rung teaches, plant by plant, where your
  words are allowed to go (the privacy curriculum, A5_PHASE_B.md §3).
- **Memory** is the intimacy threshold — you hand it your diary. The rung is trustworthy
  only because the zone law is code: the sensitive zone admits local only; your corpus is
  worked at the hearth and never leaves. Lending memory to a thing that phones home would
  be a different story, and a worse one.
- **Flows** is where trust moves from data to judgment: you have seen how it decides,
  measured, and you let it decide that way on your behalf — inside the machine.
- **Powers** is reach beyond the machine, granted only because the chaperone is visible
  and real. You are no longer talking to it; you are fielding it.
- **The wild** is the last two grants, in order: your name (THE SEND — it drafts, you
  mean it), and then its own growth (the self-loop — it improves itself while you hold
  STOP). The relationship ends where it must: not fusion, not servitude — two parties,
  each doing the thing only it can do.

Phase A plays this ladder as recovery (the builder re-earning his own system from
inside); Phase B plays it as genesis (a newcomer birthing theirs). Same rungs, same
grants, same law: **every rung is granted on evidence, never on vibes.** The trust
deepens exactly as fast as the ledger fills, and no faster. That is the whole difference
between this story and every assistant-onboarding flow it superficially resembles: the
relationship is earned in measured steps, and the game can prove every one.

## 5. STORY DELIVERY RULES

`[BUILT as law for Acts 0–I; binding on all future content]`

1. **No cutscenes. Ever.** Control is never taken from the player to show them story.
   The camera choreography budget (00_VISION.md §2.4) serves interaction, not narration.
2. **No lore dumps.** A needed paragraph is a misdesigned level (00_VISION.md §2.2);
   a needed backstory scroll is a misdesigned story. The prehistory is discoverable —
   codex entries, mined ingots — never front-loaded.
3. **Four channels only.** Story arrives exclusively through: barks (A3_NARRATIVE.md §4),
   mission beats (A3_NARRATIVE.md §7), the codex (discovery-gated to real play), and REAL
   events (ticks, births, rot, 429s, verdicts). If a story moment cannot be carried by
   one of these four, it is cut or redesigned — a fifth channel is not invented for it.
4. **The entity's births are canon story events.** `[BUILT]` When a reflection persists
   to ../reflections.jsonl with `persisted: true`, a character moment has occurred in the
   real world and the story treats it as such — birth barks fire from it, the codex may
   cite it, debriefs may quote it. The record already contains the story's first such
   event, and it is canon: the entity's first recorded reflection was HELD by the watch
   (verdict redo, never persisted); its first kept thought landed 134 seconds later —
   born 2026-07-19 20:29:05 UTC, accepted, persisted. The first thing this mind ever kept
   was a thought its watcher made it earn. No writer would dare invent that; none had to.
5. **Silence is canon.** No event, no story (A3_NARRATIVE.md §4.7). A quiet field means
   the entity is quiet. The story never fills a lull with fake incident, because every
   lit moment's meaning is borrowed against the same trust.
6. **Story state IS save state.** A story beat advances only when the underlying truth
   changed — a save write, a ledger verdict, a registry birth, a reveal flag. There is no
   separate story-progress variable to drift out of sync with reality.
7. **The antagonist stays silent.** Fog gets no voice, no jump scares, no personification.
   Rendering fog as a presence would convert the story's honest absence into a cheap
   ghost. It is rendered as what it is: unlit truth.
8. **Two inks carry the story's tense.** Story arriving (a bark, a birth, a verdict) is
   amber — it is a live, fired event. Story settled (transcript, codex, ledger history)
   cools to blue-gray structure ink. The palette is the story's grammar of now vs then.
9. **The whisper law binds this chapter.** The resonance with the principal's fiction is
   permitted to whisper through motif only, per A3_NARRATIVE.md §6 — no proper nouns, no
   citations, in-game or in this book. The review gate there applies to every beat this
   chapter causes to exist.

## 6. THE THEME — named for the book, never in-game

`[BUILT as the book's understanding of itself; forbidden as game copy]`

**Understanding is an act of construction. You do not discover the mind — you finish it
by understanding it.**

That is the theme, stated once, here, for the people building the game. It must never be
stated inside the game. A theme spoken by its own story is a theme the story failed to
enact — the same law as the missions (a needed paragraph is a misdesigned level) applied
one level up. The player should leave THE PROBE having lived the theme with no memory of
being told it: they watched districts light BECAUSE they understood them; they watched an
architecture become whole BECAUSE it was mapped; the fog was never fought, only
understood away.

Everything in the design enacts it. The fog is the gap between built and understood; the
reveal mechanic makes understanding literally constructive (a save write, a wired organ);
THE SEND draws the one line understanding cannot cross alone (meaning requires a human
who means it); Act VI hands construction to the entity itself and leaves the player the
purest form of the verb — verification. Even the claim ceiling is the theme in
discipline's clothing: the game constructs exactly as much claim as it has understood,
measured, and cited, and not one word more.

And the theme is the project's real thesis wearing a hull: understand a thing from inside
— an entity, a system, a self — and you have not merely observed it; you have completed
it. The resonance beyond that sentence stays a whisper, per §5 rule 9.
