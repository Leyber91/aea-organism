something the earlier sheet could not: a state, a projection, a dimension, a context.

## Established conventions to keep

- Designation codes: two letters, a dash, two digits — `SR-41`, `OB-02`, `DL-07`, `PH-01`.
- Every seal or plate carries the code, the plain name, and where it exists the real proof file.
- Title blocks read: DIAGRAM ID, REVISION, SCALE, DISTRICT (always `ECHO-07`), CATALOG STATUS.
- The glyph strip appears top-right on every atlas sheet with the note "six glyphs. one language."
- Stat blocks are three lines maximum and only ever carry measured facts.

## What is still missing, and why round 3 exists

Everything above is **frozen and beautiful**. None of it shows: an object changing state, two
objects at a shared scale, an orthographic projection a modeller could build from, the composed
game screen as a player sees it, or the honest negative states (the unbuilt, the fogged, the
dead). That is round 3.
