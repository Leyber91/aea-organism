## THE TOXIC COMBINATIONS WE EXPECT TO FIND

`Ordo fallax`: combinations that run, produce output, and are worse than not running.

**1. `Auratus gravis` returns, and it is the only one already measured.** A frame handed to a rod that
did not need it. `nemotron-3-ultra-550b` went bare 1.00 to framed 0.25, damaged at every temperature.
**PREDICTION: at least one rod in the L2 set loses accuracy when framed, and the loss exceeds ten
points.** If no rod is harmed, the harm was specific to one rod or one plant and `Auratus gravis` needs
demoting from a law to an anecdote.

**2. `Tumens propositi`**, *the swelling one, of the objective*. `append` seated without `consolidate`.
**PREDICTION: the objective grows without bound and then truncates**, reproducing exactly what free-form
notes did at L5, where a checkpoint became 4801 characters of a rod's own deliberation and hit the token
cap in 100% of trials. The same shape, one level down, in a different organ. If append stays bounded on
its own, that finding was about free-form text rather than about accumulation, and it narrows.

**3. `Coartans` seated with nothing to compress.** `consolidate` above an absent `set`, which is
`Arbiter vacui` wearing a different component. **PREDICTION: it returns a confident, empty objective and
no error**, because nothing in the bench checks a precondition before running a part.

**4. THE FRAME above an absent GOAL.** A method with no objective: *how* stated, *what* missing.
**PREDICTION: total failure, matching L0's 0 of 304.** A frame cannot supply a goal, because they answer
different questions. If framing rescues a goal-less call even partially, C-12's hard floor is softer than
Chapter I claimed and that chapter needs a second correction.

## THE UNCONVENTIONAL COMBINATIONS WE EXPECT TO WORK

`Ordo obliquus`: combinations that skip a rung below and function anyway. If these work, the ordering
claim weakens further, and it already has one counterexample from L1.

**5. FRAME without MEASURE.** Skip L1's scorer entirely. **PREDICTION: it works.** The answers get better
and nobody can tell, which makes it functional in output and blind in knowledge. This is the sharpest
test of C-63 available at this level: if a rung two levels up runs fine with a rung below it missing, the
ordering claim is describing necessity of *knowledge* rather than necessity of *operation*.

**6. FRAME plus READOUT without MEASURE.** Both instruments, no scorer. **PREDICTION: it works, and this
is where the readout finally pays.**

**7. `objective.set` without THE FRAME.** An objective carried with no method named. **PREDICTION: it
works and adds little**, because C-12 already established that stating the goal is what matters and this
only moves where the goal is stated from.
