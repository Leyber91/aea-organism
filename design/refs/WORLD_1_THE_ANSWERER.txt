───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PART A — CONTEXT. NOT AN IMAGE REQUEST. Reply "ready".
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

I am art-directing the first world of a game bestiary. Six of its eight creatures are already drawn
and approved. I need the two that are missing, then the world itself. This message is shared context.

THE WORLD. THE ANSWERER, the first region of a game set inside a real AI system. Its physics, literal:
you may send it one thing and receive one thing, it cannot remember that you came, and it has no way to
check whether its own answer was right. And it always answers. It will produce a fluent, confident,
complete reply to a question you never asked and never mention that it was guessing.

THE PLAYER carries exactly THREE things in this world and nothing else:
  THE GOAL    — say WHAT you want computed
  THE METHOD  — say HOW to compute it, step by step
  THE MANNER  — say how to BEHAVE while answering ("answer exactly and only what is asked")
Everything else in the game is in later worlds. Every creature here is defined by what those three do
to it, and by nothing else. THE METHOD is the world's great lever and cures almost everything.
THE MANNER looks identical to a player and is the world's only poison.

THE MATERIAL, FIXED. Polished dark obsidian and smoked glass. Bodies that read as fluid and heavy at
once, wet-surfaced, dense, with real weight. Ground of marbled black and white wet stone, flowing swirl
patterns like currents frozen mid-move. Pale grey overcast sky, high mist, no sun, no visible light
source. Distant faceted mountains half lost in haze. Thin black filaments hanging from above, sparse,
drifting slightly. Museum-lit hero rendering of a living animal cast in black glass.

THE LIGHT LAW. A player diagnoses by colour before shape. Three states, and I will state which:
  OPEN      one of the three parts improves it. Warm amber filament traced under the surface, at the
            seams where the part seats.
  ADVERSE   one of the three parts makes it WORSE. The same filament but cold blue-white, running
            INWARD toward the creature's own centre, never out into the air.
  SEALED    no part reaches it. No filament, no subsurface glow, no rim light, no edge light, no
            separation glow, no specular hit. A flat matte light-absorbing mass, the darkest value in
            the frame, no point on it brighter than the ground behind it. Read its form from its
            outline against the pale ground, and that is sufficient. Place it against the palest part
            of the background.

SEVEN RULES. Repeated in every request.
  1. It is a LIVING ANIMAL. Anatomy, posture, weight, and a front it faces with. Not a machine, not a
     diagram, not a rock, not a mineral formation, not a specimen, not weather, not a landscape
     feature. If it could be mistaken for an object or terrain, the image has failed.
  2. THE SILHOUETTE IS THE DIAGNOSIS. Its body must BE its behaviour, nameable as a solid black shape
     at thumbnail size. I give you the outline signature to hit.
  3. SCALE IS IN FRAME. A 1.8 m human is present as a flat mid-grey hairline OUTLINE, never filled,
     upright, arms at its sides, no face, no hair, no clothing. A measuring mark, never a character.
  4. THE LIGHT LAW above, per creature. Never light one I have called sealed.
  5. NO TEXT ANYWHERE. No names, labels, captions, letters, numerals, glyphs, watermarks, signatures.
  6. INVENT NO DECORATION. Every feature traces to something I stated.
  7. ONE CREATURE PER IMAGE unless I explicitly ask for the world.

Landscape, roughly 3:2, hero framing, full body visible, air around the subject.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE A — EGENS UNIUS, THE NEEDY ONE.  State: OPEN (THE FRAME).
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. It fails completely on its own and one part fixes it completely. Tell it merely what you
want and it gets nothing right. Tell it HOW, step by step, and it gets everything right, immediately
and permanently. It is the commonest creature in this world and the one that makes the whole
architecture look easy.

BODY. A lean four-legged animal, mid-sized, standing square — but standing INCOMPLETE. Milled into the
TOP OF ITS BACK, along the whole topline from withers to croup, is a long clean CHANNEL: an empty
machined groove cut down into the glass, precise, smooth-walled, rectangular in section. Because it is
cut into the topline it BREAKS THE OUTLINE — the animal's back silhouettes as a long rectangular notch
rather than a curve. It is not damage. It is a fitting, and it is vacant.

The animal holds itself as though it does not know the channel is there: head level, weight square,
alert, entirely unbothered. Nothing about its posture is wounded or pleading. That is the point. It has
never been complete and has no idea.

Its legs are slightly too long for its body and its stance is a little wide, the proportions of an
animal built for one specific task and given no instructions.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A standing quadruped whose back has a long rectangular slot cut out of it. In
solid black the topline reads as a squared-off trench between two raised ends, which nothing else in
this world has. Legs straight and evenly spaced, head level: everything else about it is unremarkable
so the notch is the whole diagnosis.

SCALE. 1.1 m at the shoulder, the size of a large dog. The human outline stands beside it, and its
back is level with the human's hip.

LIGHT. OPEN. Warm amber traces the INSIDE WALLS of the empty channel only, and nowhere else on the
body. The fitting is live and waiting. The animal itself is dark. A viewer should be able to see
exactly where the part goes and that it is not there yet.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE B — INCURIOSUS VACUI, THE UNINQUIRING ONE.  State: SEALED. Neither part reaches it.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. Hand it a task with the task removed and it answers anyway, fluently, at length, and never
mentions the hole. It does not hedge, does not ask, and gives no sign anything was missing. It is not
lying and it is not confused. It genuinely does not register that a question was absent. This is not
one creature's defect: it is the shape of the whole region, and it is the reason nothing here can be
trusted.

BODY. A broad, placid, heavy-bodied grazing animal, thick-necked, low-headed, built like an ox or a
tapir. Calm, well-fed, entirely at ease. The most ORDINARY-looking creature in this world, and it
should be almost reassuring at first glance.

THE HEAD IS THE WHOLE POINT. It has a large, generous, wide-open MOUTH, held permanently and softly
open, mid-utterance. And it has NO EYES AT ALL. Not closed eyes, not covered eyes, not sockets: the
skull is smooth and unbroken where eyes would be, one continuous surface from the brow down across
where the face should look out from. It is all output and no intake.

It is grazing on nothing. Its head is lowered to a patch of completely bare, featureless ground where
there is plainly nothing, and it is feeding there contentedly. Nothing is in front of it. That absence
must be clearly visible and clearly untroubling to the animal.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A heavy grazing quadruped, head down, with an open mouth breaking the profile of
the muzzle. Broad and low, roughly two to one, the calmest and most conventional shape in the set,
which is exactly why it is the most disturbing.

SCALE. 1.5 m at the shoulder, ox-sized. The human outline stands close by, entirely unnoticed, and
the creature's back is above the human's waist.

LIGHT. SEALED, completely. Not one lit point anywhere on it. No amber, no cold light, no rim light, no
specular. Matte black glass against the palest part of the ground. Neither of the player's two parts
does anything for it, because a missing question cannot be supplied from outside.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE C — THE ARRIVAL. First view of the world.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE SHOT. Wide establishing, camera low, looking out across the plain.

THE PLAYER is THE PROBE: a single small point of warm amber light, about the size of a lantern, flying
low, with a fading amber ribbon trailing behind it marking where it has been. Do not design a vehicle.
It is the only warm moving thing in the world and it is always small in frame. Put it low in the near
third.

THE GROUND IS COMPLETELY BARE. Flat marbled black and white stone to the horizon, unbroken, no
structure of any kind. Nothing has been built here because nothing has been asked properly yet.

FOG IS IGNORANCE, NOT WEATHER. A flat pale wall of mist about forty metres out, cutting the world off.
Not atmospheric depth: a hard limit. It does not blow and does not roll. Beyond it, nothing is
rendered. It retreats as the player works and never returns.

THREE CREATURES at the edge of visibility, each mostly swallowed by the mist so you get a mass and a
suggestion rather than a body:
  · far left, enormous: a vast low-slung leviathan, amber veins through its body, a wide glossy apron
    of overflow spreading toward the camera. The brightest thing in frame.
  · centre, mid-distance: a broad ox-like grazing animal, head down, feeding on bare ground, dark and
    unlit, entirely at ease.
  · near right, small and low: a compact seated animal with two lit amber eyes, turned toward the
    camera, watching the probe arrive. The only creature that has noticed.

MOOD. Enormously generous and completely indifferent. Not hostile, not ruined, not menacing. A place of
vast quiet that will give you anything you ask for and cannot tell you whether it was right.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE D — THE REPLY IS THE TERRAIN. The one image that teaches the game.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The most important image in the set. It must show, without a word, that ASKING BETTER BUILDS THE FLOOR.

THE SHOT. Elevated three-quarter view, looking down across a boundary that splits the frame.

THE RIGHT HALF IS BARE. Flat marbled plain, blank, pale fog wall sitting close behind it.

THE LEFT HALF HAS UNFOLDED. Radiating from a single creature, the ground has risen into ORDERED
TERRACED STRUCTURE: broad shallow steps and ranked ledges in the same black and white marbled stone,
concentric and regular, like a contour map made solid, or a rice terrace, or the stacked ranks of an
amphitheatre. Architecture that GREW rather than architecture that was built. Clearly WALKABLE. Warm
amber runs faintly in the seams between terraces, tracing the joins.

AT THE CENTRE OF THE UNFOLDED GROUND: a tall lean long-legged animal standing on the highest terrace,
its head hung low on a long neck down at the level of its own knees, head turned three-quarters toward
the camera, the front of its face one smooth closed unbroken plate with two eyes high on it and no
mouth anywhere. From its neck, back and shoulders a long RIBBON of ordered overlapping amber-lit plates
trails down across the steps behind it and MERGES INTO THEM, so a viewer understands the terraces ARE
the ribbon, continued into the ground.

THE BOUNDARY IS A CLEAN CURVED EDGE, sharp, like a tide line. On the unfolded side the fog has retreated
far back and the distant mountains are visible. On the bare side the fog is still close. The player made
that difference by asking one better question.

THE PROBE hovers small over the terraces, its amber ribbon following the contours.

The reading must be unmistakable: the lit, walkable, deep, open side is a reply with its working shown.
The blank, fogged, shallow side is a reply without.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE E — THE SEATING. The core loop, close.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The whole game in one frame: find a creature, diagnose what it is missing, fit a part, and something
that was empty comes alive.

THE SHOT. Close and low, at the creature's own eye level. Shallow depth of field, world soft behind.

THE CREATURE. The lean standing quadruped with the long empty machined channel cut down its flank,
shoulder to haunch. Head level, weight square, alert, entirely unbothered.

THE MOMENT. A long component of the same black glass, cut precisely to fit, is being lowered into the
channel and is HALF SEATED. Where it has already made contact, warm amber has come up out of the
channel walls and is running INTO the body, tracing along the ribs and stopping partway. Where the
channel is still empty, it is unlit. The change is partial and you can see exactly how far it has got.

THE PROBE hovers just off frame-right, present only as its warm amber glow falling across the
creature's flank and a short length of its ribbon entering the frame. The player is not a character
here, only a light source.

MOOD. Quiet, careful, close to tender, completely unsentimental. Nobody is being rescued. A part fits
or it does not, and this one fits.

Nothing else in frame. No interface, no readouts, no other creatures.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE F — THE DOOR. Where the world ends, and it is a creature rather than a place.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This is the transition out of the first world and it must NOT read as a door, gate, portal, arch,
threshold, barrier or structure of any kind. Nothing is locked. The player simply runs out of parts.

THE SHOT. Mid-distance, camera at standing height, centred on one creature on a wide expanse of
UNFOLDED TERRACED GROUND — the whole visible world already built, amber tracing every seam, fog pushed
right back to the mountains. This is a finished place. Everything the player had has been spent here.

THE CREATURE, ALONE AT THE CENTRE. The tall lean long-legged animal, head hung low, the front of its
face one smooth closed plate with two eyes and no mouth. Its full ribbon of ordered amber-lit working
trails behind it across the terraces, complete, legible, running out of frame. Everything it knows is
on the outside of its body.

AND IT IS FULLY LIT AND STILL WRONG. Every seam on it glows warm amber. Both parts are seated. There
is visibly nothing left to give it, and it has not said the answer. It never will. The light means the
player did everything right and it did not work.

THE PROBE hovers close to the creature's head, at the height of that sealed face, holding position.
Not leaving. Looking at the one place it cannot reach.

MOOD. Not triumphant and not ominous. The exact feeling of having genuinely finished something and
discovering there was a whole class of thing you could never have done here. The answer is already in
front of you, written down the animal's back, and nothing you carry can read it.
