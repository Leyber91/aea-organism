# 10 · THE BENCH PLATE — one frame of the finished game (the main interface, mid-run)

One frame of the finished game THE PROBE: the bench — the game's primary interface — captured
mid-run, rendered as a diegetic milled instrument. This is the single most important interface
reference. Speculative details intentional. Self-contained.

## STYLE LAW (hard constraints)

- Field: one matte near-black milled plate, face-on, filling most of the frame; thin inner rim
  hairline; squared corners; the void dark around its edges.
- Structure ink `rgb(120,155,175)`: ALL engravings, hairlines, chip bodies, quiet text.
- AMBER (`#ffb000`/`#d4a24c`): ONLY the travelling packet on the rail (the brightest element),
  the `1/4RPM` live tick on THE METER, and the faint warm standby inside seated chips.
- Type: IBM-Plex-Mono engraved capitals, tabular numerals, small and exact; EXACTLY the text
  specified below, all legible; nothing else written.
- Register: Territory-Studio / Expanse console — an instrument you could operate. Engraved, not
  printed; machined, not glossy.
- FORBIDDEN: multicolour/neon, gloss/glassmorphism, rounded consumer UI, drop-shadow cards,
  floating holograms, people, gibberish or extra text, watermarks.

## THE GAME (context)

The bench composes real AI parts into a construct and fires it: a REAL model call runs, receipts
print, records stand. Six parts are seated today — the player's rung-2 being, `c-07`, is mid-run.

## THE MOMENT

RUN is live. The packet left THE DRAW 0.6 seconds ago and is passing THE LADDER. The meter shows
one call in the window. The trace has printed three lines so far. Cost estimate held FREE (the
draw fell to the hearth).

## EVERYTHING IN FRAME (complete inventory)

1. **HEADER STRIP (top edge, engraved):** left `THE BENCH · C-07`, right `POWER 1998 LIVE`.
2. **TASK LINE (beneath header, quiet):** `TASK T-04 GROUNDED ANSWER · OPEN TARGET · ZONE PRIVATE`.
3. **THE RAIL (centre, the spine of the plate):** one continuous milled horizontal channel with a
   thin structure-grey light-line along its floor, double-hairline shadowed edges. SIX chips
   mounted on it by small stud joints, left to right, each a small dark instrument badge with a
   glyph, an engraved name, and one stat beneath:
   - `THE DRAW` — filled circular glyph · `POWER 6`
   - `THE FRAME` — pentagon glyph · `—`
   - `THE METER` — circular glyph · `1/4RPM` (this stat ticked live: slightly brighter)
   - `THE LADDER` — circular glyph · `RODS 7`
   - `RECALL` — circular glyph · `MEM 48`
   - `THE MEASURE` — circular glyph · `4 AXES` (seated at the tail)
4. **THE PACKET:** one bright amber point travelling the rail between THE LADDER and RECALL,
   trailing a short fading wake — the brightest element in frame.
5. **THE TRACE (below the rail, three engraved grey lines, newest brightest):**
   `IGNITE c-07 · t-04 GROUNDED ANSWER`
   `DRAW · ZONE PRIVATE · EST FREE`
   `HOP granite -> FELL THROUGH` (the words FELL THROUGH in warm amber)
6. **THE RECORDS LINE (beneath the trace):** `LAST 1.44S · BEST 0.98S`.
7. **FOOTER (bottom edge):** left `RUN — LIVE`, centre `CLEAR [HOLD C]`, right `EXIT [F]`.
8. The plate's rim: a thin inner hairline border; outside it, near-black void. Nothing else.

## COMPOSITION & CAMERA

16:9, the plate face-on with the slightest perspective (top edge a hair farther). The rail is the
compositional spine; the amber packet the single focal point. Everything engraved, aligned to a
strict grid, tabular numbers — an instrument, not a webpage.
