# G3 · THE EARNED TITLE — one frame of the finished game (the naming ceremony)

One frame of the finished game THE PROBE: the moment a composed being earns its NAME. Titles are
never chosen — a construct runs, proves a behavior with a receipt, and the doctrine it
instantiated is branded onto it. A certificate struck in metal, not a level-up banner.
Speculative details intentional. Self-contained.

## STYLE LAW (hard constraints)

- Field: the matte near-black ceremonial plate fills the frame; vast dark margins.
- Structure ink `rgb(120,155,175)`: chip engravings, the receipt line, hairline rules.
- AMBER (`#ffb000`/`#d4a24c`): the branded TITLE letters (freshly hot, the brightest element),
  the thin proof-underline, and the faint warm glow inside the three chips.
- Type: IBM-Plex-Mono engraved capitals; EXACTLY the text specified below, legible; the title
  larger than everything else; nothing else written.
- Register: solemn, precise, earned — quiet pride, zero confetti energy.
- FORBIDDEN: multicolour/neon, badges/ribbons/stars, gloss, glow-everything, people, extra text.

## THE GAME (context)

A construct is identified by its part-signature — the chips it seats. It EARNS a title only when
a run produces a receipt proving a repeatable behavior a bare draw cannot show. The title is the
existing doctrine the behavior instantiated. No receipt, no name.

## THE MOMENT

Construct `c-07` (THE DRAW · RECALL · THE MEASURE) just completed run B: after a full state
reset, it recalled three memories written by run A and its answer measurably changed — grounded,
not hallucinated. The doctrine BACKWARDS CHANNEL is being branded onto it right now; the letters
still carry heat.

## EVERYTHING IN FRAME (complete inventory)

1. **THE PART-SIGNATURE CHAIN, centre:** three small linked machined instrument chips in a
   horizontal row, joined by short hairline links, each glowing faintly warm from the run:
   engraved `THE DRAW` (filled circular glyph) · `RECALL` (circular glyph, stat `MEM 48`) ·
   `THE MEASURE` (stat `4 AXES`).
2. **THE TITLE, above the chain:** engraved amber capitals, larger than all other text, freshly
   branded with a faint heat-glow on the letterforms: `BACKWARDS CHANNEL` — the brightest
   element in frame.
3. **THE PROOF-UNDERLINE:** one thin amber rule beneath the title, connecting down by a hairline
   to the receipt.
4. **THE RECEIPT, below the chain, quiet structure-grey, one line:**
   `RUN B · AFTER RESET · MEM 3 RECALLED -> ANSWER CHANGED · PASS 2.08s · FREE`
5. **THE CONSTRUCT ID, small, lower right corner:** `c-07`.
6. The plate's matte near-black surface with faint engraved hairline rules; vast dark margins.
   Nothing else.

## COMPOSITION & CAMERA

16:9, centred ceremony, straight-on with the faintest downward angle onto the plate. The title
dominates by weight, the chain by placement, the receipt by quietness — read top to bottom:
name, being, proof. Solemn stillness.
