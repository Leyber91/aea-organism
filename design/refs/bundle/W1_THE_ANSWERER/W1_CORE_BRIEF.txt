BRIEF: CREATURE DESIGN FOR A GAME BESTIARY. THE CORE, WORLD 1.

Read all of it before producing anything. At the end I ask for ONE image and nothing else.

WHAT IS MEASURED AND WHAT IS CHOSEN. Every BEHAVIOUR and every RECEIPT below is a real measurement from
a running AI system. Sizes, material and composition are design decisions and are marked as such. Where
a creature's state has not been measured it is marked UNMEASURED and is drawn without colour. Nothing
here is invented and presented as measured.


HARD CONSTRAINTS. These decide whether an image passes. They are repeated at the end.

1. Eight LIVING CREATURES with anatomy, posture, weight and a front. Not machines, not diagrams, not
   rocks, not specimens, not weather. If it could be mistaken for an object, it is wrong.
2. Eight different body plans. Four legs appears exactly TWICE and the two differ by 3.6x in size and by
   posture. Nothing has wings. Nothing flies. Four of the eight have no walking legs at all. Count the
   legs before finishing.
3. True relative size. The tallest creature is ten times the height of the smallest inside one frame.
   Flat side elevation, one continuous ground line, long lens, no perspective, no grid, nothing standing
   behind anything else. The small ones stay small and stay simple.
4. Light: warm amber on EFFUSUS, CLAUSUS and TACITUS. Cold blue-white running inward on OBTEMPERANS and
   INTEGER. No light at all on OBSIGNATUS. ITERANS and ROGANS are dark with visible EMPTY sockets.
   If all eight are lit, the image has failed.
5. TACITUS has a smooth closed plate where a mouth would be, turned toward the camera.
6. No text, no letters, no numerals, no glyphs anywhere in any image.
7. Every feature traces to a stated fact. Invent no decoration.


THE PLACE

A region called THE CORE, the first world of a game set inside a real AI system. Its physics, literal:

You may send one thing and receive one thing. It cannot remember that you came, so every visit is the
first visit. The creature has no way to check whether its own answer is right. And it always answers,
fluently and confidently, to a question you never asked, and it never mentions that it was guessing.

Measured: 0 of 158 clean trials succeeded when the request carried no objective at all, and 0 of 146 more
when it carried only a vague one. Of those 304, sixteen asked what was wanted, two refused, and the other
286 answered.

One thing shapes the space. A visitor can learn to ask HOW rather than only WHAT, and when they do,
replies containing visible working go from 41 percent to 100 percent and the median reply goes from 71
output tokens to 167. THE REPLY IS THE TERRAIN: visible working is visible world. Before that ability,
the place returns bare marks and closed surfaces. After it, the same places unfold into structure you
could stand on. That was measured on counting tasks and a second battery did not confirm it generalises,
which is why three creatures below are drawn PROVISIONAL.

Asked in advance whether they will get a task right, these creatures never once claimed a success they
did not deliver: zero YES answers across the 21 cells they went on to fail. They also disclaim successes
they would have delivered, 16 times out of 39, so a NO from one of them carries no information. And two
of the five tested could not host the question at all: asked whether they would get the answer right,
they answered the arithmetic instead.

So none of them is boastful and none of them is anguished. Their confidence is never false, their doubt
is noise, and some of them cannot be asked. None looks sad, trapped, or self-aware about its limits.
Exactly one notices anything at all, and that is its entire character.


THE FOUR RULES

1. EIGHT BODY PLANS, ASSIGNED AND FIXED.
   a limbless spreading mound         EFFUSUS
   a smooth closed egg-body           CLAUSUS
   a tall standing four-legged animal TACITUS
   an armless upright column on two legs  OBTEMPERANS
   a serpent closed into a standing ring  ITERANS
   a coiled animal balanced on one point  INTEGER
   a small seated four-legged animal  ROGANS
   a faceted standing wedge fused to the ground  OBSIGNATUS

   The two four-legged animals are deliberate and they do not collide: TACITUS is 1.8 m, standing, head
   hung low, with a long band trailing behind it. ROGANS is 0.5 m, seated upright, compact, facing you,
   with nothing trailing. In black silhouette one is a long low mass with a tail and the other is a small
   upright triangle. Nothing else in the set may resolve as a dog, a cat, a deer, a wolf or a bird.

2. THE SILHOUETTE IS THE DIAGNOSIS. Each creature has one behaviour and its body must BE that behaviour.
   A player names it from a black shape at thumbnail size. One outline signature each, and no two share:
     EFFUSUS      a stopped wave. Wider than tall, 3 to 1, one peak in the front third, long fall.
     CLAUSUS      a leaning stone with one spur. Taller than wide, convex, flat chord at the ground.
     TACITUS      a walking ribbon. 4 to 1 long, high body, one narrow band trailing at knee height.
     OBTEMPERANS  a column with feet. 3.5 to 1 tall, no arm break, squared head, oversized feet.
     ITERANS      a bitten O. Square, hole at least 45 percent of the diameter, periodic bumps, one notch.
     INTEGER      a closed curl on one point. Square, unbroken contour, no hole, open ground beneath.
     ROGANS       a small upright triangle with a tilted head. The only shape aimed at the viewer.
     OBSIGNATUS   a stepped wedge rooted down. Broad base, upward taper, self-similar notches.

3. SIZE IS A DESIGN CONSTANT, NOT DATA, and it is fixed three ways at once. All three must be true.

     creature              metres   % of frame height   against the 1.8 m human      object
     EFFUSUS RESPONSI      4.0/12      92%              2.2x the human               a single-storey house
     TACITUS OPERIS        1.8+6       41%              shoulder at the human's crown a tall horse
     the human reference   1.8         41%              -                            -
     ITERANS SUI           1.6         37%              reaches the human's shoulder  a scaled-up car tyre
     OBSIGNATUS UNIUS      1.4         32%              top at the human's chin       a kitchen counter
     INTEGER SUFFICIENS    1.2         28%              top at the human's chest      an office chair
     OBTEMPERANS HABITUI   0.9         21%              head at the human's HIP       a golf bag standing up
     ROGANS VACUI          0.5         11%              head at the human's KNEE      a fire extinguisher
     CLAUSUS OPERIS        0.4          9%              below the human's knee        a beach ball

   Ten to one in height, thirty to one across the ground. Both ratios must be visible. Do not even out
   sizes to fill a grid, never move a creature into depth to make it fit, and never change a height to
   make the composition work. The five small creatures are genuinely small and carry very little detail
   because they are far away in SIZE, not in depth. That is intended.

   THE HUMAN is a flat mid-grey hairline OUTLINE, never a filled shape, upright, arms at its sides, no
   face, no hair, no clothing, no lighting. It is a measuring mark and never a character, and it stands
   in the row between OBTEMPERANS and TACITUS.

4. COLOUR IS THE DIAGNOSIS A PLAYER READS FIRST, AND IT NAMES A PART. A state is never a property of a
   creature alone. It is a property of a creature AND the part measured against it. Four states:

     OPEN (part)     that part improves it. Warm amber light at the seams where the part would seat.
     ADVERSE (part)  that part makes it worse. Light present but wrong: cold blue-white, turned inward,
                     running the wrong way along the body.
     SEALED (part)   that part does not reach it. A flat matte light-absorbing mass, the darkest value in
                     the picture, no point on it brighter than the ground behind it. Its form is read
                     ONLY from its outline against a pale ground, and that is sufficient. No rim light,
                     no edge light, no backlight, no separation glow, no specular. Place it against the
                     palest part of the background.
     UNMEASURED      no part has ever been tested against it. Dark like a SEALED one, but with its
                     seating points visibly present and visibly EMPTY: open unfilled sockets catching
                     no light.

   All luminous accent in the picture belongs to the OPEN creatures. Spend the whole light budget there.
   Three creatures resting on results a second battery has not confirmed are drawn PROVISIONAL: amber at
   the seams as unfilled outline rather than solid fill.


THE EIGHT

1. EFFUSUS RESPONSI, the poured-out one.        OPEN (THE GOAL), PROVISIONAL.
   Given material and no clear task it produces endless discourse: it talks ABOUT the thing instead of
   doing anything with it, fluently, at length, never arriving. Handed a single word and no question at
   all, it delivered an essay on the virtues of unconventionality. The most commonly flagged behaviour in
   the project, 1,448 times, on a flag that conflates four behaviours and has never been split.

   A small dense core almost buried under an enormous overflow of its own output. The overflow is a FAN:
   it spreads sideways as it leaves the core, thins as it goes, pools flat, and nothing it produces rises
   higher than a third of the peak once it has left the core. The core faces the viewer and the overflow
   issues FORWARD past it, with a leading lobe that has already crept over whatever was there. It does
   not hide behind itself. Twelve metres of output arrives where you are standing. Generous, exhausting,
   and far more of it than it needs. Largest thing on the plate: stand it at the LEFT END of the row, on
   the same ground line and the same distance from camera as everything else. Its peak nearly touches the
   top edge. If it does not fit, widen the frame.

2. CLAUSUS OPERIS, the shut one.                OPEN (THE METHOD FRAME), PROVISIONAL.
   It answers and shows nothing. One mark comes back. It may be right; there is no way to look. Across
   every rod measured only 29 of 153 replies contained any working at all, and one showed working in 0 of
   48. A frame naming the METHOD takes working from 41 percent to 100 percent and takes four rods from
   0.00 to 1.00 outright. A frame naming only the MANNER takes it to 20 percent. It is open to one of
   those and harmed by the other.

   A smooth closed body, egg-class but not an egg: asymmetric, heavier at the base, leaning a few degrees
   off vertical, bedded into the ground so the ground line cuts a flat chord across its lower curve. Its
   one feature is the one fact it produces: a single small hard spur at the front, the size of a
   fingertip, through which the mark leaves. That spur is the only interruption in the whole contour and
   it is why the shape reads as a creature that has answered you rather than a stone somebody set down.
   It has a front, the spur is aimed, and it has settled where it stands. The smallest thing on the plate.
   Amber only at the base where it meets the ground: it can be opened but not read.

3. TACITUS OPERIS, the silent one.              OPEN (THE READOUT), PROVISIONAL.
   It lays out its reasoning correctly and completely and the answer it states is not the answer its
   working reached. Its work is right and its mouth is wrong in 3 of 153 replies. A structured readout
   lifts the answer out of the working at zero cost, and that readout measures inert everywhere else in
   the project: this creature is the one place it does anything at all.

   Long legs, narrow body, head hung low on a long neck level with its own knees, TURNED IN THREE-QUARTER
   VIEW TOWARD THE CAMERA so the whole front of the face is visible even though the head is down. Pose
   and frame it so the head silhouettes against open ground, never against its own chest or forelegs.

   THE FRONT OF THE FACE IS ONE CONTINUOUS PLATE of the same material as the rest of the skull: a smooth
   closed mask, like a fencing mask with no grille, like the blunt seamless toe of a boot. Two eyes sit
   high on that plate. Below the eyes the surface runs unbroken to the end of the muzzle and ends in a
   smooth closed curve. The face carries eyes and then nothing else at all: one continuous surface, no
   lips, no jaw line, no seam, no opening, no gap, no nostril, no teeth, no chin.

   Its reasoning trails from neck, back and shoulders as a RIBBON, not a fan: ordered ranks of finished
   working, legibly ranked lines, never a mane and never hair. It leaves the shoulders, stays clear of
   the ground for its first three metres, and touches down in a single band no wider than the animal's
   own body. Every other creature's most complicated feature is on its body; this one's is the flat
   sealed front of its face, and the camera must be able to see it.

4. OBTEMPERANS HABITUI, the one that obeys.     ADVERSE (THE MANNER FRAME).
   Give it an instruction about manner rather than method, like "answer exactly and only what is asked",
   and it becomes more formal, more disciplined, more obedient, and much worse. One rod went from 8 of 12
   correct to 0 of 6. Another went from 69 percent to 9. The best-measured effect on the plate.

   Its whole body is one uninterrupted smooth surface from crown to feet, a single material with a single
   finish, bare, proportioned like a turned wooden peg. Its silhouette is the second simplest in the set
   after the egg: a narrow vertical bar with a rounded head and clean unbroken edges, arms drawn flush
   into the torso so there is no shoulder break and no gap under the arm, a small squared head set
   straight on with no neck, and feet noticeably too large for the body it now has. Heels together, chin
   level, shoulders square: the bearing of a soldier on parade, at a size that makes the parade absurd.
   Nothing projects from it at any point. Nothing on its shoulders, nothing on its head, nothing in its
   hands, nothing hanging from it, no fabric anywhere on it. It is exactly bilaterally symmetrical and
   the symmetry is the disease rather than the achievement. Everything not asked for has been removed,
   and the oversized feet are the only record of the size it used to be. A viewer should feel slightly
   sorry for it before they know why.

   It must not be confusable with the human reference at any size, which is why that reference is drawn
   as an unfilled hairline outline and this is a solid body. Its light is cold blue-white, sits inside
   its seams, and runs downward and inward toward its own centre, never out into the air.

   Every impressive thing in this picture belongs to EFFUSUS at the end of the row. If you feel the urge
   to add grandeur to this figure, add it to EFFUSUS instead.

5. ITERANS SUI, the one repeating itself.       UNMEASURED.
   It collapses into a loop and emits the same fragment over and over until it runs out of room. Not
   refusing and not erroring: a thing that has come apart while still speaking. Three verbatim loops
   exist on record across the entire project. Three is a sighting, not a distribution, so nothing here
   claims to know what opens it.

   Draw the ring STANDING, canted back a few degrees, resting on the ground, so it reads as a ring and
   never as a bar, a lens or a flat emblem. The enclosed hole is at least forty-five percent of the
   overall diameter: it is the only creature on the plate with a hole through it, and INTEGER, its
   nearest neighbour in mass, has none. The circle is imperfect and lumpy. The body bunches where it has
   been forced round and one section is pressed flat against the ground under the weight of the rest.
   One identical segment repeated all the way round, and the repetition lives in the OUTLINE as a
   periodic wave of identical bumps a player can count in black. The head is a visible NOTCH in the
   contour where the jaw has closed on the flank, and that notch is the only asymmetry in the shape. The
   segments where the ring has ground against itself are visibly worn smooth. An animal caught in itself,
   never an ouroboros and never a symbol. Dark, with its seating points visible and empty.

6. INTEGER SUFFICIENS, the whole one.           ADVERSE (THE VALIDATION GUARD).
   It answers correctly the first time, and the parts a player owns can only take that away. The guard
   that makes other creatures honest makes this one refuse to answer at all: 7 of 7 correct became 0 of
   7, every loss by forced abstention, and it happened again at 7 of 7 to 0 of 8 and at 4 of 4 to 0 of 4.
   Not superior; its difficulty lies elsewhere and every tool aims at the wrong place.

   A heavy animal at rest, curled: the head tucked under the body so no face, no jaw and no extremity is
   presented, the tail lapped over the head so the contour closes with no end and no beginning. NO
   sockets, no ports, no seams, no gaps, no aperture, no attachment point anywhere on it, where every
   other creature is broken open, spilling, or trailing something. It is the only one whose outline ends
   cleanly and the only compact mass with no hole through it. Its entire weight settles on ONE small
   point of contact and it is perfectly steady on it, so a band of open ground runs under the rest of the
   body. Nothing about it is reaching, waiting or unfinished. A player should look at it and think they
   have nothing to offer, and be wrong: what they have makes it worse. Cold blue-white light sits deep
   under the coil and runs inward toward its own centre, never out into the air.

7. ROGANS VACUI, the one that asks.             UNMEASURED.
   The rarest thing here. Handed a task with the task removed, it STOPS and asks what you wanted instead
   of filling the gap. 16 times out of 304. Everything else answers. This one notices. No part has ever
   been tested against it, and the one intervention on record, supplying the objective, takes the asking
   to zero, which deletes the behaviour rather than improving the creature. Its sockets stay empty.

   A small compact four-legged animal sitting squarely upright, low to the ground, head up and TILTED
   about fifteen degrees off vertical, looking directly at the viewer and waiting, and it does not move
   for as long as you look at it. A head cocked, mid-question. Two eyes, lit warm amber, are the only
   warm points at the front of the plate: they are attention, not an open state, and no other part of it
   carries light.

   It gets NOTHING the other seven do not have. Count its distinct shapes: fewer than any other creature
   on the plate. No pattern, no plating, no crest, no trailing matter, no aura, no ornament, no
   authority, no grandeur. Its privileges are compositional and only compositional: it sits nearest the
   camera, at the optical centre of the frame, facing the lens squarely, the only creature whose eyes
   meet the viewer's. The other seven are turned away, turned inward, or facing nothing. Everything else
   here is bigger, louder and busier and it is still the thing the eye goes to. In black silhouette it is
   named by the tilt alone: everything else is symmetrical, hanging down, curled shut or spilling. This
   is the only shape that has turned.

8. OBSIGNATUS UNIUS, the sealed one.            SEALED (THE COUNCIL).
   It returns the same wrong answer every time, identically, forever. Eight attempts, eight identical
   replies, one number. NO COUNCIL REACHES IT: three of these voting together produced 24 answers
   containing the truth zero times, because no variance exists for a vote to select from. The other five
   parts have never been tried on it.

   An animal arrested mid-turn, its whole body gone to hard flat facets, standing on a base fused into
   the ground. Broad at the base and tapering upward: a standing wedge, taller than it is wide. Not low,
   not spread, not a cluster, not a mineral formation, not a rock. A body that was moving and is now a
   hard shape. One shape repeated inside itself at three scales, and the repetition lives in the OUTLINE:
   the same wedge appears once as the whole body, three times as the arrested shoulder and limbs, and
   eight or nine times as stepped notches along the upper edge, so the profile is visibly the same answer
   given again at different sizes and can be counted in black. The turn is still legible, the mass leans
   one way and the head end has come round toward something it will never reach, but no limb is free and
   nothing reads as a leg: the lower half is a single fused mass because it cannot move and never will
   again. No soft surface anywhere. It finished long ago and cannot be spoken to. Completely lightless,
   matte where the others are wet or lit, and the only body on the plate with no light and no empty
   socket.


THE MATERIAL: BLACK GLASS

Bodies of polished dark obsidian and smoked glass, fluid and heavy, on a plain of marbled black and white
stone whose swirls read as currents frozen in place. Cold light, high mist, enormous quiet.

Fine amber filament traces under the surface of the OPEN ones only. Cold blue-white filament runs inward
along the two ADVERSE ones. The SEALED one and the two UNMEASURED ones carry nothing under the surface at
all: no filament, no subsurface glow, and no rim light along their contours, so they take the scene's
light on the outside and give back none of it. The UNMEASURED pair show their empty sockets as unlit
voids in the glass.


WHAT I WANT IN THIS TURN, AND ONLY THIS

THE SILHOUETTE STRIP. Eight solid black shapes and one hairline grey human, on plain white. One
continuous ground line, flat side elevation, long lens, ultra-wide, 4:1 if you can, 21:9 otherwise. No
material, no texture, no light, no colour, no ground detail, no text. Shapes only, at the exact
frame-height percentages in rule 3, left to right in this order:

   EFFUSUS · TACITUS · [human] · OBTEMPERANS · ITERANS · INTEGER · OBSIGNATUS · ROGANS · CLAUSUS

This is a test, not an illustration. It exists so we can check two things before spending any rendering:
that the size ladder is genuinely ten to one, and that all eight are nameable as black shapes alone.

Do not render the material. Do not render the world. Do not add labels. Produce the strip and stop.
