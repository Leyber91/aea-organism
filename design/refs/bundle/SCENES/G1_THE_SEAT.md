# G1 · THE SEAT (the compose verb — a part meeting the rail)

**Intent:** the tactile heart of composing: the moment a PART seats onto the bench's lit rail.
Should feel like precision hardware assembly — a component clicking into a machined channel — not
a drag-and-drop icon.

**Composition:** 16:9 close-up on a section of the bench plate, shallow depth of field. The RAIL
runs horizontally: a milled channel with a thin structure-grey light-line at its floor. A part
chip — a small dark instrument badge, hexagonal-edged, engraved `THE DRAW` in small monospace
capitals with a tiny circular glyph — hovers a few millimetres above its seat, descending; a thin
vertical mount-stud beneath it aligns with a socket on the rail. At the exact contact point, a
small precise amber arc of light begins (the seat completing). Left and right along the rail,
other seated chips slightly out of focus (`THE METER`, `THE MEASURE`), quiet and grey. Background:
the plate's matte near-black surface, hairline engravings.

**Camera & mood:** macro-photography intimacy; watchmaking. The amber contact-arc is the only
warmth and the brightest point.
