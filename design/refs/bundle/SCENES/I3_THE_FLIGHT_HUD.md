# I3 · THE FLIGHT HUD (the minimal diegetic overlay)

**Intent:** the target for the in-flight HUD: the thinnest possible instrument layer over the
world — corner-anchored, engraved, never a floating sci-fi hologram collage. The world stays the
star; the HUD is etched glass at the edges.

**Composition:** 16:9. The BACKGROUND is the world of W1 (the dark radial instrument, amber core,
rings) slightly defocused — the HUD is the subject. Overlaid, in thin structure-grey monospace and
hairline geometry, corner-anchored: TOP-LEFT a small reticle bracket with `THE PROBE` and beneath
it `ZONE PRIVATE`; TOP-RIGHT `POWER 1998 LIVE` above a thin horizontal capacity bar (mostly full,
one thin amber sliver marking today's spend); BOTTOM-LEFT a compact attitude cross with a tiny
probe glyph and `V 32`; BOTTOM-RIGHT `MOUTH 240M` (range-to-core) above `[F] BENCH · [TAB] MAP`.
Centre of frame: ONLY a minimal dot-reticle — two thin brackets and a 1px centre point that sits
over the distant amber core. All strokes 1px-thin, all corners squared, generous margins from the
frame edge. Amber appears ONLY in the capacity sliver and where the reticle centre kisses the
core's distant glow.

**Camera & mood:** looking through the probe's instrument glass — everything engraved, nothing
floating. Silence with four quiet corners of truth.
