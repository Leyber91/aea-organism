# I1 · THE BENCH PLATE (the compose interface as a milled instrument)

**Intent:** the target for the game's main interface — the bench where parts are seated and fired.
Today it is a flat text form; this reference specs it as a DIEGETIC INSTRUMENT: a milled dark
plate with a lit rail, mounted chips, and engraved readouts. Territory-Studio / Expanse console
language, our two inks.

**Composition:** 16:9, the plate face-on filling most of the frame, slight perspective. TOP EDGE:
a thin engraved header strip — `THE BENCH · C-01` left, `POWER 1998 LIVE` right, small monospace
capitals. CENTRE: **THE RAIL** — one continuous horizontal milled channel with a thin light-line,
double-hairline shadowed edges (a machined groove). Five chips MOUNTED on it via small stud
joints, evenly seated left→right: `THE DRAW` (head, a filled circular glyph), `THE FRAME`
(pentagon glyph), `THE METER` (`0/4RPM` beneath), `THE LADDER` (`RODS 7`), `THE MEASURE` (tail,
`4 AXES`). Each chip: a small dark instrument badge with the glyph, the name, one live stat —
engraved, not printed. One amber packet mid-transit on the rail between chips (the run is LIVE),
with a short fading wake. BELOW: three quiet trace lines in small grey monospace
(`DRAW t-01 · ZONE PRIVATE`, `HOP granite -> FELL THROUGH`, `PASS · 1.44s · FREE`), then the
records line `LAST 1.44S · BEST 0.98S`. BOTTOM EDGE: `RUN [SPACE]` left, `EXIT [F]` right. All
numbers tabular monospace. The plate's edges carry a thin inner rim hairline; corners squared.

**Camera & mood:** an instrument you could operate — engraved, matte, exact. The amber packet is
the single brightest element; everything else structure-grey on near-black.
