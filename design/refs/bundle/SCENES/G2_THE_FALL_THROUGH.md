# G2 · THE FALL-THROUGH (resilience — rerouting down dead rods to the hearth)

**Intent:** the doctrine RESTORABLE COHERENCE as one image: a draw tries a rod, the rod is dead,
the route falls through — and still lands, on the free hearth, and answers. Failure rerouted, not
failure hidden.

**Composition:** 16:9. A horizontal chain of four rod-nodes floats in the dark, connected by a
thin route-line, left to right. The route enters from the left as a warm amber trace. Rod 1: COLD
AND DEAD — the amber trace reaches it and deflects downward with a small, sharp break (a visible
"refusal notch"), the rod rendered dark with a faint cold-blue X-hairline. Rod 2: also dead, the
trace deflects again — two clean stair-steps of rerouting. Rod 3 (lower right): the HEARTH — a
softly steady warm ember rod; the trace lands on it and IGNITES a small amber completion bloom,
the brightest point. Above the dead rods, tiny engraved monospace notations permitted: `FELL
THROUGH`. Near the hearth: `FREE`. Black void everywhere else.

**Camera & mood:** diagrammatic drama — a subway-map of survival. Severe, clean, honest.
