# W2 · THE IGNITION (the wow moment — a real draw lands)

**Intent:** the game's one sanctioned spectacle: the instant a REAL model draw completes and the
core flares. This images the honesty law at its brightest — the biggest light in the game exists
because something true just happened.

**Composition:** wide 16:9, closer than W1 — the core dominates the lower-centre third. The
faceted amber jewel FLARES: bloom blooming through the fresnel shell, the two tori catching hot
rim-light, the vertical beam brightened. One bold amber pulse with a glowing wake arrives down a
spoke into the core at the moment of flare. The rings and other nodes stay quiet structure-grey —
the flare does NOT wash the world; contrast is the drama. Faint concentric shock-hairline (one
ring of light, thin, expanding) permitted around the core.

**Camera & mood:** held breath → strike. The frame is still mostly black; the flare is
concentrated, not a screen-wide explosion. Precision, not pyrotechnics.

**PROMPT (paste-ready):**
The amber core of a dark radial instrument flaring at the moment of ignition: a small faceted
jewel at centre-frame erupting in concentrated warm amber light through a translucent rim-lit
shell, two razor-thin orbiting tori catching hot edge-light, a thin vertical amber beam rising
brighter. One bold amber pulse of light with a glowing trailing wake arrives along a thin radial
hairline into the core at the instant of flare, and a single thin ring of light expands from the
centre. Around it, faint steel-blue concentric rings and quiet dark octahedral nodes remain calm
and dim. The flare is intense but CONCENTRATED — the frame stays mostly near-black, extreme
contrast, the amber the only warmth and the single brightest element. Austere scientific render,
precise, no lens flares streaking, no people, no text.
