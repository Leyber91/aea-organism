# G3 · THE EARNED TITLE (a being earns its first real name)

**Intent:** the naming payoff: a composed construct has just RUN and proven a behavior, and the
receipt crystallizes into a TITLE. Identity earned by execution, stamped like a certification —
never authored in advance. This is the game's most personal moment.

**Composition:** 16:9, centered ceremony on the dark plate. Centre: the construct's part-signature
rendered as a short horizontal chain of three small linked instrument badges (`THE DRAW · THE
LADDER · THE MEASURE`), each glowing faint warm from the run just completed. Above the chain, the
earned title materializes as engraved amber monospace capitals, larger than any other text:
`RESTORABLE COHERENCE`. The title's letters read as freshly branded — a faint heat-glow on the
engraving. Below the chain, the proving receipt in small quiet structure-grey monospace:
`HOP granite -> FELL THROUGH -> hearth · PASS 1.44s · FREE`. A thin amber underline connects
receipt to title — the proof line. Vast dark margins.

**Camera & mood:** solemn, precise, earned — a certificate struck in metal, not a level-up
banner. No confetti energy; the glow is quiet pride.
