# W3 · THE FOG FRONTIER (earned vs unearned — the metroidvania tell)

**Intent:** one image that teaches the game's deepest rule without a word: amber is EARNED. A lit
organ and a fog organ side by side on the same ring — what you have proven vs what still waits.

**Composition:** 4:3, medium shot low over one ring's arc. LEFT: a dark octahedral node burning
warm amber from within — solid, present, faint heat-shimmer of bloom, a quiet amber reflection on
the ring hairline beneath it. RIGHT, further down the same ring arc: the same octahedral form as a
cold-blue WIREFRAME ghost — hollow, faint, slightly transparent, no light on the ring beneath it.
The thin ring hairline connects them and runs off-frame both ways. Behind: black void, the distant
dim amber core small and out of focus far in the background. Nothing else.

**Camera & mood:** intimate, quiet, a little longing — the fog node is the frontier, sensed but
not yet real. The contrast (solid-lit vs hollow-ghost) IS the story.
