# W1 · THE INSTRUMENT AT REST (the world render's target)

**Intent:** the definitive establishing image of the world — a vast, dark, precise radial
instrument alone in the void, alive but calm. A stranger should think: *"that machine is quietly
alive, and someone flies inside it."*

**Composition:** wide 16:9, high shallow angle (about 25° above the plane), the instrument's
centre placed slightly above frame-centre. Three thin concentric structure-grey rings engraved on
a near-black floor plane that fades to void. The core jewel at the exact centre — small, faceted,
burning amber inside its translucent fresnel shell, two thin tori orbiting it. A thin vertical
amber beam rises from it into the dark. Six small octahedral nodes rest ON the rings: three or
four burn amber from within, two are faint cold-blue wireframe ghosts (FOG). Thin, quiet radial
spoke hairlines from each node to the core. One tiny amber pulse mid-way along one spoke. Far
away, a faint cold horizon band. Everything else: black.

**Camera & mood:** still, silent, austere. Astrophysics-render precision. Vast negative space —
the instrument occupies maybe a third of the frame. The amber core is the single brightest thing.
