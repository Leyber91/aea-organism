# W6 · THE STAKE (the metered reach — real budget burning)

**Intent:** the economy made visible: reaching UP the ladder to a paid cloud rod. The image of
risk — one unit of real budget burning to reach higher than the free hearth. Every builder who has
watched a token meter tighten should feel this image.

**Composition:** portrait 2:3. A vertical LADDER of horizontal rod segments floats in the dark —
five to seven thin bars stacked with even spacing, rendered as dark machined slats edged in faint
steel-blue. At the BOTTOM, the lowest rod glows a soft steady warm ember — the free hearth, always
alive. A thin amber filament climbs the ladder's spine from the hearth upward, past two dim dead
rods (cold, unlit), and REACHES the topmost rod, which ignites hot amber — the paid reach, the
brightest element. Beside the top rod, one small precise notation permitted: `1u`. The rest of the
frame: black void, generous margins.

**Camera & mood:** vertical tension — warmth below (safe), fire above (costly). Minimal,
diagrammatic, severe.
