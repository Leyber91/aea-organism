# W4 · THE FLIGHT (scale — a tiny probe inside a vast mind)

**Intent:** the player-feeling image: you are SMALL, and the thing you fly inside is immense,
calm, and alive. Duskers dread + Interstellar scale, in our two-ink language.

**Composition:** wide 16:9. The probe — a tiny angular dark craft, one warm amber aperture glowing
at its bow — occupies the upper-left third, small (no more than ~4% of frame width). Below and
beyond it, the vast radial instrument recedes: the engraved concentric rings sweeping across the
lower frame in perspective, the amber core burning far away at their centre, two or three organ
nodes as points of amber/cold-blue on the rings, hair-thin spokes. The faint cold horizon band far
beyond. The void dominates — sky above the instrument is pure black.

**Camera & mood:** wide and silent. Slight downward pitch, as if the probe just crested the outer
ring and saw the whole instrument for the first time. Loneliness + awe. No motion blur.

**PROMPT (paste-ready):**
A tiny angular dark probe craft with one warm amber glowing aperture at its bow, flying alone high
above a vast dark radial instrument. Far below it, three immense thin concentric rings of faint
steel-blue hairline light sweep across a matte near-black plane in perspective, with a small
bright amber core jewel burning at their distant centre and a razor-thin vertical amber beam
rising from it; a few small nodes glint amber and cold-blue on the rings, joined to the centre by
hair-thin spokes. A faint cold horizon band far beyond, everything else pure black void. The probe
is very small in frame — the scale is overwhelming. The amber core is the brightest element; the
probe's aperture the second warmth. Cinematic, silent, austere scientific render, no people, no
text, no motion blur.
