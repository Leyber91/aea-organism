# 11 · THE RUN TRACE — one frame of the finished game (the receipt column closing)

One frame of the finished game THE PROBE: the lower bench in close-up at the moment the measure

Honest telemetry as drama. Speculative details intentional. Self-contained.

## STYLE LAW (hard constraints)

- Field: the matte near-black plate region fills the frame; vast quiet margins around the column.
- Structure ink `rgb(120,155,175)`: all engraved text and hairlines; older lines dimmer (a
  recency gradient), newer brighter.
- AMBER: ONLY the state-words — `FELL THROUGH` warm, the final `PASS` hot (the brightest
  element) — plus the thin rule under the verdict and the small filled record square.
- Type: IBM-Plex-Mono engraved, small, tabular numerals; EXACTLY the lines specified below, in
  order, legible; nothing else written.
- FORBIDDEN: multicolour/neon, gloss, progress bars, spinners, rounded UI, people, extra text.

## THE GAME (context)

Every run prints its receipts live, line by line — each line something that really happened. The
tail part, THE MEASURE, closes the wire: no record without the measure. Records stand until
beaten.

## THE MOMENT

Construct `c-07`, task t-04. The draw lost one rod, fell to the hearth, recalled three memories,
and passed in 2.08 seconds, free. The verdict landed one second ago; the record line just
updated.
