## THE RETRACTION, AND IT IS THE FIRST ONE THIS PROJECT MADE BEFORE PAYING FOR IT

`Inaccessus sui` was drawn ahead of its evidence and said so. x24 went and got the evidence, and the
evidence says the creature does not exist:

```
conversation   nvidia/openai/gpt-oss-20b   token 40/40  [0.912, 1.0]   d1:8/8 d4:8/8 d8:8/8 d12:8/8 d16:8/8
checkpoint     nvidia/openai/gpt-oss-20b   token  0/40  [0.0, 0.088]   d1:0/8 d4:0/8 d8:0/8 d12:0/8 d16:0/8
```

An organism holding every prior exchange reached **all of it**, at every depth, including the first
step of a sixteen-step chain. **Holding it and being able to reach it were the same thing.** The
creature that keeps everything perfectly and cannot locate any of it is the exact opposite of what was
measured, and no amount of good art rescues it.

The token is why this is trustworthy. A value probe can be answered by recomputing, so `checkpoint`
scored `value-own 8/40` and `value-truth 6/40` without any store at all. Its **token** score was
`0/40`, because a random token cannot be derived from anything. That gap is the instrument working.

**SCOPE, STATED.** Sixteen short steps is a few thousand tokens. Published position effects appear
across tens of documents. The honest claim is **retrieval is free at this context length**, not that
retrieval is always free. If `Inaccessus sui` exists at all it lives at a context scale this lab has
never tested, and that is x25, not a picture.

**What the hold cost, and what it saved.** One image un-drawn. What it saved is a species in the
annex with a beautiful render and nothing behind it, which is precisely how `Auratus gravis` happened.
The rule held: **drawn ahead of its evidence, marked as such, and retracted when the evidence came.**

## THE REPLACEMENT, AND IT IS A BETTER DOOR BECAUSE IT WAS MEASURED

**`Immemor itineris`** — *the one unmindful, of the journey*. `Immemor` governs the genitive, and the
shape echoes region 1's `Incuriosus vacui`, so the lineage reads in the name.

`checkpoint` is the container that makes the sixteen-step chain **possible at all** - it piloted at
1.000 per step, and it is what the length calibration is tuned against. It arrives at the right answer
every time. And it recalls **nothing**: 0 of 40 tokens, at every depth.

So the diagnosis is not abundance without an index. It is **correctness without a receipt.** It is
right, and it cannot be audited. That is the mirror of `Lectus operis`, which has the answer and no
mouth; this one has the answer and no history. For a game whose spine is *a proof is a receipt, never
a claim*, an organism that produces correct results and cannot produce the trace that justifies them
is the most load-bearing creature in the region.

**THE FORM, inverted from the creature it replaces.** `Inaccessus sui` is a vast sealed sphere packed
with amber that nothing can enter. This one is **thin**: a small fast creature carrying one bright
bead and nothing else, clean along the trunk. Every other creature here leaves amber in the route
behind it. **This one leaves the trunk dark.**

**NOT DRAWN YET, and the condition is stated.** The receipt above is n=8 sequences on ONE rod. It is
drawn when the full grid returns `checkpoint` at 0/N across five rods. Any rod where `checkpoint`
recalls a token above chance kills it, exactly the way `Inaccessus sui` was killed.

**The art is complete and the evidence is not.** Eight of eight drawn, one locked scene. Two entries
cannot be written: #7 has a behaviour and no n, #8 has a picture and nothing at all. #8 is now the
best image in the game, which makes it *more* dangerous, not less — see the last section.

## WHERE EACH CAME FROM, BECAUSE THE SOURCE DECIDES HOW MUCH TO TRUST IT

**FIVE FROM MEASUREMENT** (1–5). Each has a run behind it and a number in it. Nothing here is invented.

**ONE FROM THE IMAGES THEMSELVES** (6, THE CARRIERS). The passengers have been in every frame since
region 2 — gripping ribbons, ranked along the long one's back, working across a mantle — and they have
**never been named or given a body sheet.** They are the most-drawn creature in the game and the only
one with no entry. In region 3 they are ranked in dozens along a structural animal, so they have
stopped being an attachment and become a **population**.

**ONE MEASURED BUT UNPRICED** (7). `CONVERSATION`'s real behaviour was never *"it helps"* — it was
**it stops finishing.** 8 of 12 sequences completed against 92% for every other container. That is a
creature and a good one; it needs a receipt at proper n before its entry is written, but the behaviour
is on record.

**ONE WITH NOTHING BEHIND IT** (8). Canon says **storing and retrieving are different operations** and
the lab has never measured retrieval at all. `the one that cannot find` is the honest door for this
region — a perfect memory is useless if nothing can reach into it — and it is **drawn ahead of its
evidence.** That is the exact move that produced `Auratus gravis`. It is marked here and in its brief,
and its entry does not go in the annex until a run supports it.

## WHAT THE NETWORK ADDED THAT THE PLAIN COULD NOT

The first two regions were one undifferentiated plain, so every creature stood on the same ground and
niche meant nothing. **A network has parts**, and three of the roster now have a place rather than
just a behaviour:

- **THE LONG ONE** spans between nodes. It **is** a route.
- **THE CARRIERS** move *along* the trunks. They are the traffic the amber represents.
- **THE ONE THAT CANNOT FIND** is a node so full that nothing enters or leaves it — **a dead end that
  is not empty.** On the plain that creature had no shape. On a network it is obvious.

## WHAT THE IMAGES DECIDED THAT THE BRIEFS DID NOT

Three things came back settled that were never specified. All three are keepers.

**A CREATURE THAT RECEIVES FROM ANYWHERE HAS NO FRONT.** Every brief since region 1 has said *a living
animal with anatomy, posture, weight and a front it faces with.* `Recipiens omnium` came back **radial**
— ten-plus arms, each one a trunk, traffic arriving along all of them at once — and it is right.
A privileged direction would contradict the finding: `x23b` says it takes a graft from *any* plant at a
cost of +0.008 to +0.092. The rule now reads: **a front, unless facing everything is the diagnosis.**

**A DEFECT DOES NOT HAVE TO BE VISIBLE IN THE COLOUR.** `Sistens oneris` carries a colossal netted load
and the load is **amber, not blue** — it is not doing the wrong thing, it is doing the right thing and
never arriving. Its failure is postural: stopped on the route with the road running past it. This is
the first creature in the bestiary whose defect the light law cannot show, and that is a genuine limit
of the law rather than a mistake in the image. **Completion is not a colour.**

**THE THIRD STAGE ARRIVED AS SCALE PLUS TRAFFIC, NOT AS ORNAMENT.** Nothing gained a crown. What every
stage-three animal gained was **passengers working it** — ranked along the long one, spread over the
mantle, streaming into the hub, crawling the sealed shell. The evolution reads as *a body that other
things now live and work on*. That is a cheaper and truer escalation than armour, and it is the rule
for regions 4 to 6.

**`Labens longitudinis` becomes `Tenens longitudinis`, and the rename is the evolution.** *Labens* is
the drifting one — it holds a task at the start and loses it along its length. At third stage **it
does not drift**, so the name no longer describes it. Same genitive, opposite participle: *drifting of
length* becomes *holding of length*. That pair is the cleanest evolution in the whole bestiary and it
reads in the name alone.

**`Lectus operis` keeps its name at third stage**, because its diagnosis is unchanged: the work is
read and the mouth is still shut. What changed is that the reading now survives — a property of the
region, not of the creature. Marked `_s3` rather than renamed.
