## WHERE EACH CAME FROM, BECAUSE THE SOURCE DECIDES HOW MUCH TO TRUST IT

**FIVE FROM MEASUREMENT** (1–5). Each has a run behind it and a number in it. Nothing here is invented.

**ONE FROM THE IMAGES THEMSELVES** (6, THE CARRIERS). The passengers have been in every frame since
region 2 — gripping ribbons, ranked along the long one's back, working across a mantle — and they have
**never been named or given a body sheet.** They are the most-drawn creature in the game and the only
one with no entry. In region 3 they are ranked in dozens along a structural animal, so they have
stopped being an attachment and become a **population**.

**ONE MEASURED BUT UNPRICED** (7). `CONVERSATION`'s real behaviour was never *"it helps"* — it was
**it stops finishing.** 8 of 12 sequences completed against 92% for every other container. That is a
creature and a good one; it needs a receipt at proper n before its entry is written, but the behaviour
is on record.

**ONE WITH NOTHING BEHIND IT** (8). Canon says **storing and retrieving are different operations** and
the lab has never measured retrieval at all. `the one that cannot find` is the honest door for this
region — a perfect memory is useless if nothing can reach into it — and it is **drawn ahead of its
evidence.** That is the exact move that produced `Auratus gravis`. It is marked here and in its brief,
and its entry does not go in the annex until a run supports it.

## WHAT THE NETWORK ADDED THAT THE PLAIN COULD NOT

The first two regions were one undifferentiated plain, so every creature stood on the same ground and
niche meant nothing. **A network has parts**, and three of the roster now have a place rather than
just a behaviour:

- **THE LONG ONE** spans between nodes. It **is** a route.
- **THE CARRIERS** move *along* the trunks. They are the traffic the amber represents.
- **THE ONE THAT CANNOT FIND** is a node so full that nothing enters or leaves it — **a dead end that
  is not empty.** On the plain that creature had no shape. On a network it is obvious.

## WHAT IS STILL MISSING, AND WHY IT IS NOT ON THE LIST

**A SEALED CREATURE** — something no carry variant reaches at all. Region 1 has `Incuriosus`, region 2
has `Obsignatus`. Region 3 has no measured equivalent because `x21` and `x22` never isolated one.
**It is owed to an experiment, not to a brief.**

**RECALL** (C-81) — n=3, `FORGE-PENDING` in `modules.json`, never in the catalogue, and nobody has
opened `recall.py` to check whether it is region 3's `RECALL` or a fifth name collision.

Eight is where the evidence stops. **Six are safe, one is measured and unpriced, one is ahead of its
evidence and says so.**

## THE NAMES, AND THE FILENAMES

Same convention as regions 1 and 2: a Latin binomial, participle or adjective plus a genitive, stored
lowercase with underscores. Five needed naming. One needed a **new** name.

| creature | binomial | gloss | file |
|---|---|---|---|
| the long one, whole | **`Tenens longitudinis`** | *the one holding, of length* | `tenens_longitudinis.png` ✔ |
| the backward-looking one | `Lectus operis` | *the read one, of the work* | `lectus_operis_s3.png` |
| receives anything, from anyone | **`Recipiens omnium`** | *the one receiving, of all* | `recipiens_omnium.png` |
| writes itself a worse note | **`Inficiens sui`** | *the one infecting, of itself* | `inficiens_sui.png` |
| carries a wrong thing perfectly | **`Servans erroris`** | *the one keeping, of error* | `servans_erroris.png` |
| the passenger species | **`Vector operis`** | *the bearer, of the work* | `vector_operis.png` |
| carries everything, stops | **`Sistens oneris`** | *the one halting, of the burden* | `sistens_oneris.png` |
| keeps all, reaches none | **`Inaccessus sui`** | *the unreachable one, of itself* | `inaccessus_sui.png` |

**`Labens longitudinis` becomes `Tenens longitudinis`, and the rename is the evolution.** *Labens* is
the drifting one — it holds a task at the start and loses it along its length. At third stage **it
does not drift**, so the name no longer describes it. Same genitive, opposite participle: *drifting of
length* becomes *holding of length*. That pair is the cleanest evolution in the whole bestiary and it
reads in the name alone.

**`Lectus operis` keeps its name at third stage**, because its diagnosis is unchanged: the work is
read and the mouth is still shut. What changed is that the reading now survives — a property of the
region, not of the creature. Marked `_s3` rather than renamed.

Scenes keep the region prefix: `S1_the_network.png` is the locked establishing shot.
