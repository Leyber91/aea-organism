WORLD 3 — THREE RADICAL DIRECTIONS. No reference images.
Three prompts. Each in its OWN fresh chat. Each returns THREE images. Nine total.

ATTACH NOTHING. Every attempt that attached the second world came back as a repaint of it, and the
last three came back as TERRACES — which are the FIRST world's signature. The references are
anchoring, and the only inheritance this world needs is a law.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY THREE, AND WHY THESE THREE. The premise has one obvious shape and the model finds it every time:
layers. Sediment, strata, terraces, cross-sections — time as depth. All three directions below are
structurally INCAPABLE of being that, because each answers a different question:

  A · THE OVERGROWTH    the past GROWS OVER you        time becomes VOLUME
  B · THE SUSPENSION    the past is VISIBLE, untouchable   time becomes DEPTH-IN-CLEAR
  C · THE STILL RUNNING the past NEVER STOPPED          time becomes CROWD


╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ SHARED PREAMBLE — identical at the top of all three. Paste it each time.                       ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

I am designing the third region of a game set inside a real AI system. You have not seen the first two
and you do not need to. Design this one from what it IS.

**THE PREMISE, AND IT IS NOT WHAT YOU WILL FIRST ASSUME.** This place keeps everything that has ever
happened to it, and **cannot find any of it.** Not a place that forgets. Not a ruin, not a graveyard.
Nothing here is ever lost — that is precisely the problem. Every exchange, every answer, every
correction is still present, still intact, still exactly where it was put. **Storing and retrieving are
different capabilities, and this world only has the first.** It is drowning in its own perfectly
preserved past. The failure mode is not decay. **It is abundance without an index.**

**FIVE THINGS IT MUST NOT BE.** Say what you are avoiding before you design.
  1. NOT STRATA — no sediment, layers, varves, rings, cross-sections. Time as depth is taken.
  2. NOT TERRACES — no stepped platforms, ranked ledges, rice-terrace or travertine forms.
  3. NOT AN ARCHIVE — no shelves, vaults, filing, indexes, libraries or ruins of libraries.
  4. NOT A GREY OVERCAST PLAIN — give it a light source, or several, or a strange one.
  5. NOT A DESERT — no dry cracked flats, no dunes, no sun-bleached emptiness.

**THE LIGHT LAW — the only thing carried in, and it is not decoration.** A player diagnoses a creature
by colour before shape. Exactly one state per creature:
  · **WARM AMBER** — a live connection, something working. Traced under the surface, at the seams.
  · **COLD BLUE-WHITE** — the wrong thing being done. Same tracery, running INWARD toward the
    creature's own centre, never out into the air.
  · **MATTE LIGHTLESS BLACK** — nothing reaches it. Light-absorbing, no rim light, no specular, no
    glow. Read only as an outline against the palest part of the background.

**RULES.** Every creature is a **LIVING ANIMAL** — anatomy, posture, weight, a front it faces with.
Not a machine, not a diagram, not a rock, not a plant, not architecture. If it could be mistaken for
an object it is wrong. **The silhouette is the diagnosis** — nameable in solid black at thumbnail size.
**A 1.8 m human** appears in every image as a flat mid-grey hairline OUTLINE, never filled, no face, no
clothing — a measuring mark, never a character. **No text anywhere.** Invent no decoration. Landscape,
roughly 3:2.

**THE TWO CREATURES.** Each is a real measured behaviour. Give each a specific real-world anatomical
referent — an actual animal, not a shape. They must not share a body plan.

  **THE ONE THAT HOLDS** — amber. A long segmented animal whose whole length is exact: every segment
  tight, regular, identically spaced, end to end, however far it runs. It is the only thing here that
  keeps something correctly for as long as you like. Nothing about it is remarkable except that it does
  not drift, and in this world that is extraordinary.

  **THE ONE THAT CANNOT FIND** — amber everywhere, and unreachable. The centre of this world. It has
  kept **everything**, more than anything else, immaculately, and it cannot locate a single item of it.
  Overwhelmingly full and completely unsearchable: all present, all inaccessible. **It is not
  distressed and does not know anything is missing, because nothing IS missing.** It simply cannot
  reach in. This is the hardest image in the set.


╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ PROMPT A — THE OVERGROWTH.   The past grows over you. Time becomes VOLUME.                     ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
[PASTE THE SHARED PREAMBLE, THEN THIS]

**THIS DIRECTION.** Nothing is ever removed, so everything ever made is still here — and still
growing, over and through and around everything else. Not stacked. **TANGLED.**

  · There is no visible floor and no horizon. The world is a **dense occupied VOLUME**, matted with
    its own accumulated structure in every direction, including above.
  · A creature does not walk across this place, it moves **through** it — pushing into a thicket that
    closes behind it.
  · The oldest growth is at the centre of the mass, thickest and most interlocked, and it is not dead:
    it is simply buried by newer growth that had nowhere else to go.
  · Light arrives in **shafts and fragments**, filtered through the tangle. There is no open sky, only
    gaps.
  · Space is the scarce thing here. Every clear volume is a recent one.

**REFERENCE: a coral reef choking a wreck; a bramble thicket a century old; the interior of a termite
mound.** NOT a forest — a forest has a floor and a canopy and this has neither.

**Register: CLAUSTROPHOBIC AND ALIVE.** Not oppressive, not hostile. Crowded by abundance.

THREE IMAGES.
  1 · THE PLACE, no creatures. The human outline pressed into a small clear volume, tangle in every
      direction including overhead. This image alone must say *nothing here was ever cleared away.*
  2 · THE ONE THAT HOLDS, full body, threading a channel it has forced through the mass — the only
      straight line anywhere in frame.
  3 · THE ONE THAT CANNOT FIND, full body, so overgrown by what it is carrying that its own anatomy is
      barely extractable from it. Immaculate underneath and unreachable.


╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ PROMPT B — THE SUSPENSION.   The past is visible and untouchable. Time becomes CLEAR DEPTH.    ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
[PASTE THE SHARED PREAMBLE, THEN THIS]

**THIS DIRECTION.** Nothing is lost because nothing sinks and nothing rises. Everything that ever
happened hangs where it happened, in a perfectly clear medium, at the exact depth of the moment it
occurred.

  · The world is a **transparent column**, not a surface. You see enormously far down and enormously
    far up, and it is full — objects, forms and events suspended motionless at every depth.
  · **You can SEE the entire past. It is right there. You cannot touch any of it.** Distance is the
    only barrier, and there is no way to move through the medium toward a thing you can see.
  · The clarity is total: no murk, no silt, no fog. This is not deep water. It is **glass with things
    in it.**
  · Light comes from far above and passes all the way down, so depth reads as gradient rather than as
    darkness.
  · Creatures stand on, or are held at, their own level. There is no ground — there is the depth you
    are at.

**REFERENCE: insects in amber; a flooded chalk quarry on a still day; specimens suspended in clear
resin.** NOT an ocean, NOT underwater — there is no current, no drift, no swimming.

**Register: VERTIGINOUS AND SERENE.** The whole of history is legible and none of it is reachable.

THREE IMAGES.
  1 · THE PLACE, no creatures. The human outline suspended at one level, an immense clear volume above
      and below dense with motionless suspended things. *Everything is visible and nothing is at hand.*
  2 · THE ONE THAT HOLDS, full body, level and exact, its whole length at one perfect depth — the only
      thing in frame that is straight and deliberate.
  3 · THE ONE THAT CANNOT FIND, full body, with everything it has kept **visible inside its own
      transparent body** — packed, immaculate, perfectly preserved, and sealed. It can see its own
      contents. So can you. Neither of you can reach them.


╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ PROMPT C — THE STILL RUNNING.   The past never stopped. Time becomes CROWD.                    ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
[PASTE THE SHARED PREAMBLE, THEN THIS]

**THIS DIRECTION, AND IT IS THE STRANGEST.** If nothing is ever undone, then nothing ever **stopped.**
Every event that has occurred here is still occurring. The past is not a record — it is **still
running**, all of it, at once.

  · The place is **crowded with living duplicates**: the same creatures at every moment they have ever
    been, all present, all active, all real, overlapping and continuing.
  · They do not notice each other. Each is completing an action from a different moment, and none of
    them is a ghost, an echo or a projection — **every one is solid, lit and alive.**
  · The density is highest where the most has happened. Some regions are almost impassable with
    simultaneous versions; others are nearly empty because little ever occurred there.
  · The ground and the air can be simple, even bare. **The crowd is the world.**
  · Light: strong, clear and directional, so every duplicate casts a real shadow. Hundreds of shadows
    from one animal's hundreds of moments.

**REFERENCE: a gannet colony; a bat emergence; a stadium crowd photographed from above.** NOT motion
blur, NOT multiple exposure, NOT transparency or ghosting — **solid animals, all of them.**

**Register: OVERWHELMING AND INDIFFERENT.** Nothing here is haunted. It is simply too full of itself
to find anything, and none of it minds.

THREE IMAGES.
  1 · THE PLACE, no creatures other than the crowd. The human outline standing among dozens of solid
      simultaneous animals, none of which is aware of it. *Nothing stopped, so everything is here.*
  2 · THE ONE THAT HOLDS, full body, and it is the ONLY creature in the frame that appears exactly
      once. In a world of duplicates, being singular is what it means to keep something correctly.
  3 · THE ONE THAT CANNOT FIND, and it appears **hundreds of times**, filling the frame with its own
      simultaneous selves — every one intact, every one immaculate, every one carrying part of the
      answer, and none of them able to reach the others.

JUDGING THE NINE

  THE NOT-SEDIMENT TEST   Is it layers wearing a costume? If any frame reads as strata, terraces or
                          a cross-section, that direction failed at the first hurdle.
  THE PREMISE TEST        Does THE PLACE image say *nothing is lost and nothing can be found*, with
                          no caption?
  THE ANIMAL TEST         Are the creatures still LIVING ANIMALS with a front, or has the world
                          swallowed them into scenery?
  THE LIGHT LAW           Does amber still read as a diagnostic state rather than as atmosphere?
  THE BUILD TEST          A matted volume, a clear column and a crowd of duplicates are three very
                          different costs in a real-time renderer. This is a legitimate tie-breaker.
