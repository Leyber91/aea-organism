# WORLD 3 — THE REMEMBERER · REMEMBER

**Status: not started. No images exist yet.** This folder holds the instructions; the renders land here
as they are approved.

The faculty this world gives the universe: **it can CARRY.** What happens to something survives the
exchange. In the first two worlds every visit was the first visit — no correction stayed made, and a
creature repaired was a creature that would need repairing again. Here what you do stays done, which is
relief and also weight, because mistakes stay too.

## THE THREE DIRECTIONS

| | memory is | what changes |
|---|---|---|
| **A · THE ARCHIVE** | a **place** | the spent craters FILL. Each holds a seated lens with a glow that does not cool, amber lines joining socket to socket. The scar field becomes a network, and the plain gets brighter instead of dimmer |
| **B · THE MIGRATION** | a **movement** | the passengers LEAVE their hosts and travel. The first motion in this universe. Loaded ones glow; worn trails braid into paths between creatures that trade often. The ground is unchanged |
| **C · THE STRATA** | a **depth** | the world BUILDS UP. Banded ground, and where it breaks you see a section: World 2's cold craters sealed at mid-depth, World 1's terrace steps fossilised deeper still |

**A and C both change the ground and will fight. B changes only the creatures and composes with either.**
World 2 shipped as two combined directions and that only worked because they were orthogonal by
construction. Judge pairs, not singles.

**The anchor inverts rather than repeats.** `Labens longitudinis` degrades along its own length in World
2, and the player watched a repair fail to travel a few metres. In World 2's final image there is
exactly one intact specimen, far off, out of reach. **This world is where that one lives**, so the
anchor is drawn WHOLE — same animal, same segments, finished instead of failing.

**READ THIS BEFORE COMMITTING NINE TURNS OF ART.** World 2's components were also mostly unmeasured
when its art was made, and the measurement afterwards found **five of seven components and not one that
improves an answer.** `THE CORRECTOR` may have to become `THE WITNESS`. World 3 stands on thinner ground
than that.

The experiments owed, in order: **`x21` CONVERSATION** (the largest hole in the project),
**`x22` CHECKPOINT at power** (one task, one chain length is not a result), **`x23` INHERITANCE**
(upward costs nothing 5.12 to 5.25, downward loses a box 5.5 to 4.5 — the only creature-to-creature
interaction anywhere in this project, and exactly what direction B would depict).

`organism.py` needs a **CARRY stage** before any of them can run. Its pipeline is SHAPE, FIRE, READ,
REPAIR, JUDGE, and nothing in it persists between runs — the unit of measurement stops being a trial
and becomes a sequence.
