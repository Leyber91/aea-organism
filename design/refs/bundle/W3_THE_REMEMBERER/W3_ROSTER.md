## WHAT IS STILL MISSING, AND WHY

**No door creature.** World 4 is THE KEEPER — it runs while nobody is watching — so World 3's wall
should be a creature that carries perfectly while the player is present and is empty when they
return. Nothing has measured that. It must not be invented; `Auratus gravis` is what invention
produces.

**~~No downward-inheritance creature.~~ CLOSED by x23b.** Downward costs **+0.092**, inside the band.
Prediction 4 claimed it would lose a whole unit; it is refuted with data rather than left untestable.
**Inheritance is free in every direction measured.**

**But the Law IV question is still open, and the run could not ask it.** No `same_plant` arm ever
fired: the two groq rods both score 1.00 solo, so neither is up or down relative to the other and the
gate assigned them no arm. Every arm was cross-plant or self. **Whether crossing vendors costs more
than staying inside one is unmeasured**, and it needs a same-plant pair with a real baseline gap.

**And one anomaly held back deliberately.** `nemotron-3-ultra-550b` scores 0.78 solo and reads *above*
it on four of five arms that hand into it — +0.222 from granite, +0.222 from cerebras. A receiver
performing better on inherited state than on the truth is either a real effect or n=3 noise, and at
18 steps per arm +0.222 is four steps. **Not claimed.**

**`CONVERSATION` is unpriced.** x21 showed its real behaviour is *"it stops finishing"* — 8 of 12
sequences completed against 92% for every other container — and that was never given a receipt at
proper n.
