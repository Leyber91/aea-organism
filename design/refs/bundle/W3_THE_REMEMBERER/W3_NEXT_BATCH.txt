╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ 1 · S2 — THE CROSSING.  THE MOST IMPORTANT IMAGE IN THE REGION AND IT DOES NOT EXIST YET.      ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Same world, same style, same light law as the previous images.

This region exists to teach ONE action: take a thing from one place, carry it somewhere else, and
have it still be there. Every image so far shows the network or shows a creature. **None shows the
act.** This one is the region's thesis and everything else is support.

  · **TWO CLEARLY DIFFERENT PLACES IN ONE FRAME.** Not two similar nodes. One dense, dark and closed
    with almost no amber; one open and brightly lit from within. A viewer must be able to point at
    both and say they are not the same kind of place.
  · **A REAL GAP BETWEEN THEM** — a single trunk spanning it and nothing else, so the route is the
    only way across and the drop below it is visible.
  · **ONE LOADED CARRIER** (the isopod species from the body sheet) at the middle of the span,
    amber glowing along its flanks because it is loaded, moving from the dark place toward the bright
    one. It is small. The distance is the subject, not the animal.
  · **THE PROBE** — a single point of warm amber light with a fading amber ribbon, flying alongside
    the span, small in frame.
  · Human outline standing at the near end of the trunk, watching it go.

The question the image must answer without a caption: **where did it come from, where is it going,
and what happens if it does not arrive.**

║ 2 · S3 — THE STALL.  The failure state, as a place rather than as a portrait.                  ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Same world, same style, same light law as the previous images.

**Sistens oneris** has stopped on a main trunk under its own load, and the region is backing up behind
it. Its portrait already exists; this is the same animal seen as a **traffic condition**.

  · The stalled animal in the MIDDLE DISTANCE, not the foreground, seen from behind and slightly
    above so the blocked route reads before the creature does.
  · **DOZENS OF CARRIERS BANKED UP BEHIND IT** along the trunk, stopped, still loaded, still amber.
    Some have started onto a thinner side branch that is clearly a worse route.
  · The amber in the trunk ahead of the blockage is **thin and starved**; behind it, dense and packed.
    That difference across the blockage is the whole image.
  · Its load is **amber, not blue.** It is not doing the wrong thing. It is doing the right thing and
    never arriving, and the picture must not accuse it.
  · The probe threading past the jam. Human outline on the side branch for scale.

Register: **CONGESTED, NOT HOSTILE.** Nothing here is malicious. The road is simply occupied.


╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ 3 · BODY SHEET — TENENS LONGITUDINIS.  The format that worked, applied to the one you fly on.  ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Same world, same style, same light law as the previous images.

Do this **exactly like the Vector operis body sheet** — flat mid-grey studio ground, multiple views of
one animal, hairline human outline at the left for scale, no landscape, no atmosphere. That sheet is
the best reference document in the set and this animal needs one more than any other, because the
player **flies along its body** and it has only ever been drawn as scenery.

**THE ANIMAL.** The long one at third stage: an enormous segmented body, every segment exact and
identically spaced end to end, armoured and plated, amber running unbroken from one end to the other.
At the previous stage it came apart along its length. It does not any more, and the sheet must make
that legible: **the regularity is the diagnosis.**

SIX PANELS on one sheet:
  1 · full length in profile, small, so the whole animal fits and the segment rhythm reads
  2 · the head end, three-quarter, close
  3 · a single mid-body segment, large, showing how one joins the next
  4 · underside of three segments, showing the grip
  5 · the same three segments **with passengers ranked along them in order** — the third-stage tell
  6 · a cross-section-free view of it anchored to a trunk, showing how it becomes a route

No text, no labels, no callouts. The panels alone.

║ 4 · *** CANCELLED. THE NUMBER CAME BACK AND IT KILLED THE CREATURE. ***                        ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

**NEVER FIRE THIS.** `Inaccessus sui` is retracted.

x24 asked whether an organism holding every prior exchange can reach into it. It reached ALL of it:
40 of 40, at every depth, including the first step of a sixteen-step chain. Holding it and being able
to reach it turned out to be the same thing. The creature that keeps everything and can find none of
it is the exact opposite of what was measured.

The render stays on disk as evidence of the rule working. It is not annexed and it is not a species.
Full reasoning in W3_FULL_ROSTER.md.

**ITS REPLACEMENT IS DESIGNED AND ALSO ON HOLD** — `Immemor itineris`, the one unmindful of the
journey. It arrives correctly every time and remembers nothing of how: token recall 0 of 40 while
completing the chain at 1.000 per step. Thin, fast, carrying one bright bead, and it leaves the trunk
DARK behind it where every other creature here leaves amber. Same discipline as before: it gets drawn
when the full grid confirms 0/N across five rods, not on one rod at n=8.

The hold cost one image. It saved a species with a beautiful render and nothing behind it, which is
exactly how `Auratus gravis` happened. Keep doing this.

║ 5 · S4 — THE APPROACH.  Arrival. Every region needs one and this one has none.                 ║

Same world, same style, same light law as the previous images.

The player's **first sight** of the region, from outside and above, still at distance. The locked

  · The whole network seen as **one object** against the sky — its extent visible, its far edge

  · **The region has a shape.** Denser and brighter toward one region of the mass, thinning and

Register: **ARRIVAL.** The previous two regions were places you were already in. This is the first one
you can see the outside of, and that is what makes the crossing later mean something.
