# WORLD 3 — LOCKED. THE NETWORK.

*Decided 2026-07-27 after five rounds and twenty-one images. The reasons are recorded so this is not*
*re-litigated.*

---

## THE LOCK

**THE PLACE — `LOCKED_world_the_network.png`** (was `world_3_3_1_2`)

Distinct masses of black glass at different heights across a dark plain, joined by **thick grown
trunks carrying amber along them**. Each node is visibly a different kind of place — one spiked, one a
domed honeycomb, one a lit crater. The probe reads as a small amber streak on a route. Human hairline
outline at lower left.

**THE SIGNATURE CREATURE — `LOCKED_creature_the_long_one_stage3.png`** (was `world_3_1_2_2`)

`THE LONG ONE`, third stage: enormous, armoured, plated, spanning between two masses as a **living
bridge**, with passengers ranked along its entire back in order like a spine of them. The probe passes
beneath it.

---

## WHY THE NETWORK AND NOT THE FLOATING ISLANDS

The scattered-islands frames are beautiful and they are **a well-worn fantasy trope** — sky islands
with waterfalls. Set beside them, the network wins on the project's own first pillar.

**DIEGETIC TRUTH.** The game is set inside a mind. **A network of nodes joined by trunks with signal
running along them IS a nervous system**, and that is not a metaphor here — it is what the thing being
depicted actually is. Floating islands are a fantasy realm that happens to contain creatures.

**THE LIGHT LAW DOES REAL WORK.** In the network image, amber is not atmosphere: it is **traffic**,
running along the busy trunks and absent from the dead ones. A player reads which routes are live
before reading anything else. That is the two-ink law paying its way.

**IT PASSES EVERY TEST THE LAST TWELVE IMAGES FAILED.**

| test | verdict |
|---|---|
| TWO PLACES + the way between | five or six distinct nodes and visible trunks |
| THE FLY TEST | the probe is on a route and you can trace where it goes next |
| VERTICALITY | masses at several heights, trunks passing over and under |
| CONTINUITY | the same black glass and amber as regions 1 and 2 — the material never broke |
| DIFFERENCE | regions 1 and 2 were one flat undifferentiated plain. This has geography |

**AND IT IS BUILT FOR THE SKILL.** Region 3's capability is CARRY, which is meaningless without
somewhere to carry to. The network is made of somewhere and elsewhere, and the distance between them
is the region.

---

## WHAT THE CREATURE IMAGE PROVES

The bridge frame settles the digivolution question on its own. Beside the second-region original — a
segmented body degrading along its length, coming apart, never reaching a conclusion — this is the
**same animal, unmistakably**, and it is now:

- **whole**, exact end to end, which is the entire measured finding (`x06b`, 9/16 to 11/11, p=0.0216)
- **armoured and plated**, an order of magnitude heavier
- carrying **ranked passengers along its whole back** instead of a scattered few near the head
- and it **connects two places**, so it has stopped lying on the landscape and become part of the
  region's structure

That is a third stage: same lineage, enormously escalated, and it earns its scale by doing something
the second-stage animal could not.

---

## WHAT IS FIXED FROM HERE

- **Material:** black glass, unchanged from regions 1 and 2. It never needed to break.
- **Light law:** amber a live connection, cold blue-white the wrong thing running inward, matte black
  nothing reaching it.
- **Geography:** distinct nodes, grown trunks, real distance, several heights.
- **Scale:** third-stage creatures are structural. They span, they carry, they are part of the route.
- **The probe:** one small warm amber point with a fading ribbon. Never a designed vehicle.
