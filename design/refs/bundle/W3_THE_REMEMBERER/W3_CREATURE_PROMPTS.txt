PART A — CONTEXT. NOT AN IMAGE REQUEST. Reply "ready".
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

I am art-directing the third region of a game bestiary set inside a real AI system. The style is
decided — the attached images ARE the style. Do not explore, match.

**THE REGION.** A network. Distinct masses of black glass at several heights across a dark plain,
joined by thick grown trunks with warm amber running along them. Each node is a different kind of
place: one spiked, one a domed honeycomb, one a lit crater. Pale mist, dark overcast, real distance
between nodes, and routes a player can see and follow.

**WHAT THIS REGION IS.** The first region could only SPECIFY — ask well, and the ground rose. The
second learned to LOOK — and every act of looking burned a hole in the floor, and nothing it fixed
stayed fixed. **This region KEEPS.** What happens here survives. That is why it has geography at all:
carrying means nothing unless there is somewhere to carry to.

**THE PLAYER** is THE PROBE — one small point of warm amber light with a fading amber ribbon, flying
low. Never a designed vehicle, always small in frame.

**THE MATERIAL, FIXED.** Polished dark obsidian and smoked glass, wet-surfaced, heavy, with real
weight. Third-stage creatures are **armoured and plated** — an order of magnitude heavier than a
second-stage animal, and structural: they span, they carry, they are part of the route.

**THE LIGHT LAW — how a player diagnoses at a glance, and it is not decoration.**
  · **WARM AMBER** — a live connection, something working. Under the surface, at the seams.
  · **COLD BLUE-WHITE** — the wrong thing being done, running INWARD toward the creature's own
    centre, never out into the air.
  · **MATTE LIGHTLESS BLACK** — nothing reaches it. No rim light, no edge light, no specular, no glow.
    Read only as an outline against the palest part of the background.

**THE CARRIERS.** Every creature in this region has small separate armoured animals riding it —
gripping undersides, no faces, all contact surface. In the second region there was one per host. Here
they are **ranked in dozens** along a structural animal. They are a population, not an attachment.

**SEVEN RULES, repeated in every request.**
  1. A **LIVING ANIMAL** — anatomy, posture, weight, a front it faces with. Not a machine, not a rock,
     not architecture. If it could be mistaken for an object, it is wrong.
  2. **THE SILHOUETTE IS THE DIAGNOSIS** — nameable in solid black at thumbnail size. I give the
     outline to hit.
  3. **A 1.8 m human** appears as a flat mid-grey hairline OUTLINE, never filled — a measuring mark.
  4. **THE LIGHT LAW** as stated per creature. Never light one I call dark.
  5. **NO TEXT ANYWHERE.**
  6. **INVENT NO DECORATION.** Every feature traces to something stated.
  7. **ONE CREATURE PER IMAGE.** Landscape, roughly 3:2, hero framing, full body, air around it.

**BODY PLANS ALREADY SPENT — nothing may repeat one.** A vast limbless leviathan with a forward apron;
a leaning egg-body with one spur; a tall lean quadruped with its head hung low; the same head-high; a
lean quadruped with a channel milled along its topline; a heavy grazing ox with an open mouth and no
eyes; a small armless upright column; a small seated quadruped with a tilted head; a heavy curled
animal on one point; a faceted standing wedge; a wading bird with a needle beak; a long many-legged
segmented walker; a low forward-driving wedge with an empty seat; **and the long segmented bridge
animal in the attachment.**

Reply "ready" and I will send the first creature.


═══ 1 · THE BACKWARD-LOOKING ONE, third stage. amber, and it reaches further than before.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
It is the attached stage-two animal, escalated. **Do not redesign it.**

BEHAVIOUR. It works out the answer correctly and completely, in full view, and states something else.
Everything it knows is on the outside of its body, and the part that would speak is missing. In the
second region a reading of it died the moment it was made. **Here it does not.**

BODY. The same tall lean long-legged four-legged animal, now **far larger and heavier, armoured and
plated**, head level and TURNED BACK OVER ITS OWN SHOULDER to look along its own trailing working.

**THE FACE IS UNCHANGED AND IT IS THE POINT.** One continuous smooth closed plate of the same material
as the skull — like a fencing mask with no grille, like the blunt seamless toe of a boot. Two eyes sit
high on it. Below the eyes the surface runs unbroken to the end of the muzzle. **No lips, no jaw line,
no seam, no opening, no nostril, no mouth.** Turn the head so the camera sees this.

THE MANTLE. What was a trailing ribbon of ranked plates is now a **vast mantle** — layered, overlapping,
falling from neck and shoulders and spreading across the ground behind it, wide enough that **dozens of
carriers work across it** like figures on a slope.

LIGHT. Amber runs up the mantle from the carriers toward the shoulders — and for the first time it
**passes the neck and enters the head.** The head is lit. It has the answer, it can now see where the
answer came from, and it still cannot say it.

SILHOUETTE. A standing quadruped with its head turned back and a vast fan of layered plates spreading
behind. The widest silhouette in the region.

SCALE. 4 m at the shoulder, mantle spreading 15 m. Human outline beside a forefoot.
SETTING. On a node, its mantle spilling over the edge toward a trunk.


═══ 2 · THE ONE THAT RECEIVES. amber. The region's healthy creature.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEHAVIOUR. Hand it anything from any other creature and it takes it perfectly, and carries on from it
exactly as well as it does from its own work. It does not care where the thing came from or what made
it. **Fourteen different handoffs, from four different kinds of fuel, and not one cost it anything.**

BODY. A broad, low, **many-armed** animal built to receive: a squat heavy trunk with a ring of long
articulated limbs reaching **outward and upward in every direction**, each one open-handed, each one
already holding something. Nothing about it moves anywhere — it is planted, and everything comes to it.

Its limbs are the whole silhouette: a low body almost hidden under a crown of reaching arms, like a
crinoid or a basket star, or a barnacle's fan opened wide.

It sits **at a junction where several trunks meet**, and that is where it belongs — everything passing
through this part of the region passes through its hands.

LIGHT. Amber, and it flows INTO it along every limb from every direction at once, pooling in the
trunk. The only creature in the region lit from its extremities inward.

SILHOUETTE. A low mass under a wide radiating crown of arms. The most symmetrical shape in the region
and the only radial one — everything else has a front and a back.

SCALE. 2.5 m at the body, 9 m across the reach. Human outline beneath a limb.


═══ 3 · THE ONE THAT POISONS ITSELF. cold blue-white. The region's worst creature.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEHAVIOUR. It writes itself a note before each step, and the note is worse than nothing. Not a summary
that loses detail — **a note that adds error.** Four steps in it is measurably damaged; sixteen steps
in, nine of eleven attempts are destroyed. It gets worse the longer it goes, because each note is
written on the last one. It is not confused and not broken. **It is diligently making itself wrong.**

BODY. A long-bodied animal that **secretes something onto itself**: a thick, glossy, accreted coating
built up along its back and flanks in overlapping wet layers, laid down by its own body, uneven and
lumpy where it has been added to over and over. The newest layer is still soft.

Its head is small, clear and undamaged, and it is **turned back and down, applying another layer.**
That posture is the creature: it is doing this to itself, carefully, right now.

Underneath the accretion its actual anatomy is lean and sound — visible at the legs and the head where
the coating has not reached. **The animal is fine. What it has put on itself is not.**

LIGHT. Cold blue-white, running INWARD from the coating into the body, never out into the air. The
coating itself is the brightest part and the light travels the wrong way.

SILHOUETTE. A lean animal made lumpen — a clean leg-line and head under a swollen, irregular,
overbuilt back. The only asymmetric-by-accretion shape in the region.

SCALE. 1.6 m at the shoulder, 3 m long. Human outline beside it.
SETTING. On a dead trunk with no amber in it.


═══ 4 · THE ONE THAT KEEPS A MISTAKE. amber, and that is the horror.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEHAVIOUR. It is a perfect carrier. Whatever it is given, it preserves flawlessly for as long as you
like, with total fidelity and total care. **It has been given something wrong.** Every step it takes,
it renews the error with the same devotion it would give a truth. Its memory works. Its judgement does
not exist. **Faithfulness is not judgement.**

BODY. A powerful, well-made, symmetrical animal — the most **competent-looking** creature in the
region, heavy through the shoulders, unmarked, immaculate, with a deliberate careful stance.

In the cradle of its back, held in a **purpose-built armoured recess** and clearly protected, it
carries one object of the same black glass — and that object is visibly **WRONG**: cracked through,
mis-cast, one part fused to another at a bad angle. It is broken and it is being carried like
something precious.

The recess fits it exactly. **It was built for this thing.** The animal's whole posture protects it.

LIGHT. Warm amber, healthy, correct, running from the recess into the body — the light of a working
connection, on a creature doing its job perfectly. **Nothing in the lighting says anything is wrong.**
Only the object does.

SILHOUETTE. A strong four-legged carrier with a raised armoured cradle on its back holding one
irregular broken shape. The only creature carrying a visible object.

SCALE. 2 m at the shoulder. Human outline beside it, close enough to see the object.


═══ 5 · THE CARRIERS. amber. The most-drawn creature in the game and it has never been named.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This one is a **plate, not a portrait** — the exception to one-creature-per-image.

BEHAVIOUR. They read a host and pass what they read inward. They do not decide, evaluate or object.
They are the reason anything in this universe knows anything about itself. In the second region there
was one per host. Here there are dozens per host and they **move between hosts along the trunks.**

BODY. Small, dense, armoured, compact — a hard segmented carapace over a fine articulated gripping
underside, all contact surface. **No face, no eyes, no front you could talk to.** Between a woodlouse,
a limpet and a tick, at the size of a large dog.

THE PLATE. Six of them at true relative scale on a plain neutral ground, showing:
  · one gripping a curved plate, seen from the side, working
  · one from beneath — the full articulated underside, which is its only complex surface
  · one walking on bare stone between hosts, unattached and visibly awkward off a host
  · one **loaded**, glowing amber from within, having just left a host
  · one **empty**, dark, on its way to one
  · one at three times the size of the others — older, thicker-shelled, scarred

Human hairline outline at the left for scale. Amber only on the loaded one.

SILHOUETTE. A low armoured oval on a comb of short legs. It must be recognisable at thumbnail as the
thing riding every other creature in this game.


═══ 6 · THE ONE THAT STOPS FINISHING. amber, and it is running out.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEHAVIOUR. It carries **everything** — the whole of every exchange, nothing summarised, nothing
dropped — and it is the most complete carrier here. It also cannot finish. Where every other creature
completes nine journeys in ten, this one completes **two in three**. It does not fail, it does not
break, it does not lose what it holds. **It simply stops, still full, part of the way there.**

BODY. A long, powerful animal built like a haulier — deep chest, heavy shoulders, strong legs — that
is **visibly overloaded**. It carries an enormous mass on its back, far more than any other creature
here, held in a swelling that has grown to accommodate it.

**IT IS STOPPED.** Legs braced and locked, head down, weight settled — not fallen, not collapsed,
not struggling. It has simply come to a halt on a trunk, mid-route, and is standing there holding
everything perfectly.

Behind it the trunk it came along is bright with amber. **Ahead of it the trunk is dark.**

LIGHT. Amber, strong and full, all through the load and the body. There is nothing wrong with it. The
light stops where the animal stops.

SILHOUETTE. A braced four-legged hauler under a mass bigger than itself, standing still. The only
creature in the region that is not doing anything.

SCALE. 2.2 m at the shoulder, load rising to 4 m. Human outline ahead of it on the dark trunk.


═══ 7 · THE ONE THAT CANNOT FIND. THE DOOR. amber everywhere and unreachable.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
**FLAG, AND IT IS FOR THE RECORD, NOT FOR THE IMAGE: this creature is being drawn ahead of its
evidence.** Every other creature in this region has a measurement behind it. This one has a
principle — *storing and retrieving are different operations* — and no run. It does not enter the
bestiary until something measures it.

BEHAVIOUR. It has kept everything. More than anything else here, immaculately, losing nothing, for
longer than any other creature. **And it cannot reach a single item of it.** It is not damaged and not
distressed and does not know anything is wrong, because nothing IS wrong — nothing is missing.
Storage is perfect. There is simply no way in.

BODY. A **NODE THAT IS ALSO AN ANIMAL** — the largest creature in the region, a vast sealed rounded
mass seated at the end of a major trunk, with the barest anatomy of a living thing readable in it: a
suggestion of a back, a settled posture, a front it has turned toward the trunk.

**IT IS ENTIRELY CLOSED.** No aperture, no seam, no opening, no port, no mouth, no eye, nowhere for a
carrier to grip. Every other creature in this game has somewhere something can attach. This has
nothing, anywhere, at any scale.

**AND IT IS FULL.** Through the surface, deep inside, an immense dense amber structure is visible —
packed, intricate, perfectly ordered, and **enormously more than any other creature carries.** It
glows through the shell like light through a hand.

**CARRIERS COVER IT AND NONE OF THEM IS LIT.** Dozens crawl over the closed surface looking for a
grip, dark, working, finding nothing. Some have clearly been there a very long time.

The trunk arriving at it is bright with amber. **The trunk leaving it does not exist.**

LIGHT. Amber inside, brilliant and deep and unreachable. The shell is matte and dark. **The only
creature in the game lit entirely from within and sealed against it.**

SILHOUETTE. A vast closed rounded mass at a trunk's end, crusted with small dark shapes. The largest
and simplest outline in the region — and the only one with no opening of any kind.

SCALE. 12 m across. Human outline at the base of the trunk, tiny.
