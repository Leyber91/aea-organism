## THE FOUR THAT ARE MISSING AND WHY EACH MATTERS

**1 · CHECKPOINT ALONE, ON A ROD THAT DRIFTS.** This is *the world's central creature* and it has
never been cleanly measured. x21's control contained the treatment; x22 only ever ran it beneath a
critic that was rewriting every step. **The one thing World 3 is named for is the one thing unmeasured.**

**2 · CONVERSATION AS A CREATURE.** Not "does it help" — it does not. Its behaviour is that **it stops
finishing**, a container that carries everything and collapses under the weight. That is a creature and
a good one, and it has no receipt.

**3 · THE DOOR.** World 4 is THE KEEPER: *it runs while nobody is watching*. So World 3's wall should
be a creature that carries perfectly while the player is present and is **empty when they return** —
not damaged, not drifting, reset. Nothing measures presence-versus-absence. **It must not be invented;
`Auratus gravis` is what invention produces.**

**4 · RECALL.** C-81, the file-system channel, n=3, FORGE-PENDING in `modules.json` and never in the
catalogue. Nobody has opened `recall.py` to see whether it is World 3's `RECALL` or a fifth name
collision.

## WHAT WOULD FILL THEM

One run fills the first two: `checkpoint` and `conversation` seated on **v4** — the peak assembly,
with **no critic** — on rods the gate says have headroom, scored on completion *and* accuracy
separately. That is x24, and it is the run x21 was supposed to be before its control was broken.

The door needs a different instrument entirely: the same organism run twice with a gap, which nothing
in the lab currently does. `Chain` runs a sequence; there is no notion of *between sessions*.

**Until the first cell is filled, World 3's headline is unearned.** It is named THE REMEMBERER and the
component it is named for has never been measured alone on a task it could help.
