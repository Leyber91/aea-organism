# 02 · WORLD BRIEF — what THE PROBE is (attach to every generation)

THE PROBE is a game played INSIDE a real, running autonomous AI entity. The player flies a small
probe through the entity's "body" and composes real AI parts into small machines that actually
execute. **Every light, number, and pulse in this world is live system truth** — nothing is
decorative. The game's thesis: *wire living proofs of a mind, each more complete than the last,
until you hold the whole one — and it keeps running after you close the tab.*

## THE WORLD — a radial living instrument (not a city, not a spaceship interior)

Seen from the probe, the entity is a vast dark **orrery / radial instrument** resting in a
near-black void with a faint cold horizon band far away:

- **THE MOUTH (the core):** at the exact centre — a small faceted icosahedron jewel wrapped in a
  translucent fresnel shell, orbited by two thin counter-rotating tori (its orrery). It burns
  **amber only because the entity's heartbeat is genuinely alive**. A thin vertical amber beam
  rises from it. This is the hub every draw routes through.
- **THREE CONCENTRIC RINGS:** thin structure-grey circles on the dark floor around the core — the
  privacy zones (innermost = most restrictive: sensitive → private → public). They are boundaries,
  drawn like a precision instrument's engraved rings.
- **THE ORGANS:** small dark octahedral nodes resting ON their ring. A **wired** organ burns amber
  from within (GOVERNOR, MEMORY, LOOP — and the MOUTH itself). An **unbuilt** organ is a faint
  cold-blue wireframe ghost — **FOG** — visibly present but unearned (SENSES, HANDS).
- **SPOKES:** thin, quiet radial hairlines from each organ straight to the core. Everything routes
  through the hub.
- **PULSES:** small bright amber points of light travelling along the spokes toward the core — each
  pulse is ONE real system event (a model draw, a governance verdict, a heartbeat tick). A failed
  event travels as a dim grey point. Pulses fade as they arrive (afterglow = proof).
- **THE PROBE:** a tiny angular dark craft with one warm amber aperture — always small in frame,
  dwarfed by the instrument.

## THE BENCH — the compose interface (a milled instrument, not a menu)

A dark matte plate, machined hairline edges, IBM Plex Mono type. On it:

- **THE RAIL (the spine):** one horizontal lit channel across the plate. PARTS mount ONTO it like
  components seated on a workbench rail.
- **THE PARTS (chips):** THE DRAW (head — the power inlet that performs one real model call),
  THE FRAME (shapes the prompt), THE METER (refuses before any overspend), THE LADDER (the ranked
  rods a draw falls down), THE MEASURE (tail — closes every run into a RECORD). Each chip is a
  small instrument badge with a glyph, a designation, and one live stat.
- **FIRING:** press RUN → a REAL model call executes. An amber packet travels the rail head→tail;
  the trace prints receipts line by line (`DRAW t-01 · zone private`, `HOP granite → FELL THROUGH`,
  `MEM 3 RECALLED`, `PASS · 1.44s · FREE`). Cost shows as `1u` (real budget) or `FREE` (the local
  hearth). A refusal prints verbatim (`REFUSED: no draw at the head`) and the amber withdraws.
- **STATES:** COMPOSE (quiet, structure-grey) · RUN (amber packet live) · HALT (amber withdrawn,
  the reason printed) · records kept as `LAST 1.44s · BEST 0.98s`.

## The stakes (why images should feel tense, not decorative)

Energy is a real metered budget: the free local hearth always answers (slow, warm, safe); reaching
up the ladder to a cloud rod costs real units and can STARVE. Composing more complete beings is
the whole game — each new organ lit is EARNED, and the fog is the frontier.
