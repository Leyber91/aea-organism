## WHAT THIS IS

A game called **THE PROBE**. The player flies a tiny maintenance probe inside a **real, running
artificial mind** and assembles working machines out of its real parts. Nothing in it is simulated:
every light on screen corresponds to something that actually happened in real infrastructure.

**The world is a thinking POWER INSTALLATION — not space, not a body, not a city.** The vocabulary is
industrial and electrical: power plants, fuel rods, a hearth, a grid, brownouts, capacity, a meter,
starvation. The centre is a **reactor**, not a star. The rings are **containment perimeters**, not
orbits. If the image reads as sci-fi space opera, it is wrong.

## THE VISUAL LAW — non-negotiable, and it is the whole identity

1. **TWO INKS ONLY.**
   - **VOID:** near-black, neutral, `#08080a`. Occupies most of every frame.
   - **STRUCTURE:** a warm-neutral grey, `#aaa69d`. All geometry, all lines, all text.
2. **AMBER IS THE THIRD INK AND IT IS EARNED.** `#ffb000` (hot) and `#d4a24c` (warm). Amber appears
   ONLY where something genuinely fired. It must read as **the brightest thing in the frame and cover
   less than 5% of it.** Amber used as decoration, tint, or general glow is the single worst failure
   this project can make.
3. **NO BLUE.** Not cyan, not teal, not a blue-grey cast. The greys are warm-neutral.
   The ONE exception: unbuilt regions may use a cold desaturated blue-grey `#4a6076` as **fog**, drawn
   as thin dashed wireframe only.
4. **ONE HARD LIGHT** from the upper left (azimuth ~135°, elevation ~55°). Top and left edges catch a
   specular hairline; bottom and right fall to black; every raised object casts a real contact shadow
   down-right. Any mark that contradicts that light does not belong.
5. **MATTE CAST METAL.** Fine anodised grain on every surface. A smooth gradient reads as plastic and
   is a failure. Engraved text: pressed INTO the metal, a hair darker than its ground, with a light
   lower edge.
6. **TYPE:** IBM Plex Mono or an identical technical monospace. ALL CAPS. Wide letter-spacing.
   Tabular figures. **NO EMOJI ANYWHERE. NO SIGNATURES. NO WATERMARKS.**
7. **MOOD:** an austere instrument photographed in a dark room. Duskers, Territory Studio interface
   design, Return of the Obra Dinn's material discipline. Not a dashboard. Not a UI kit.

## THE HONESTY RULE THAT SHAPES THE COMPOSITION

The game may only show light where something real happened. So in every image, **the majority of the
structure is DARK** and a small minority is lit. A frame where everything glows is not merely
off-brand — it is a lie about the system. Darkness is the default state and it is the point.
