# SCENES 14–17 · THE SCENARIOS ENCOUNTERED

*Paste `00_CANON_BLOCK.md` first, then ONE scene below. One image per message. Never two.*

**These are not invented situations.** Each one happened during the walk of 2026-07-25 and the numbers
in them are measured. That is why they are worth drawing.

---

## SCENE 14 · THE FRAME THAT SUBTRACTED

**What happened:** a scaffold was fitted to a cheap rod to make it better. Generic guidance made it
**worse** — a task it passed 3 times out of 3 unaided, it failed 3 times out of 3 once framed.

**The image:** a close, raking view along a milled rail. Two octagonal chips are seated on it.
- The LEFT chip is engraved `BARE` and carries a healthy warm sliver of light at its seat.
- The RIGHT chip is engraved `FRAMED`. It is **physically larger** — visibly heavier, more machined,
  more apparatus — and its seat is **completely dark**. A hairline crack runs across its face.
- Between them, engraved small into the plate: `3/3` under the left, `0/3` under the right.
- The rail continues past both into blackness.

**The point to make visible:** the bigger, more elaborate part is the dead one. Amber appears ONLY at
the left chip's seat — one small mark in the whole frame.

## SCENE 15 · THE THING IT COULD NOT KNOW

**What happened:** asked a question about its own infrastructure, the rod answered *"I don't have
access"* three times out of three. Given one retrieved memory, it answered correctly three times out
of three.

**The image:** a two-part composition, split by a vertical milled groove down the centre of the frame.
- LEFT half: an empty recessed octagonal socket in dark metal. Above it, engraved: `NO GROUND`. Below
  it, engraved: `0/3`. Nothing is lit. The socket is a hole.
- RIGHT half: the same socket, now filled by a seated chip engraved `RECALL`, with a warm sliver at
  its base, and a **single thin amber filament** running from the chip down into the groove — the
  connection carrying something. Below: `3/3`.
- Nowhere in the frame is there a face-glow. The light lives at the joint only.

**The point:** the difference between an empty socket and a filled one is the difference between
"I cannot" and "here it is."

## SCENE 16 · IT CANNOT CRITICISE ITSELF IN ONE BREATH

**What happened:** a rod told to find the error in its own answer, in the same call, found nothing —
nine attempts out of nine. Asked as a **separate** call, it found something nine times out of nine.

**The image:** two chips on the same rail, and the composition is about the GAP between them.
- LEFT: a single chip engraved `ONE BREATH`. It sits alone. Around it the rail is unbroken, continuous,
  seamless metal. Its seat is dark. Engraved beneath: `0/9`.
- RIGHT: **two** chips engraved `ANSWER` and `JUDGE`, and between them the rail is **physically cut** —
  a clean machined gap, a real interruption in the metal with both cut faces catching the light. A
  single amber filament arcs across that gap. Engraved beneath: `9/9`.
- The gap is the subject of the image. Make it unmistakable that the break is what made the light.

**The point:** self-examination requires a boundary. The cut in the rail is not damage; it is the
mechanism.

## SCENE 17 · THE COMPLETE MACHINE THAT WASTED ITSELF

**What happened:** a construct with every organ seated closed every canonical item it could reach — and
wasted 94% of its wall-clock, because most of its organs had no reason to be there. A construct with
four organs matched its result in a third of the time.

**The image:** a wide, flat-on view of one long milled rail crowded with **eight** seated octagonal
chips, shoulder to shoulder, filling the frame edge to edge. Eight, exactly eight.
- Only **three** of the eight have a warm sliver at their seat.
- The other **five** are seated but **utterly dark**, and each of those five carries a small engraved
  `x` mark on its lower chamfer.
- Above the rail, engraved large and spaced wide: `19/19 CLOSED`.
- Below the rail, engraved in the same size: `94% WASTED`.
- The two numbers are the same weight and the same colour. Neither is amber. **Neither wins.**

**The point:** completeness and efficiency are in tension, and the image must refuse to say which
matters more. A fully populated rail that is mostly dark is the most honest picture of the trap.

## AFTER THE IMAGES — a note for whoever uses them

These are **art direction, not artefacts.** The real map in the game must be generated from live data,
because which regions are lit is a fact about what actually ran — a hand-drawn map showing six rings
lit would be a fabricated resource, and that is the one thing this project forbids. Use these images to
settle material, light, weight and composition. Then build the map in code and let the truth light it.
