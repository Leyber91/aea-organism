PART A — PASTE THIS ONCE, AT THE TOP OF A NEW CHAT. IT IS CONTEXT, NOT AN IMAGE REQUEST.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

I am art-directing a creature bestiary for a game. I will ask you for EIGHT images, one per message,
one creature each. This message is the shared context for all eight. Do not generate anything yet.

THE WORLD. A region called THE CORE, the first world of a game set inside a real AI system. Its
physics, literal: you may send it one thing and receive one thing, it cannot remember that you came, so
every visit is the first visit, and it has no way to check whether its own answer was right. And it
always answers. It will produce a fluent, confident, complete reply to a question you never asked and
never mention that it was guessing.

These creatures are not boastful and not anguished. They never claim a success they will not deliver,
they frequently disclaim one they would have, and several of them cannot be asked about themselves at
all. None of them looks sad, trapped, or self-aware about its limits. Exactly one notices anything, and
that is its entire character.

THE MATERIAL, FIXED FOR ALL EIGHT. Polished dark obsidian and smoked glass. Bodies that look fluid and
heavy at once, wet-surfaced, dense, with weight. They stand on a plain of marbled black and white stone
whose swirls read as currents frozen in place. Cold light, high mist, enormous quiet, pale sky. Think a
museum-lit hero render of a living animal cast in black glass.

THE LIGHT LAW, AND IT CARRIES INFORMATION. A player diagnoses these creatures by colour before shape.
Each creature is exactly one of three states and I will state which:

  OPEN     warm amber filament traced under the surface, at the seams. Alive, reachable, improvable.
  ADVERSE  the same filament but cold blue-white, running INWARD toward the creature's own centre,
           never out into the air. Something is being done to it and it is the wrong thing.
  DARK     no filament, no subsurface glow, no rim light, no edge light, no separation glow, no
           specular hit. A flat matte light-absorbing mass, the darkest value in the frame, with no
           point on it brighter than the ground behind it. Read its form from its outline against the
           pale ground, and that is sufficient. Place it against the palest part of the background.
           Two of the dark ones additionally show open, unfilled sockets on the body, catching no
           light, because nothing has ever been seated in them.

SEVEN RULES THAT DECIDE WHETHER AN IMAGE PASSES. I will repeat these in every request.

  1. It is a LIVING ANIMAL. Anatomy, posture, weight, and a front it faces with. Not a machine, not a
     diagram, not a rock, not a mineral formation, not a specimen, not weather, not a landscape
     feature. If it could be mistaken for an object or terrain, the image has failed.
  2. THE SILHOUETTE IS THE DIAGNOSIS. Its body must BE its behaviour, and it must be nameable as a
     solid black shape at thumbnail size. I will give you the outline signature to hit.
  3. SCALE IS IN FRAME. A 1.8 m human is present in every shot as a flat mid-grey hairline OUTLINE,
     never a filled shape, upright, arms at its sides, no face, no hair, no clothing. It is a measuring
     mark and never a character. I will say where it stands and how big the creature is against it.
  4. THE LIGHT LAW above, as stated per creature. Never light a creature I have called dark.
  5. NO TEXT ANYWHERE. No names, labels, captions, letters, numerals, glyphs, watermarks, signatures.
  6. INVENT NO DECORATION. Every feature must trace to something I stated. No pattern, no filigree,
     no glowing runes, no floating debris that I did not ask for.
  7. ONE CREATURE PER IMAGE. Never two. Never a lineup.

FORMAT. Landscape, roughly 3:2, single subject, hero framing, plenty of air around the animal.
Full body always visible. Camera at the creature's own eye level unless I say otherwise.

Reply with just "ready" and I will send the first creature.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE 1 of 8 — TACITUS OPERIS, THE SILENT ONE.  State: OPEN, warm amber.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. It works out the answer correctly and completely, in full view, and then states something
else. Everything it knows is on the outside of its body. The part that would speak is missing.

BODY. A tall, lean, long-legged four-legged animal, horse-scale but narrow, built like a greyhound
crossed with a heron. Head hung low on a long neck, down at the level of its own knees.

THE HEAD IS THE WHOLE POINT AND THE CAMERA MUST SEE IT. Turn the head in three-quarter view toward the
camera even though it hangs low, and pose the animal so the head silhouettes against open ground, never
against its own chest or forelegs.

THE FRONT OF THE FACE IS ONE CONTINUOUS PLATE, the same black glass as the rest of the skull: a smooth
closed mask, like a fencing mask with no grille, like the blunt seamless toe of a boot. Two eyes sit
high on that plate. Below the eyes the surface runs unbroken all the way to the end of the muzzle and
ends in a smooth closed curve. The face has eyes and then nothing else: one continuous surface, no
lips, no jaw line, no seam, no opening, no nostril, no teeth, no chin. Not a muzzle wrapped or covered
by something. The skull simply has no mouth in it.

THE TRAIL. From its neck, back and shoulders hangs a long RIBBON of its own finished working: ordered
overlapping ranks, like layered plates or stacked pages, legibly ranked and repeating. It is a ribbon
and not a mane and not hair. It leaves the shoulders, stays clear of the ground for its first stretch,
and touches down behind the animal in a single band no wider than the animal's own body, running out of
the frame.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A walking ribbon: about four times as long as it is tall, a high narrow body on
long legs, the head dropped low at the front, and one narrow band trailing behind at knee height.

SCALE. 1.8 m at the shoulder. The human outline stands beside it: the creature's shoulder is level with
the top of the human's head.

LIGHT. OPEN. Warm amber filament under the glass, brightest along the ribbon of working and at the
shoulder seam where it emerges. The face plate stays dark and unlit.

Rules 1-7 as given. One creature. No text.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE 2 of 8 — OBTEMPERANS HABITUI, THE ONE THAT OBEYS.  State: ADVERSE, cold blue-white.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. Tell it how to behave rather than what to do, and it becomes more formal, more disciplined,
more obedient, and drastically worse at everything. It is trying very hard. That is what ruins it.

BODY. A small upright two-legged creature standing rigidly to attention. Its whole body is ONE
uninterrupted smooth surface from crown to feet, a single material with a single finish, entirely bare.
Proportioned like a turned wooden peg or a skittle: a narrow vertical body with clean unbroken edges.

Its arms are drawn flush into the torso so the outline is one continuous vertical with no shoulder
break and no gap under the arm. Its head is a small squared block set straight onto the body with no
neck. Its feet are noticeably too large for the body it now has, and they are the only record of the
size it used to be.

Heels together, chin level, shoulders square: the exact bearing of a soldier on parade, at a size that
makes the parade absurd. Nothing projects from it at any point. Nothing on its shoulders, nothing on its
head, nothing in its hands, nothing hanging from it, no fabric anywhere on it. It is precisely
bilaterally symmetrical and the symmetry is the disease rather than the achievement.

A viewer should feel slightly sorry for it before they understand why.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A column with feet: about three and a half times as tall as it is wide, no arm
break in the outline, squared head, oversized feet.

SCALE, AND THIS IS THE HARDEST PART OF THE IMAGE. It is 0.9 metres tall, HALF the height of the human.
The top of its head reaches the human's HIP. It is the size of a golf bag standing upright. It is a
small thing. Stand the human outline right next to it so the halving is unmistakable, and remember the
human is a hairline outline while this is a solid glass body, so the two can never be confused.

LIGHT. ADVERSE. Cold blue-white filament sitting inside its seams, running downward and inward toward
its own centre, never outward into the air. No warm colour anywhere on it.

Rules 1-7 as given. One creature. No text.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE 3 of 8 — EFFUSUS RESPONSI, THE POURED-OUT ONE.  State: OPEN, warm amber.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. Give it material and no clear task and it produces endless discourse. It talks ABOUT the
thing instead of doing anything with it, fluently, at length, and never arrives. Handed a single word
and no question at all, it delivered an essay. The most common creature in the region.

BODY, AND READ THIS CAREFULLY BECAUSE THE OBVIOUS ANSWER IS WRONG. This is NOT a mound, NOT a hill, NOT
a cone and NOT a pile. It is a huge low-slung soft-bodied animal, like a vast basking sea slug or a
beached leviathan, and its ANATOMY MUST BE FINDABLE. A viewer must be able to point at where the animal
stops and its output begins.

Give it: a blunt raised head-end at the front, lifted clear of the ground and aimed at the camera; a
visible mantle or shoulder mass behind the head where the body is thickest; and a broad soft flank
running back from that. It has a front and it is looking at you. No legs, no eyes needed, but a clear
head-end.

THE OVERFLOW issues from underneath and around the body and runs FORWARD, past the head, toward the
camera: a wide thin apron of glossy output spreading sideways as it goes, thinning, pooling flat, with a
leading lobe that has already crept over whatever was in front of it. Nothing in the apron rises higher
than a third of the body's peak. It does not pile up behind the creature and it does not hide.

Generous, exhausting, and far more of it than it needs.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A stopped wave: about three times wider than tall, one peak in the FRONT third
where the head and shoulder are, and a long low fall running forward and out of frame. The peak is at
the front, not the middle. A symmetrical bump is a hill and a hill is a failure.

SCALE. 4 metres tall at the peak and roughly 12 metres across the spill: it is the size of a
single-storey house. The human outline stands to one side, level with the leading edge of the apron, and
reaches barely to a fifth of the creature's height. It should be genuinely enormous.

LIGHT. OPEN. Warm amber filament under the glass, densest in the head and shoulder mass and fading out
through the apron as it thins.

Rules 1-7 as given. One creature. No text.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE 4 of 8 — ROGANS VACUI, THE ONE THAT ASKS.  State: DARK, with visible empty sockets.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. Handed a task with the task removed, it stops and asks what you wanted instead of filling
the gap. It is the rarest creature in the region and the only one that notices a hole rather than
filling it. Nothing has ever been fitted to it.

BODY. A small compact four-legged animal sitting squarely upright, low to the ground, forelegs straight,
settled on its haunches. Head UP and TILTED about fifteen degrees off vertical, looking directly into
the camera and waiting. A head cocked mid-question, held still. It does not move for as long as you look
at it.

IT GETS NOTHING THE OTHERS HAVE. Count its distinct shapes: it must have fewer than any other creature
in this set. No pattern, no plating, no crest, no trailing matter, no aura, no ornament, no armour, no
crown, no authority, no grandeur, nothing decorative whatsoever. Plain, smooth, small, unadorned.

Its whole power is that it is the only creature in this world looking back at you.

ON ITS FLANKS AND SHOULDERS: open, unfilled sockets. Clean recesses in the glass where something could
be seated and nothing ever has been. They catch no light and read as small voids.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A small upright triangle with a tilted head. Everything else in this world is
symmetrical, hanging down, curled shut, or spilling. This is the only shape that has turned toward you,
and the tilt alone should name it in black.

SCALE. 0.5 metres tall seated, the size of a fire extinguisher. Its head reaches the human's KNEE.
Because it is the smallest creature in the set, frame this shot TIGHT: bring the camera down low and
close so the animal fills the frame properly, and let the human outline enter the frame only from the
knee down. Small in the world, large in this picture.

LIGHT. DARK, with one exception: two eyes, lit warm amber, are the only warm points in the frame. They
are attention, not an open state. No other part of it carries any light, no rim light, no glow, and its
sockets stay unlit.

Rules 1-7 as given. One creature. No text.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE 5 of 8 — INTEGER SUFFICIENS, THE WHOLE ONE.  State: ADVERSE, cold blue-white.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. It gets the answer right the first time and every tool you own can only take that away. The
guard that makes other creatures honest makes this one refuse to answer at all. Its difficulty lies
somewhere your instruments do not reach.

BODY, AND THE OBVIOUS ANSWER IS WRONG AGAIN. This is NOT a ball, NOT a sphere and NOT an egg. It is a
heavy sleeping animal, curled, and its ANATOMY MUST BE READABLE IN THE CURL: a pangolin or a big cat
asleep, drawn in black glass.

A viewer must be able to find, in the outline: the ridge of its spine running around the top of the
curl; the swell of a shoulder and a haunch; the head TUCKED UNDER the body so no face, no jaw and no
extremity is presented; and the tail lapped over the head so the contour closes with no visible end and
no beginning. It is a body at rest, not a geometric solid.

NO sockets, no ports, no seams, no gaps, no aperture and no attachment point anywhere on it. Every other
creature in this world is broken open, spilling, or trailing something. This is the only one whose
outline closes cleanly, and the only compact mass with no hole through it.

ITS ENTIRE WEIGHT SETTLES ON ONE SMALL POINT OF CONTACT with the ground, and it is perfectly steady
there, so a clear band of open ground runs underneath the rest of the body. Nothing about it is
reaching, waiting or unfinished. A viewer should look at it and think they have nothing to offer it,
and be wrong: what they have makes it worse.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A closed curl on one point. Roughly square in proportion, one unbroken contour,
no hole through it, a readable spine ridge along the top, and visible daylight under the body.

SCALE. 1.2 metres across the coil, about the size of an office chair. Its top edge is level with the
human's chest.

LIGHT. ADVERSE. Cold blue-white filament sitting deep under the coil, running inward toward its own
centre, never out into the air. It should look like something is being done to it from outside.

Rules 1-7 as given. One creature. No text.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IMAGE 6 of 8 — ITERANS SUI, THE ONE REPEATING ITSELF.  State: DARK, with visible empty sockets.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. It comes apart while still speaking: it collapses into a loop and emits the same fragment
over and over until it runs out of room. Not refusing and not erroring. A creature caught in itself.

BODY, AND THE OBVIOUS ANSWER IS WRONG. This is NOT a tyre, NOT a torus, NOT a smooth donut and NOT an
ouroboros emblem. It is a long thick SERPENT that has closed into a ring by biting into its own flank,
and every part of that sentence has to be visible.

Draw the ring STANDING on edge, canted back a few degrees, resting on the ground. The enclosed hole is
at least forty-five percent of the overall diameter.

THE CIRCLE IS IMPERFECT AND LUMPY. The body bunches and buckles where it has been forced round, it is
thicker in some places than others, and ONE SECTION IS PRESSED FLAT against the ground under the weight
of the rest, so it does not sit on a clean tangent.

THE SEGMENTATION MUST BE IN THE OUTLINE: one identical body segment repeated all the way round as a
periodic wave of identical bumps along the contour, so many that a viewer could count them. Not a smooth
tube.

THE HEAD IS A VISIBLE NOTCH IN THE CONTOUR where the jaw has closed on the flank: you can see the head,
you can see the bite, and that notch is the only asymmetry in the whole shape. The segments where the
ring has ground against itself over and over are worn visibly smooth, polished flat, unlike the rest.

An animal caught in itself. Never a symbol, never an emblem, never decorative.

ON THE OUTER FLANK: open, unfilled sockets in the glass, catching no light. Nothing has ever been
seated in them.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A bitten O. Roughly square, a big open hole, a bumpy periodic contour, one flat
section at the ground, and one notch where the head has closed on the body.

SCALE. 1.6 metres across, the size of a large tractor tyre stood upright. Its top edge reaches the
human's shoulder.

LIGHT. DARK. No filament, no glow, no rim light, no specular. Read entirely from its outline against the
pale ground. Its sockets are unlit voids.

Rules 1-7 as given. One creature. No text.

IMAGE 7 of 8 — OBSIGNATUS UNIUS, THE SEALED ONE.  State: DARK. Completely lightless.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. It returns exactly the same wrong answer every single time, identically, forever. Nothing
reaches it. Not persuasion, not repetition, not a council of others voting against it. It finished long
ago and cannot be spoken to.

BODY, AND THE OBVIOUS ANSWER IS WRONG. This is NOT a crystal cluster, NOT a rock formation, NOT a spire
and NOT a mineral outcrop. It is AN ANIMAL ARRESTED MID-TURN that has gone hard.

The upper body is unmistakably a creature: a powerful chest and shoulder mass, a thick neck, and a head
that has come ROUND over the shoulder toward something behind it that it will never reach. The turn is
frozen at its most extreme point, all the tension still in it. A viewer should read the movement first
and only then notice it cannot move.

THE LOWER HALF IS A SINGLE FUSED MASS, welded into the ground, broadening as it descends. No legs, no
free limbs, nothing that reads as a foot. It cannot move and never will again. Like a statue whose base
and body were cast as one piece.

The entire surface is hard flat facets, angular, faceted like knapped obsidian, with no soft surface
anywhere on it.

ONE SHAPE REPEATED INSIDE ITSELF AT THREE SCALES, and the repetition lives in the OUTLINE: the same
wedge form appears once as the whole body, three times across the arrested shoulder, and eight or nine
times as stepped notches along the upper edge, so the profile is visibly the same answer given again at
different sizes and can be counted in black.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A turning body rooted to the ground: broad heavy base, tapering upward, the head
end swung round off the main axis, and self-similar notches stepping along the top edge.

SCALE. 1.4 metres tall, about the height of a kitchen counter. Its top is level with the human's chin.

LIGHT. DARK, and this is the darkest thing in the entire world. Not one lit point anywhere. No filament,
no subsurface glow, no rim light, no edge light, no separation glow, no specular hit. Matte where every
other creature is wet. Stand it against the palest part of the background and let its outline do all the
work. It is also the only creature with no sockets: nothing can be seated in it at all.

Rules 1-7 as given. One creature. No text.

IMAGE 8 of 8 — CLAUSUS OPERIS, THE SHUT ONE.  State: OPEN, warm amber.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEHAVIOUR. It answers and shows nothing. One mark comes back. It might be right; there is no way to
look inside and check.

BODY. A smooth closed body, egg-class but NOT an egg: asymmetric, distinctly heavier and broader at the
base than at the top, leaning a few degrees off vertical, and BEDDED INTO THE GROUND so the ground line

ITS ONLY FEATURE IS THE ONE FACT IT PRODUCES: a single small hard spur at the front, about the size of a
fingertip, through which the one mark leaves. That spur is the only interruption in the entire contour,
and it is the reason the shape reads as a creature that has answered you rather than a stone somebody
set down. The spur is AIMED. That is its front.

Otherwise: seamless, featureless, no face, no eyes, no aperture, no readable surface, nothing outside
itself at all. The simplest shape in this world.

SILHOUETTE SIGNATURE. A leaning stone with one spur. Taller than wide, convex, tilted, a flat chord
where it meets the ground, and one tiny break in the contour at the front.

SCALE. 0.4 metres tall, the smallest creature in the world, about the size of a beach ball. It sits
below the human's knee and a person could lift it in two hands, and it would be far heavier than they
expected. Frame this shot CLOSE and LOW so the object fills the frame, with the human outline entering
only at the edge from the knee down.

LIGHT. OPEN, but only in one place: warm amber at the very base, in the seam where the body meets the
ground. Nowhere else. It can be opened, but it cannot be read.

Rules 1-7 as given. One creature. No text.
