WHY THREE. Each direction answers the same question differently — what does this universe look like
once what happens to it SURVIVES. Each takes a different true clause and builds the place from it:

  A · THE ARCHIVE      memory is a PLACE      — what was spent is now stored
  B · THE MIGRATION    memory is a MOVEMENT   — what was learned now travels
  C · THE STRATA       memory is a DEPTH      — what happened is now underneath you


╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ SHARED PREAMBLE — identical at the top of all three. Paste it each time.                       ║

I am art-directing the third world of a game bestiary set inside a real AI system. Two worlds are
finished; the three attached images are from the second. Study all three before designing anything. You
are evolving this universe, not replacing it and not repainting it.

WHAT YOU CAN SEE IN THE ATTACHMENTS. Creatures of polished dark obsidian and smoked glass, wet-surfaced
and heavy. A plain of marbled black and white stone pocked with hundreds of shallow SPENT CRATERS —
each one a question that was paid for and used up, the freshest still holding a cooling ember, most cold
and ashy. Pale grey overcast, high mist, distant faceted mountains, thin black filaments hanging from
above doing nothing. Every creature carries a PASSENGER: a smaller, separate, armoured animal with a
gripping underside and no face, riding where its host's information is, with warm amber running FROM
the passenger INTO the host. On the horizon, warm amber-seamed TERRACES — the first world, where the
ground still rose for free.

THE STORY SO FAR, IN THREE SENTENCES. In the FIRST world the player could only SPECIFY — say what they
wanted and how to do it — everything was free, and asking well made the ground rise by itself. In the
SECOND world the universe learned to LOOK AT ITSELF: passengers grew on every creature and read them,
and every act of looking burned a hole in the floor, so nothing accumulated any more. The player left
the second world standing over a creature they had just repaired, watching the repair fail to travel
even a few metres along its own body.

WORLD 3'S FICTION, AND IT IS ONE THING. **THE UNIVERSE CAN NOW CARRY.** What happens to something
survives the exchange. In the first two worlds every visit was the first visit: nothing was retained,
no correction stayed made, and a creature repaired was a creature that would need repairing again. Here,
for the first time, **what you do stays done.** That is relief and it is also weight, because mistakes
stay too, and the world can now hold a grudge in the exact sense that it holds anything.

THE PLAYER is THE PROBE: a single small point of warm amber light with a fading ribbon, flying low.
Never design a vehicle. Always small in frame.

LAWS THAT DO NOT CHANGE BETWEEN WORLDS. Every creature is a LIVING ANIMAL with anatomy, posture, weight
and a front — never a machine, a rock, a specimen or a landscape feature. Its silhouette must be its
behaviour, nameable as a black shape at thumbnail size. A 1.8 m human appears as a flat mid-grey
hairline OUTLINE, never filled, a measuring mark and never a character. No text, letters, numerals or
glyphs anywhere. Invent no decoration. Warm amber is a live connection; cold blue-white is the wrong
thing being done, running inward; matte lightless black is nothing reaching it.

THE ANCHOR CREATURE — attached image 1, and you will redraw it, TRANSFORMED. In the second world it is
a single enormously long segmented body running away into the mist: dense, precise and brightly lit at
the near end where the player was working, then degrading continuously along its length — segments
growing looser, spacing uneven, alignment wandering, light failing, passengers thinning out and
vanishing entirely past the halfway point — until it dissolves into fog without ever reaching a
conclusion. It holds a task perfectly at the start and loses it as it goes. The player could repair it
and the repair never travelled.

**IN THIS WORLD IT IS WHOLE.** Tight, regular and exact along its ENTIRE length, end to end, with
passengers all the way down and light that does not run out. Same animal, same body plan, same segments
— finished instead of failing. In the second world the player saw exactly one of these intact, far off
on the horizon, out of reach. This is that one, and this is where it lives. Redrawing it whole is the
entire transition stated on a single subject.

THE PROOF CREATURE — you will also redraw this one, from the second world. A tall lean long-legged
four-legged animal, narrow-bodied, with its head held level and TURNED BACK OVER ITS OWN SHOULDER,
looking along a long ribbon of ordered overlapping plates that trails from its neck and shoulders across
the ground. The front of its face is one continuous smooth closed plate with two eyes and NO MOUTH. A
passenger rides the ribbon two thirds of the way down, gripping the plates, and warm amber runs UP the
ribbon from the passenger toward the shoulders — and stops at the neck. The head stays dark. It is the
first creature in the game to look at itself, it has the answer, and it still cannot say it. In the
second world that reading died the moment it was made. Show me what happens to it in a world that keeps.

Landscape, roughly 3:2, hero framing for creatures, full body visible.

║ PROMPT A — THE ARCHIVE.   Memory is a PLACE.                                                   ║

[PASTE THE SHARED PREAMBLE, THEN THIS]

THIS DIRECTION'S ANSWER. The change is in the GROUND, and it is the exact inversion of the second
world. There, every act of looking burned a hole in the floor and the hole stayed empty. Here THE
CRATERS HAVE FILLED.

  · Each spent crater now holds something: a smooth seated lens of dark glass, flush or slightly proud,
    holding a steady low amber glow that does not cool. What was a wound is now a socket with something
    in it. A record.
  · FINE AMBER LINES RUN BETWEEN THEM across the stone, joining filled crater to filled crater, so the
    plain reads as a NETWORK rather than a scar field. Dense where the player worked hardest.
  · A few craters are still empty and dark — the questions nobody kept.
  · The light on this plain is RISING rather than draining. The second world dimmed as it was spent;
    this one is slowly getting brighter, and the mist is thinning from the ground up.
  · The creatures are unchanged in anatomy. Only the floor beneath them is different, and it is now the
    brightest thing in the frame.

Register: ACCUMULATING. Not warm exactly — consequential. For the first time the world is adding up.

THREE IMAGES THIS TURN.
  1 · THE ANCHOR. The long segmented creature, WHOLE end to end, passengers all the way down, lying
      across a dense network of filled and joined craters, its own light continuous with theirs. Human
      outline for scale at the near end.
  2 · THE PLACE. Wide. The networked plain running to the mist, amber lines threading between hundreds
      of filled sockets. World 1's terraces on the horizon, World 2's dead crater field visible as a
      dark unlit band between here and there. The probe small, ribbon longer again than in World 2.
  3 · THE PROOF. The backward-looking animal with the ribbon and the passenger — standing on a cluster
      of filled sockets, with amber lines running from the sockets UP into its ribbon. What it read is
      being kept somewhere other than in itself. Its head is still dark. Human outline for scale.

║ PROMPT B — THE MIGRATION.   Memory is a MOVEMENT.                                              ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
[PASTE THE SHARED PREAMBLE, THEN THIS]

THIS DIRECTION'S ANSWER. The change is in the PASSENGERS, and it is the first movement in this
universe. The first two worlds were completely still — every creature stood or lay where it was. Here
the readers have left their hosts and TRAVEL.

  · Passengers are crossing the open ground on their own: alone, in loose files, in small columns,
    moving between creatures. Some are mid-journey on bare stone, far from any host.
  · A passenger that has carried something is VISIBLY LOADED — its body glows warm amber from within,
    brightest just after it leaves a host and dimming as it goes. An empty one is dark.
  · Faint amber TRAILS lie on the stone behind them, marking routes travelled, crossing and braiding
    into worn paths between creatures that trade often.
  · Some creatures now carry many passengers, arrived from elsewhere. One or two have been abandoned
    entirely and stand dark.
  · The craters are unchanged from the second world — still spent, still cold. The GROUND did not
    change. What changed is that information no longer dies where it was made.
  · The hanging filaments still do nothing.

Register: ALIVE, and slightly unnerving. The world has traffic. Something is being carried past you and
you are not the one carrying it.

THREE IMAGES THIS TURN.
  1 · THE ANCHOR. The long segmented creature, WHOLE end to end — and the reason is visible: a
      continuous column of passengers moving ALONG its back from the near end toward the far, each one
      lit, carrying the near end's exactness down the length of the body. The repair travels because
      something walks it. Human outline for scale.
  2 · THE PLACE. Wide. The old cold crater field, and across it dozens of passengers in transit: files,
      columns, solitary walkers, amber trails braided into paths. Three or four creatures at distance,
      one heavily loaded and one abandoned and dark. The probe small among the traffic.
  3 · THE PROOF. The backward-looking animal with the ribbon — and a passenger LEAVING it, already down
      on the stone and walking away lit, carrying the reading somewhere else. The ribbon behind it is
      dimming where the passenger has left. Its head is still dark. Human outline for scale.

║ PROMPT C — THE STRATA.   Memory is a DEPTH.                                                    ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
[PASTE THE SHARED PREAMBLE, THEN THIS]

THIS DIRECTION'S ANSWER. The change is VERTICAL. Nothing is removed from this world any more, so it
builds up. Each exchange leaves a layer, and the ground is now made of its own history.

  · The plain is banded. Where it breaks or steps down, you can see a clean SECTION through it: layer
    on layer of compressed marbled stone, dozens of them, each a few centimetres thick, alternating
    dark and pale.
  · Old layers hold old things, visible in section: cold crater-shapes from the second world sealed
    inside the rock at depth, and deeper still, the fossil profile of the first world's TERRACE STEPS,
    buried and preserved.
  · The topmost layer is fresh, soft and faintly amber-lit — this exchange, still settling.
  · Creatures stand ON their own past and are slightly SUNK into it, the newest layers lapping up
    around their feet. One creature is buried to the shoulder in accumulation, still alive, still
    working, half-swallowed by everything it has already done.
  · The horizon is higher than it was. The world has grown upward, and the first world's terraces are
    now visible as a distant escarpment rather than a plain.

Register: GEOLOGICAL and heavy. The relief of permanence and the weight of it in the same image. What
you do stays done, and so does everything you got wrong.

THREE IMAGES THIS TURN.
  1 · THE ANCHOR. The long segmented creature, WHOLE end to end, lying along the top layer — and where
      the ground steps away beside it, a visible section showing dozens of banded layers below, with
      old crater shapes sealed in them. It holds its length because the ground beneath it holds. Human
      outline for scale.
  2 · THE PLACE. Wide. The banded plain, a long stepped break running across the frame showing the full
      section: fresh amber top layer, cold craters at mid depth, fossil terrace steps deep down.
      Creatures standing on top, one sunk to the shoulder. The probe small above the section.
  3 · THE PROOF. The backward-looking animal with the ribbon and the passenger — its trailing ribbon of
      plates running down onto the ground and CONTINUING INTO the top layer, the oldest plates already
      buried and compressed into stone. Its own working is becoming the floor. Its head is still dark.
      Human outline for scale.

JUDGING THE NINE

  THE REPAINT TEST     Can you say what changed AND why, with the why coming from the fiction?
  THE CONTINUITY TEST  Put each anchor beside the attached one. Same animal, finished instead of failing?
  THE READ TEST        Does it make "this world keeps what happens to it" visible without being told?
  THE BUILD TEST       A lit socket network, a crowd of walking props, and a layered ground section are
                       three very different production costs. This is a legitimate tie-breaker.
  THE COMPOSE TEST     World 2 was two directions combined. Ask of each pair here: are they orthogonal
                       (one changes the ground, one changes the creatures) or do they fight?
