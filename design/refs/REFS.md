## What REF-01 locks as canon (adopted into the SPECs + the build)

- **Ember-in-cage organs:** every organ is the same thin wireframe octahedral cage; EARNED = a
  bright amber ember inside; FOG = completely empty. (Supersedes solid-vs-ghost.)
- **Two-tori core:** the faceted amber icosahedron wrapped in exactly two thin tilted tori +
  the hair-thin vertical beam. Label: THE MOUTH · THE HUB.
- **Per-zone ring treatments:** inner sealed-double (SENSITIVE · LOCAL ONLY) · middle solid
  (PRIVATE · NO TRAINING) · outer finely-dashed (PUBLIC · OPEN). Rings are permission
  boundaries, never orbits.
- **Labels-on-approach:** tiny engraved IBM-Plex-Mono capitals, structure-grey, never amber.
- **The caption line:** "EVERY LIGHT IS SOMETHING THAT REALLY HAPPENED" — the game's standing
  tagline surface.
