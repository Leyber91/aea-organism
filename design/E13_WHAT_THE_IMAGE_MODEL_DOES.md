# E13 — WHAT THE IMAGE MODEL ACTUALLY DOES

*Read off roughly forty generations across seven rounds, 2026-07-26/27. Not theory: each row is a*
*thing that happened more than once. Companion to E11 (how a brief fails) and E12 (how a world evolves).*

---

## THE SIX BEHAVIOURS THAT HELD EVERY TIME

**1 · ONE SUBJECT PER FRAME IS THE SINGLE BIGGEST LEVER.** Nine creatures in one plate gave each a
ninth of the model's attention and four came back as blobs — a hill, a ball, a tyre, a spire. The same
four, one per frame, came back solved. Nothing else in this list is worth as much.

**2 · A REAL-WORLD REFERENT LANDS. AN ABSTRACT DESCRIPTION DRIFTS.** Every time a referent was named
the image hit it: *like a fencing mask with no grille*, *the blunt seamless toe of a boot*, *a remora
on a shark*, *a scaled-up car tyre stood upright*, *a golf bag standing up*. Every time the same idea
was described in the abstract, it wandered. The referent does more work than three sentences of
adjectives.

**3 · POSITIVE ANATOMY BEATS NEGATION, AND NEGATION SUMMONS.** *"No robes, no crown, no staff, no mark
of office"* produced a crowned hierarch three times running. *"One uninterrupted smooth surface from
crown to feet, proportioned like a turned wooden peg"* produced it correctly on the first attempt.

**4 · NAMING THE WRONG ANSWER PREVENTS IT.** The four blob creatures were each fixed by an explicit
line — *this is NOT a mound / NOT a ball / NOT a tyre / NOT a crystal cluster* — placed **before** the
positive description. The model reaches for the nearest common object; saying which one it is
reaching for stops it.

**5 · ATTACHED IMAGES BEAT PROSE ABOUT IMAGES.** Three rounds of description produced beige machines.
One round with the previous world attached produced the whole system. When evolving anything, attach
what you are evolving *from* — the model needs the evidence, not the account of it.

**6 · GIVE IT THE BEHAVIOUR AND IT DESIGNS BETTER THAN THE SPEC.** *"A second thing observes the
first, and the information travels inward"* produced the passenger — a distinct armoured organism
gripping the host's information ribbon, amber running inward. Better than anything specified. The
brief's job is the **fiction and the constraint**, not the geometry.

## THE FIVE FAILURES THAT ALSO HELD EVERY TIME

**1 · A MECHANISM'S SHAPE OVERRULES ITS STATED FUNCTION.** Apertures on filaments that *read without
warming* came back as **pendant lamps**. A cone of light falling from a fixture **means illumination**,
and no amount of "this only reads" survives it. If a mechanism needs a sentence explaining what it is
not, the form is wrong.

**2 · ABSENCES DO NOT RENDER.** *"It has no mouth"* never once appeared. The model draws a face and a
face has a mouth. It only landed when written as a **positive surface**: one continuous plate, two
eyes high on it, unbroken to the end of the muzzle.

**3 · EXTREME SCALE RATIOS FLATTEN.** A 30:1 range across one frame collapsed toward uniform every
time, even with metres, frame-height fractions and human landmarks given together. Extreme scale needs
its own frame or its own sheet.

**4 · COMPETING INSTRUCTIONS RESOLVE AS COMPROMISE.** Ask for visible working *and* a terse reply and
you get neither cleanly. The model does not prioritise; it averages.

**5 · EVERYTHING GETS RIM LIGHT.** Lighting a subject is what rendering *is*. Darkness only held when
specified as a **material property plus a placement**: light-absorbing, the darkest value in frame, no
point brighter than the ground behind it, placed against the palest part of the background.

## WORLD 3'S MORPHOLOGY PROBLEM, WHICH IS NEW

Worlds 1 and 2 are both **single frozen moments** and every technique above assumes that. World 3's
faculty is **persistence** — what happens survives — and persistence is *temporal*. A still image has
no time.

**That is the actual design problem and it is different in kind from anything solved so far.** The
three directions written before the experiments (`ARCHIVE` / `MIGRATION` / `STRATA`) were not
orthogonal: archive and strata are both accumulation wearing different clothes.

There are only a few honest ways a still image carries duration:

| device | time becomes | risk |
|---|---|---|
| **accumulation** | depth. Strata, rings, sediment | reads as geology, not biology |
| **trace** | a path. Wakes, worn grooves, polished tracks | reads as decoration on an unchanged world |
| **simultaneity** | extent. One body showing several of its own stages at once | reads as confusion unless the body is legibly one animal |
| long exposure | smear | loses the silhouette, which is the diagnosis |
| repetition | a sequence | needs several frames, and one-per-frame is the rule |

The last two are ruled out by behaviours 1 and the silhouette law. **The three that survive are
accumulation, trace and simultaneity, and they are genuinely orthogonal** — one changes the ground,
one changes the surface, one changes the creature.

**And simultaneity is already in the bestiary.** `Labens longitudinis` degrades continuously along its
own length: tight and exact at the near end, dissolving by the far one. **That creature's body is
already its own timeline.** Generalising it is not an invention, it is reading what we already drew.
