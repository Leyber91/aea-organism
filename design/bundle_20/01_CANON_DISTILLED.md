# THE PROBE — CANON, DISTILLED

Everything the artist needs about this world, and nothing else.

## 1. What the game is

The player pilots a small exploratory probe inside LEYBER — a living artificial intelligence
running on the player's own machine, assembled from real, free AI models. The game never fakes
anything: every number on screen is a live measurement, every machine the player builds actually
executes real work, and failure is drawn, annotated and acknowledged rather than hidden.

The journey: begin alone in the dark with one keyless socket; learn the entity organ by organ;
then assemble your own assistant from its parts and set it loose. The antagonist is not a villain
— it is FOG: the gap between what has been built and what is understood. Mapping is the act of
completing. The moral centre is a moment late in the game where the machine drafts a real message
and only the human hand may send it.

Never depict the intelligence as a face, figure, eye or robot. It is always architecture,
instruments, and light.

## 2. The world

A vast dark plain called THE FIELD, under a night sky with a low amber horizon band.

- **THE FOUNDRY** — a row of fifteen power plants across the horizon, each a real AI provider.
  Some are local and private and never sleep; some are distant, metered, and rate-limited; one
  is keyless and answers anyone. Only the ones the player has unlocked carry light.
- **THE NEXUS** — a low slab at the field's centre where the player composes machines. Concentric
  rings are etched into the ground radiating from it.
- **THE ARCHIVE** — a mine descending below the field: strata of dark crystalline shelves, veins
  of preserved memory, a single shaft of light going down.
- **THE MIND SPIRE** — the tallest structure; where thought is routed.
- **THE WATCHER GATE** — an aperture that judges every autonomous act and returns a verdict.
- **THE BROADCAST MAST** — dark until a real message is sent by a human hand.
- **THE SOCKET** — a small pylon at the edge of the foundry, keyless, the first thing that ever
  answered. Where the game begins.

## 3. The architecture the game teaches — 29 elements in six families

Each family owns one glyph shape. The shapes are the only iconography in this world.

**FIVE AXES — how far an entity reaches (PENTAGON):**
PATH: it defines its own next step · MULTIPLICITY: many minds in parallel, synthesised ·
ABSTRACTION: memory, tools, and writing new skills · PROMPTING: a scaffold makes a cheap mind
beat its raw self · ASYNC: it acts unattended, in its own time.

**TEN SEEDS — the organs (CIRCLE):**
SUBSTRATE: the grid of model-plants that answers · SHARP OBJECTIVE: every task carries a
falsifiable scorer · CRYSTALLIZE: freeze a repeated behaviour into a reusable tool ·
FLEXIBILIZE: kill a node mid-run, the route falls over, the task still completes ·
SELF-VERSION: a run writes a new skill, a later run uses it · SELF-MODEL: it reports what it is
made of and judges its own work · CEILING-DETECT: it senses its own limit and escalates ·
TRANSCENDENCE: it reaches real external tools · BOUNDARY: private work never leaves the machine,
the sacred wall · BACKWARDS CHANNEL: one run leaves a capsule the next run reads.

**THREE VERBS — the per-tick rail (TRIANGLE):**
COMPOSE: assemble parts into a whole · PROPAGATE: the honesty node, show the live trace of who
did what · OBSERVE: the state is visible.

**FOUR MECHANICS — growth between heartbeats (SQUARE):**
CRYSTALLIZE · FLEXIBILIZE · SELF-VERSION · CEILING-DETECT.

**FOUR OPS — the operator's loop (DIAMOND):**
DESIGN: tag the task before running · TIME: every tick stamped and observable · SHIP:
unskippable, produce a real artifact in the world · LEARN: each run measurably improves the next.

**THREE PRINCIPLES — what emerges (HEXAGON):**
EMERGENCE OVER IMPOSITION: no central controller · RESTORABLE COHERENCE: a failure reroutes and
the system still completes · OPERATOR-OBSERVABLE TIME: an append-only timeline anyone can audit.

## 4. The six measured combination doctrines

THE SOLO LAW: an easy task wants one good mind; a council hurts it · THE DIVERSE COUNCIL: a hard
task is rescued by unlike models voting — diversity, not repetition · THE LONE VERIFIER RISK: one
judge can be wrong alone · THE GENETIC RELAY: minds hand a capsule down a chain and the toolkit
grows · THE RAMIFICATION: decompose, escalate, depth-cap — a tree of small minds ·
THE CRYSTAL PATH: search once for the winner, then run cheap forever.

## 5. The six rungs of the journey

CHAT — alone in the dark; one keyless socket answers.
SOURCES — collect model-plants; capacity is a set of locks.
MEMORY — mine the past into recallable ingots.
FLOWS — one door routes thought: solo, council, swarm, or crystallised path.
POWERS — governed hands that may touch the outside world, behind a membrane.
THE WILD — the assembled assistant stands free of any tether and runs on its own.

## 6. The voice of this world

Terminal register: lowercase, terse, concrete. Never ornamental, never mystical, never cute.
Inscriptions are short and declarative. Examples of the correct tone, all canon:

- "failure is information. not an exception."
- "the machine can draft. only the human can mean it."
- "what is not known, sleeps. what is known, connects. what is understood, resonates."
- "memory is weight. we descend to understand."
- "fifteen silent witnesses. one still speaks."

Captions on drawings use the same discipline: a designation code, a plain name, and where useful
one measured fact. Never marketing language. Never exclamation.
