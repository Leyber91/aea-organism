## 1. RESEARCH — what the best playable aesthetics 2018–2025 actually teach

The rule of this section: extract the PRINCIPLE, never the style. We are not becoming
Sable or Tunic; we are learning why they read as authored and current.

**Sable (Shedworks, 2021)** — Moebius line + flat color. The transferable lesson is not
the line: **depth in a stylized world is LAYERED, not free** — Shedworks got readability
from three authored layers: light/shadow ("helps players figure out where they sit on a
surface"), distance fog called "really, really key," and outlines fading with distance
to carry perspective and hide pop-in [S-1][S-2]. Ours buys depth with fog + emissive
hierarchy; §5 adds the missing layer (authored surface gradients).

**Solar Ash (Heart Machine, 2021)** — the lesson is **motion is identity**: the trail,
the gesture of traversal, is an art asset with the same authority as any building, and
studio DNA survives a renderer change when tone/atmosphere pillars are named and kept
[S-3]. Our probe's trail is currently the least-authored thing on screen (§4).

**Hyper Light Drifter / Breaker (Heart Machine)** — **palette discipline + silhouette
readability are the style pillars that survive any technology jump**; Breaker's team
frames its cel look as "a style that can be easily broken," so the pillars are guarded
explicitly [S-4][S-5]. Our two-ink law IS this discipline, already stricter than theirs.
What we lack is the silhouette half (§4.2).

**Tunic (2022)** — "the rigidness of the vertical lines and the regularity of the grid"
chosen ON PURPOSE, plus a named one-word style test ("tunicy") every asset must pass
[S-6]. Lesson: **regularity is legal when it is a decision, not a default** — and a
named test catches drift. Ours exists: A11 §1's one-second test.

**Cocoon (Geometric Interactive, 2023)** — buildings "seem grown out of their
surroundings"; each world's palette held close to one anchor hue so the player never
misreads which world they hold [S-7]. Lesson: **form should look generated by the
world's own logic, and hue carries meaning or it doesn't ship.** Our amber already
carries meaning (A11 §1); our building forms still look placed, not grown (§4.2).

**Animal Well (Billy Basso, 2024)** — dense pixel light: ALL lighting dynamic, normal
maps under 2D art, and the decisive upgrade was a **rim-light shader** that pushed
everything into shadow with highlighted edges, tying foreground and background together
[S-8]. Lesson: **density comes from light behavior, not geometry count.** Our fresnel
shells are exactly this instinct `[BUILT]`; §5 extends it to the ground and wires.

**Neon White (Angel Matrix, 2022)** — HUD information dispersed at speed: silhouette-
first cards, oversized unmistakable type, "clear, concise, consistent" [S-9]. Lesson:
**clarity at speed is itself a style** — confident scale beats decoration. Our
10/13/20–28px scale is disciplined but never confident; §5 move 11 fixes that once.

**Chants of Sennaar (Rundisc, 2023)** — wireframe-exposed edges began as compensation
for missing textures, then became the comic-plate identity (Druillet/Moebius/Schuiten
color blocks) [S-10]. Lesson: **a limitation becomes a signature the moment it is
claimed out loud and pushed to consistency.** Ours: two inks + no assets. Claimed here.

**Citizen Sleeper (Jump Over the Age, 2022)** — the UI is the game because "the form
perfectly reflected the content — the AI you play can only experience the world through
text and interfaces" [S-11]. Lesson: **UI-as-substance is a legitimate, current art
direction** — the strongest external validation of our terminal-voice motif (A11 §3.4).

**Exo One (Exbleative, 2021)** — a photography-informed developer spending the budget
on sky and atmosphere control; critics called it "a masterclass in atmosphere" [S-12].
Lesson: **atmosphere can be the content** when authored like a photograph — graded,
directional, never uniform. Our fog is already narrative (density is a save value
`[BUILT]`); our horizon still needs the photographic grade (§5 move 6).

**Outer Wilds (Mobius, 2019)** — one governing prompt ("camping in space"); the art
director's goal: "not just make the game look pretty but enhance the design" [S-13].
Lesson: **every visual choice traces to one sentence.** Ours: amber is understanding;
the void is the not-yet-understood (A11 §2).

**Hardspace: Shipbreaker (Blackbird, 2020–22)** — fully diegetic UI grounded in one
period fiction (cassette futurism), instruments modeled on real jets and Apple-II-era
monochrome, deliberately worn, never sleek [S-14]. Lesson: **diegetic UI stays credible
when the whole interface commits to one device fiction** — our PROBE OS one-device rule
(04 §2) is this law already.

**Awwwards-class WebGL (Lusion; Active Theory)** — the studios setting the web ceiling
use Three.js "for atmosphere and framing rather than spectacle"; the separator between
web-tier and game-tier frames is the COMPOSED post stack — bloom + depth treatment +
grade + grain as one authored chain, shipped to mobile [S-15]. Lesson: **post is not
decoration; it is color management** — the frame is finished in the stack. Ours is
half-built (§4.3).

## 2. WHAT READS "2000s" vs WHAT READS CURRENT — the tells

Named concretely so screenshots can be judged against them. A frame fails on tells it
exhibits, not on age of technique.

**Reads 2000s (the offender list):**
1. Default material response — one flat albedo + uniform roughness across large
   surfaces; nothing on the surface was decided by a human [S-16].
2. GridHelper floors and tron-grids — the canonical programmer-art placeholder [S-16].
3. Repeated tiling window textures on box primitives, worse when linear-filtered to blur.
4. Rows of identical props at even spacing — composition by loop index.
5. Uniform fog with no authored horizon — depth exists but nobody graded it.
6. No color management — linear output, no tone curve, banding in the darks (dark
   scenes band without dithering; fix = sub-1/255 noise pre-quantization [S-17][S-18]).
7. Hard UI boxes, beveled buttons, default fonts.
8. Sprite-glow inflation — additive halos on everything, lens flares.
9. Default tone mapping / no exposure decision.

**Reads current (the modern tells):**
1. Authored gradients — sky, ground, and surfaces carry deliberate ramps; nothing is
   flat unless flatness is the statement [S-1][S-15].
2. Palette discipline — few hues, each with a JOB (HLD, Cocoon, Sennaar) [S-4][S-7][S-10].
3. Silhouette-first shape language — objects identifiable at thumbnail distance [S-5][S-9].
4. Dithering/grain as texture — noise used intentionally, from Obra Dinn's stabilized
   1-bit dither to web-tier film grain [S-19][S-20].
5. Oversized confident typography at sanctioned moments [S-9].
6. A composed post stack: bloom + grade + vignette + grain as one chain [S-15].
7. Light as meaning — rim light, light that travels, light that tracks data [S-8].
8. Diegetic, one-device UI fiction [S-11][S-14].

The blunt summary: 2000s = defaults left visible; current = every layer shows a
decision. Our UI shows decisions everywhere; our 3D field still shows defaults (§4.2).

## 3. R128-SAFE TECHNIQUE LIST — what our engine can do, with numbers

Constraint: three.js r128, no build step, no asset downloads, existing composer =
RenderPass + UnrealBloomPass(0.65, 0.4, 0.5) locked (A4 §8). All verified against r128
API surface; nothing below requires a newer release.

1. **Vertex colors** — `geometry.setAttribute('color', ...)` + `vertexColors: true` on
   MeshStandardMaterial. Cost: bytes per vertex, zero per-frame cost. Our boxes are
   8–24 verts; authored vertical gradients are effectively free [S-21].
2. **onBeforeCompile injection** — r128 supports patching built-in materials; lets the
   ground/buildings keep MeshStandardMaterial (fog + lights intact) while adding ramp
   or distance terms. Cost: one-time shader compile.
3. **Ramp/matcap shading** — MeshToonMaterial with a 2–4 px NearestFilter gradient map,
   or MeshMatcapMaterial (lighting baked into a 64px canvas texture, "does not respond
   to lights," near-zero cost) [S-21]. Fit: ours is a lights-as-meaning game, so matcap
   is legal only on structure-ink props, never on anything that must track live state.
4. **Instancing** — InstancedMesh, r128-native. Numbers: 10,000 Meshes = 10,000 draw
   calls vs 1; a 10k-object scene measured 15fps as meshes vs 60fps instanced; a real
   scene cut 9,000 draw calls to 300 [S-22]. Budget law: any repeated prop class that
   passes ~50 copies gets instanced. Caveat: keep instanced geometry simple — the win
   is draw calls, not vertex count [S-22].
5. **Baked gradients in CanvasTexture** — our existing zero-asset trick (windows, DOT
   sprite) extends to ramps, ground rings, and noise tiles. `NearestFilter` for crisp
   pixels, `RepeatWrapping` for tiles. Cost: one canvas rasterize at build time.
6. **Fog art direction** — FogExp2 density as a live variable `[BUILT — 0.011 → 0.009
   on foundry_full]` is already beyond most titles; per-district density/color stepping
   is the Sable per-biome move [S-1] and costs one lerp per frame.
7. **Cheap post within the locked stack** — the bloom pass is our entire glow budget
   (bloom re-tune banned, A11 §6.2). What CAN change at near-zero cost: (a) a final
   ShaderPass doing gamma-correct output + ordered dither — one 8x8 Bayer lookup or
   ALU hash per pixel, no texture fetch, "add a small value to every pixel right before
   it is quantized" [S-17][S-18]; (b) `material.dithering = true` on built-in materials
   (r128 flag) for banding on those surfaces [S-23].
8. **Dither/grain shaders** — full retro-dither is a solved web technique [S-20], but
   Obra Dinn's devlog is the warning: screen-space patterns SWIM under camera motion and
   needed camera-tied pattern-warping to stabilize [S-19]. Law for us: dither stays
   sub-perceptual (banding repair) or proves motion-stable before it may be visible.
9. **sRGB output** — `renderer.outputEncoding = sRGBEncoding`; with an EffectComposer
   the encoding must be applied by the LAST pass (GammaCorrectionShader) because
   composer render targets are linear — exactly the vendored-pass path already named in
   04 §8.1 `[PLANNED]`.
10. **Points budgets** — our particle draw calls today: dust 800 + embers 180 + trail 90
    + stars 1200 = 4 draw calls total `[BUILT]`. Points scale to tens of thousands
    before draw-call pressure; per-point size/alpha needs a ShaderMaterial or attribute
    + onBeforeCompile, still one draw call.

## 4. THE AUDIT — our own frame, judged by §2, honestly

Read against world.html as on disk 2026-07-20 (line refs to the shipping file).

### 4.1 What already reads CURRENT — protect these
- **The void composition** — one small intense light in a vast dark field (A11 §2). No
  2000s game holds this much restraint; it is our strongest frame asset. `[BUILT]`
- **Two-ink discipline** — stricter palette law than HLD or Sennaar, and the hue means
  something (the save file). Ahead of the reference class, not behind it. `[BUILT]`
- **The terminal voice + receipts** — Citizen Sleeper proves UI-as-substance is a
  current direction [S-11]; ours is backed by live data, which no reference title has.
  `[BUILT]`
- **Authored sky** — custom dome shader, zenith→horizon gradient + breathing amber band
  0.10–0.18 (world.html:366–374). This is a decided surface, not a default. `[BUILT]`
- **Narrative fog** — fog color == clear color, density is a save value. Fog with a JOB
  passes the modern bar by definition. `[BUILT]`
- **Fresnel rims** — probe warm / world cold (world.html:414–420) is the Animal Well
  rim-light instinct [S-8]. `[BUILT]`
- **ACES tone mapping + exposure 1.1** — a decision, not a default (world.html:361). `[BUILT]`
- **One-device OS fiction, corner brackets, no radius, no shadows** — Hardspace-grade
  diegetic commitment [S-14]. `[BUILT]`

### 4.2 What reads DATED — the named offenders
1. **GridHelper(400, 80) at 0.35** (world.html:385) — offender class §2.1/2: the
  canonical placeholder floor, shipping in every boot frame.
2. **Box-primitive plants with repeated random-window CanvasTexture** (windowTexture,
  world.html:426–431; makePlant 433–446) — 64x128 linear-filtered canvas, repeat 2x1.2:
  tiling-window tell AND blur tell in one. The window pattern is also random rather
  than load-derived — a texture pretending to be data in a game whose law is data.
3. **Fifteen near-identical silhouettes at loop-index spacing** (world.html:450–455) —
  one box + one cylinder each, evenly spaced in a straight row: composition by loop
  index, no silhouette language. The row reads placed, not grown [S-7].
4. **Flat 1600x1600 ground plane, one albedo, one roughness** (world.html:382–384) —
  default material response across the largest surface in every frame.
5. **No color management** — outputEncoding unset (linear); the UI bible already names
  this deviation (§8.1) and gate 3 (WebGL amber == CSS amber) is UNVERIFIED. With a
  near-black palette this also means visible banding risk in every dark gradient
  (backlight sprites, sky) [S-17].
6. **Uniform-emissive road strips** (world.html:485–492) — light with no falloff; flat
  neon reads 2000s; light in this game must behave like something traveling or pooling.
  The beacon beam is the same class (uniform-opacity additive cylinder,
  world.html:531–532) and sits inside the canonical portrait — fixed with Move 7's ramp
  technique. — edited 2026-07-20 (nine-critic closure)
7. **Flat starfield** — 1200 points, one color, one size (world.html:388–391): the
  screensaver tell.
8. **Scanlines as wallpaper** — already a named deviation (04 §8.8); a texture pass
  with no signal behind it is exactly the "unearned signal" A11 §6 bans.

### 4.3 The honest summary
The IDENTITY layer (UI, palette, composition, type, fiction) is current — in places
ahead of the reference class. The SURFACE layer of the 3D field is where the 2000s
lives: four default-visible surfaces (grid, ground, boxes, roads) plus the missing
color-management tail. The plan touches exactly that layer, nothing identity-bearing.

## 5. THE EVOLUTION PLAN — twelve moves, signature-strengthening only

Format per move: WHAT → WHY (research) → r128 sketch → cost (S = under half a session,
M = about one session, L = needs its own ticket) → rung it rides (P0_PROTOTYPES.md).
No move adds a third hue, a fifth ritual, or an unearned pixel. Bloom stays locked.

**MOVE 1 — Kill the GridHelper; ground becomes the concentric field.** Replace the grid
with ground rings: faint structure-ink concentric hairlines generated around ORGANS THAT
ARE LIVE (radius/level from node state), fading by distance and level, on the existing
plane. Why: deletes offender #1 and replaces a default with the A11 §3.1 motif — the
ground itself cites the map. Sketch: onBeforeCompile on the ground material; uniform
array of vec4 (xz, radius-scale, level) for up to ~20 organs; `smoothstep` hairlines on
ring distance; base albedo keeps 0x071018. Rings are STATIC at idle: no rotation, drift,
or breathing; ring MOTION remains event-only (the A11 §3.1 pulse, A BIRTH's ring), per
the closed A4 §9 idle-motion inventory. Binding, named: xz = NODES[organ].pos; intensity
= the live NODES[].lvl already driven by /state via refreshPlantLights
(world.html:679–682); radius-scale = fixed per organ class — no free parameter. With lvl
as the driver, ring brightness changes only when the underlying live value changes, so
motion stays event-backed by construction. DURING-P0, same ticket, same shader: fold a
low-frequency world-space value-noise term into this onBeforeCompile injection (±2–3%
luminance on albedo/roughness, xz-stable so it cannot swim, ~10 lines, no new pass —
legal under A11 §4.5, which governs screen-space treatments, not surface materials) so
offender #4's flat plane actually dies with this move. Cost M. Rides P0 (with the
EMISSIVE-TUNE ticket that already rides first there). `[PLANNED]` — edited 2026-07-20
(nine-critic closure)

**MOVE 2 — Authored dark on every structure: vertical gradient via vertex colors.**
Bases darker (into the ground shadow), a barely-lighter band at silhouette top; applied
to plants, kiosks, archive boxes. Why: Sable's layered-depth lesson [S-1] — surfaces
must show a decision; kills the flat-structure half of offender #2/#3's default response
(citation corrected 2026-07-20: this move never touches offender #4's ground plane —
that fix is Move 1's noise term). Sketch: per-vertex color attribute at build time,
`vertexColors: true`; ~30 lines. Dependency: tuned ONCE, after Move 5's color
management lands — pre-gamma the gradient is sub-perceptual on these albedos and
pushing amplitude without the dither invites offender #5's banding. Cost S. Rides P0.
`[PLANNED]` — edited 2026-07-20 (nine-critic closure)

**MOVE 3 — Silhouette pass on the foundry row: plants GROWN from their data.** Replace
the one-box-one-cylinder plant with 2–3 stacked masses whose heights/setbacks derive
from REAL, NAMED plant properties — corrected 2026-07-20: `tier` is NOT a per-plant
field anywhere in /state or the PLANTS registry (it exists only as a rod/draw-ladder
concept), so the derivation is: stack count = the plant's rod count / top rod tier from
/state.energy.rods[] (or model_fitness rows keyed by plant); heights from rpm_cap, with
the throttled/uncapped cases named; class from the existing privacy field. No
hand-assigned tiers — that would be bespoke visuals by the back door, the exact A11 §8
language-fork this move exists to avoid. "nvidia massive / pollinations open / ollama
local-small" are PREDICTIONS of the mapping, not targets — if live data disagrees, data
wins (truth law, 04 §0). This move also formally retires the world.html:452 hand-scale
(`p[0]==='nvidia'?1:...`), the bespoke seed already on disk. Why: silhouette-first
language [S-5][S-9]; Cocoon's grown-not-placed [S-7]; and it is Phase-B-legal because
the silhouettes are GENERATED from live plant data, never hand-authored (A11 §8's
language lock). Sketch: extend makePlant(scale) to makePlant(plantRecord); masses
remain BoxGeometry; break the even spacing with deterministic jitter seeded by plant
name hash. Cost M. Rides P2 (the pool x the acts — the rung already touching plants).
`[PLANNED]` — edited 2026-07-20 (nine-critic closure)

**MOVE 4 — Window truth: the texture becomes a meter.** Regenerate each plant's window
CanvasTexture from live load — lit-window count = f(rpm_now/rpm_cap), NearestFilter for
crisp pixels, regenerate on refreshPlantLights() (throttled, it already polls at 6s).
Why: kills the tiling-blur tell (offender #2) AND upgrades honesty — the window pattern
stops pretending and becomes a real instrument; evidence density at building scale
(A11 §3.4). Sketch: rewrite windowTexture(p) → windowTexture(loadFraction); mark
`texture.needsUpdate`. Binding, named so the meter and the window brightness can never
wire to different sources: loadFraction = rpm_now/rpm_cap from /state's plants[] rows
(08_TECH.md §/state) — the same source already driving emissive at world.html:679–682.
Kill the `repeat(2, 1.2)` (world.html:435) when the texture becomes a meter: a
horizontally duplicated meter reads as double the lit count — repeat(1,1) +
NearestFilter, one window-column pattern per plant. Cost S. Rides P2. `[PLANNED]`
— edited 2026-07-20 (nine-critic closure)

**MOVE 5 — Color management tail: sRGB out + sub-perceptual dither.** Re-costed
2026-07-20: this is a MIGRATION, not a one-pass patch — every authored hex on disk was
tuned display-space under linear output, and a LinearTosRGB tail lifts the whole frame
(~0.05 linear reads ~0.24 display: the tuned void greys out). The spec, in order:
(1) vendor GammaCorrectionShader as the composer's LAST pass (the 04 §8.1 path — that
ledger entry narrows from "the three passes" to Gamma-only; update 04 §8 when this
lands so two truths don't coexist), with the FXAAShader vendored into the same tail
(composer tail = FXAA -> gamma+dither): with the composer running, the renderer's
`antialias:true` is silently dead in r128 (non-multisampled render targets), and no
move may ship jagged edges — verify on a ?still crop of the foundry-row silhouettes.
Fold the one-line ordered dither (8x8 Bayer or ALU hash, amplitude under 1/255) into
the final pass. (2) `convertSRGBToLinear()` sweep over every authored THREE.Color plus
`texture.encoding = sRGBEncoding` on every CanvasTexture (windows, DOT) — this restores
the tuned look BY CONSTRUCTION instead of a hand re-grade; manually pre-linearize the
custom sky/fresnel ShaderMaterial uniforms, which skip the built-in conversion.
(3) gate 3 (WebGL #d4a24c == CSS #d4a24c) is IMPOSSIBLE as exact equality — emissive
amber passes through ACES + exposure 1.1 before the gamma pass — so redefine it with a
`toneMapped = false` reference swatch (Material.toneMapped exists in this r128 build)
or an explicit tolerance. (4) The perceived-bloom shift is real even though the locked
numbers do not change: record it in the 04 §8 ledger as [DECISION-LUIS] with an
on-hardware A/B screenshot pair per A4 §8.2, full ?still re-baseline + §8.7
re-records, and the bloom lock re-blessed by Luis as "these numbers under the new
gamma" — otherwise P0 ends in an illegal ad-hoc bloom retune or a silent sRGB revert.
Acceptance adds a 400% greyscale crop of a dark gradient to the ?still gates, so the
under-1/255 claim (the entire legal basis for not counting the dither as a second
texture pass, A11 §4.5) is screenshot-verified, not asserted. Why: offender #5; dark
scenes band without it [S-17][S-18]. Cost M (re-costed from S). Rides P0, FIRST in the
visual packet. `[PLANNED]` — edited 2026-07-20 (nine-critic closure)

**MOVE 6 — The photographic horizon.** Deepen the sky's horizon treatment: a second,
tighter luminance band just above the horizon line (structure-ink cold, amber band
unchanged and still capped 0.10–0.18) so silhouettes read against a graded light line
instead of even darkness. Why: Exo One's atmosphere-as-content [S-12]; §2-current
tell 1; makes the one-image (A11 §2) read at thumbnail harder. Sketch: one more
`pow(1-abs(h), k)` term in the existing sky fragment shader. Cost S. Rides P0. `[PLANNED]`

**MOVE 7 — Roads become wires: falloff in STRUCTURE ink at idle, amber only under a
real trace.** Rewritten 2026-07-20: the first draft ("idle roads read as conductors
holding charge") authored persistent idle amber on wires — and A11 §4.1's closed list
of legal idle amber (probe · beacon · windows-tracking-load · discovered nodes ·
presence chip · HUD values · sky band) does not contain roads; unmeasured pooling is
signal-that-is-not-truth (A11 §6) and collides with §3.2's "a trace's afterglow is a
fading proof, not a persistent glow / no fake traffic, ever". The move as ruled: idle
wire falloff is STRUCTURE ink only — a gradient within the rgba(120,155,175) family
(UV-based falloff baked into a CanvasTexture ramp per strip), so the strips stop being
uniform neon while staying honest. Warm/hot appears on a wire ONLY during a real trace
and its 600–900ms decay (A11 §3.2, already law). If amber-at-idle wires are ever
wanted, the honest path is a [DECISION-LUIS] amendment adding lit roads to A11 §4.1's
closed list, with the pooling intensity bound to a named real value (e.g. per-link
cumulative routed calls from the tracelog JSONL) and the endpoint named in this move.
Implementation note so it is not discovered mid-build: roadMat is SHARED and trunkMat
is a clone (world.html:485–486), both driven by direct emissiveIntensity writes
(foundry_full sets .45/.55 at world.html:675) — per-strip ramp CanvasTextures require
per-strip material clones while keeping the intensity path intact. Same session, same
technique: the beacon beam is this offender class too (uniform-opacity additive
cylinder, world.html:531–532, sitting inside A11 §2's canonical portrait) — give it a
vertical alpha ramp via a 1D CanvasTexture alphaMap, bright at the ground ring,
dissolved by ~60% height; DURING-P0 (ride the EMISSIVE-TUNE ticket) or here beside the
road ramp. Why: offender #6; light-as-meaning [S-8]; prepares the autograph motif.
Cost S. Rides P1 (records + trace ledger — the trace's data rung). `[PLANNED]`
— edited 2026-07-20 (nine-critic closure)

**MOVE 8 — The trace itself ships as gradient light.** When click-to-trace lands, the
packet is a short additive quad-strip with head-hot/tail-cooling vertex colors walking
the wire — never a uniform-color line. Traversed wire cools hot → warm → structure over
the 600–900ms decay (A4 §6, already law). Why: the autograph motif is the game's only
traveling light (A11 §3.2); uniform lines would land it in offender #6. Sketch:
BufferGeometry strip, per-vertex color, position updated along the polyline at real
call pace. Cost M (the visual half of an already-planned feature). Rides P1. `[PLANNED]`

**MOVE 9 — Starfield with depth.** Two Points layers: 900 faint small + 200 brighter
with slight per-point size variance (attribute + onBeforeCompile), luminance variance
only within structure ink — no new hue. BOTH layers sit on the structure-ink hue: the
as-built stars are 0x8a97a8 (world.html:391), already off the 120,155,175 ink, and the
rebuild is the moment to fix that drift; the brighter layer stays below the bloom
threshold (04 §6 bloom palette law — structure never crosses). Fold `map: DOT` (or a
round-point discard) into the same pass: the as-built PointsMaterial has no sprite
