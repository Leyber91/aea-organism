## THE ADDENDUM (paste after the main block, before the category)

ADDITIONAL CANON — the information the previous sheets did not carry:

STATE IS THE POINT. This game is about live change, and the catalogue sheets froze everything.
Amber has exactly two tiers: IDLE 0.35 (a thing exists and is warm but not working) and FIRED
1.0 (it is working right now); a fired thing flares in 120 milliseconds and decays back over
600-900. Map glyphs have exactly three states: HIDDEN (absent, not drawn at all), SENSED
(outline only, dashed, labelled UNCHARTED), KNOWN (solid, amber, named). Model-vessels decay
through: FRESH, STABLE, DRIFTING, COOLING (temporarily refusing), ROTTING (dying), DEAD.
A construct run passes through: COMPOSE (cold, being wired), RUN (packet travelling, live trace),
HALT (a link severed, the failure marked ON the wire where it broke), RECORD (the measured
result, the only reward). An organ site before it is built shows SCAFFOLDING, not emptiness.

SCALE, in one system (previous sheets each used their own): the probe is 1.2 metres across.
A kiosk or socket is 4.5 metres. A bench plate is 2 metres wide. A power plant is 20 metres.
The nexus slab is 8 metres. A district landmark is 40-60 metres. The mind spire is 200 metres.
The field horizon is 2 kilometres. A human hand appears exactly once in this world: at THE SEND.

THE PALETTE, exactly: void #050a10 to #0a1420. Structure is blue-gray rgba(120,155,175) at five
fixed opacities only: 0.70 values, 0.45 labels, 0.28 secondary, 0.14 hairlines, 0.08 faint rules.
Amber is #ffb000 hot and #d4a24c warm. Nothing else exists.

THE CAMERA: the player flies in third person, camera 20 metres behind and 6 above the probe,
60 degree field of view, everything beyond 150 metres dissolving into fog of the same colour as
the sky. Three depth bands must always separate: near objects at 85 percent contrast, mid at 40,
far as silhouette only.

CRITICAL — THESE DRAWINGS BECOME 3D MODELS. From here on, any sheet showing an object must be
usable by a modeller: orthographic projections (front, side, top) aligned on a shared baseline
with dimension lines, not just a three-quarter beauty view. Where a form is round, show the
profile. Where it opens, show it open and closed. No lighting tricks that hide geometry.
