## 0. Why this chapter exists

The book is complete top-down — twenty-one chapters, every dimension defined (BOOK.md
audit 2026-07-20). Complete is not buildable. A finished design read as a to-do list
invites the recorded failure of this workspace: brilliant strategy, zero artifacts
(09_PRODUCTION.md risk R1). The countermeasure is the same one every chapter already
obeys — decompose until each piece is individually shippable and individually falsifiable,
then ship them in an order where every rung retires a named risk and no rung needs a rung
above it.

This chapter is that decomposition. It restates the final goal once, at the top, then
cuts DOWN into fourteen prototypes, P0 to P13. Each rung is small enough for its stated
session count, proves something the book currently only claims, and ends at a Luis
go/no-go. The ladder is not a new plan — it is the existing book (the forge queue, the
production queue, the mission tickets, the evidence chapter) arranged into the one
sequence the evidence supports.

and every number on every screen still live system truth, two inks, claim ceiling held.**

That is the top. Everything below is the goal cut into falsifiable pieces.

## 2. THE DECOMPOSITION PRINCIPLES — what binds the order

Six constraints shape the ladder. None is taste; each has a citation.

1. **The bench comes first — the evidence says so twice.** R1_EVIDENCE.md finding 10
   (SpaceChem, 2% completion): the first composition must be trivial and immediate —
   2 parts, one click, one visible run. A13's census: USE is 0 of 29, the widest gap in
   the book, and "the game lives or dies exactly there" (R1 findings 1–3). P0 is
   therefore THE BENCH minimal, before any new act content.
2. **Forges are pair sessions, one organ each, in queue order.** A9_FORGE_PROTOCOL.md
   section 6 fixes seven forges (recall, think, wire, C3, voyager, STOP, DGM) and STOPS
   there. The ladder never books two organs into one rung and never reorders the queue.
3. **The boring test gates every rung.** A rung ships only if the acted-on systems are
   real (09_PRODUCTION.md section 4). Standing clause on every exit criterion below; not
   repeated per rung.
4. **THE SEND stays pinned — and the income clock pulls its pipeline early.** The Act V
   boss ceremony does not move (09 section 4). But A9 section 7 is explicit: forges 1–2
   (recall grounds the draft, think routes it) are the draft's only real dependencies —
   the wire and C3 give it a world to REACH, not the ability to DRAFT. So the
   draft-pipeline prototype (P7) lands immediately after the think forge, roughly the
   ladder's midpoint, not at the campaign's end. Income beats polish on ties; this is
   that law made structural.
5. **Playability-slice items land where evidence justifies them, never all at once.**
   SCAN / ECHOES / home / companions come from the playability-slice work (Luis,
   2026-07-20; A13 section 5 — BOOK.md registers its chapter when it lands). ECHOES needs
   a world with real history to revisit (exists after P3: rot, ticks, forge scaffolding).
   Companions need a collection worth showing (exists after P6/P7: multiple constructs
   with records, a draft in hand). They enter at P5 and P10 respectively, with a named
   pull-forward trigger — not as one block.
6. **This ladder is itself a re-plan, and it justifies itself under the re-planning
   guard.** 09 section 4: re-planning only against new evidence. The new evidence exists
   by name: R1's verified research pass (21 confirmed claims), A13's station census
   (USE 0/29), playtest 001's two findings, and D-P1's recommendation. P0–P2 enter ahead
   of slice 2's Act II items and this sentence names the displacement: the EMISSIVE-TUNE
   ticket keeps its first slot (it rides P0), coach-marks ride P3 with the Act II content
   they serve, nothing else in the 09 section 3 queue is displaced — Act II simply starts
   three small rungs later, on a bench the evidence says must exist first.

**Positioning, mapped to structure:** "open-ended inside rungs, gated between rungs" is
the ladder's grain — inside every rung the bench tasks are open-target (R1 finding 9:
problems the designers have not solved; beat your own record on any axis), and between
rungs the act bosses gate (03_PROGRESSION.md). "Each player's collection of assistants is
their character to show" is why constructs persist as files from P0, accrue records from
P1, and become companions at P10. MAGIC OUT OF THE REAL is the honesty law wearing its
positioning: every rung's exit is a real behavior measured, never a trick staged.

## 6. THE STOP RULE

Binding, after EVERY rung, no exceptions:

1. **The ledger line lands first.** 09_PRODUCTION.md gains its section 1 or section 2
   entry in the same commit as the rung's last change. A rung without its ledger entry
   did not ship (09's change discipline).
2. **Luis go/no-go.** Player one plays what shipped; the verdict is recorded, and a
   no-go stops the ladder — the next rung gets zero hours until the finding is triaged
   (the playtest-001 pattern: findings become build items, not plan rewrites).
3. **Re-read this ladder against the evidence.** Ten minutes, not a session: does the
   next rung still retire the biggest live risk? A rung may be re-ordered, split, or cut
   ONLY by citing new evidence by name — a shipped artifact, a playtest finding, a Luis
   decision, a rot event (R3-class). The named pull-forward triggers in this document
   (P10's companions trigger) are pre-authorized examples of exactly this.
4. **Re-planning without new evidence is the failure mode, named.** Re-reading this
   ladder and producing P0_PROTOTYPES_2.md is this workspace's recorded trap
   (09 section 4). If the ladder holds, the only legitimate act is executing the next
   rung. If it does not hold, the amendment happens HERE, dated, with the evidence
   cited — never in a parallel document.
5. **The queue stays the queue.** The next rung enters 09 section 3 by name; nothing
   enters ahead of it without displacing something by name. This ladder and 09's queue
   are one object at two grains; if they ever disagree, that disagreement is a bug in
   this file, and 09 — the log of record — wins until this file is amended.
